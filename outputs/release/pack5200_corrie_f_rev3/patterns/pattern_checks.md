## 5. B-29 crown draft

The drawn dome (`corrie_f.LID`, superellipse n 4, 30 mm wall + 55 mm dome) meets the wall vertically, so it cannot be flattened as one piece (least squares: p95 strain about 40 %). The crown is drafted by radial development (every meridian keeps its 3D length) with four corner darts that take the extra perimeter: flat 1137.5 mm against the 989.2 mm wall. Each dart opens 36.8 mm, legs 98.2 mm (ASSUMPTION LID-DART). Strap channel line 265.5 mm on the flat against 261.3 mm over the 3D dome.
