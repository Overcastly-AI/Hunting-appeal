# G4: Lever rig: frame coupons (weeks 2-5)

**When:** build weekend 2, then runs day and night  
**Rig:** kit B lever fatigue rig (CA1_fatigue_rig.md), 40 C box, 3-point bend fixture  
**Kits:** B, D

Build the rig once; every frame coupon runs on it. CA-1 at the release level comes first: frame release R1 cannot happen without it. The 40 C box also runs CS-1 (BL-037) beside CA-4.

About **35.5 h** hands-on and **570 h** of machine time.

## Safety (read before you start)

- The crank and lever move for days: guard the crank with a box, keep hands out while it runs, unplug before touching a coupon.
- 40 C box: thermostat set and checked with a thermometer before you leave it.
- CA-5 static pulls reach 4.4 kN at the stub: blanket over the rig, stand to the side.

## 1. CA-1: PAHT-CF fatigue coupons, 0.40 (fallback >= 0.313)

**Why:** H1 print release R1: R1 = CA-1 passes 0.40 with 3/3 run-outs AND MT-1c shows a cinch swing <= 117 % (peak-to-peak / mean) at a mean <= 150 N per anchor (structural countersign, adopted 2026-11-13, analysis/ca1_mt1c_confirm.md); 0.40 also feeds option F-1 (BL-037)  
**Specimen:** 12 XY + 4 Z + 3 spare bars, printed and annealed like the frame, 7 days at 23 C / ~50 % RH

| load | N | lb | kg on the scale |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| XY bar peak at the R1 level 0.40 | 1126 | 253 | 114.8 |
| XY bar valley (R 0.1) | 113 | 25 | 11.5 |
| load cell reads (4:1 lever) | 282 | 63 | 28.7 |
| XY bar peak at the lowest fallback level 0.313 (only if 0.40 fails) | 881 | 198 | 89.8 |

**R1 rule (adopted 2026-11-13, structural countersign):** R1 = CA-1 passes 0.40 with 3/3 run-outs AND MT-1c shows a cinch swing <= 117 % (peak-to-peak / mean) at a mean <= 150 N per anchor (structural countersign, adopted 2026-11-13, analysis/ca1_mt1c_confirm.md). Run 3 XY bars at **0.40**. Then, if 0.40 fails: 3 XY bars at the table level for the measured swing, >= 0.313 (<= 0 %: 0.313 / <= 5 %: 0.316 / <= 10 %: 0.319 / <= 20 %: 0.325 / <= 30 %: 0.331 / <= 50 %: 0.345 / <= 70 %: 0.361 / <= 100 %: 0.386 / <= 115 %: 0.398). Swing > 115 %: STOP, no fallback level: the structural-fea-engineer re-runs the cinch case. The BL-038 / BL-039 levels 0.276 and 0.404 are retired: the 0.276 rule left out the cinch mean stress and the R 0 (frame) vs R 0.1 (coupon) gap (body Goodman flip 0.2926, margin 0.943 at 0.276).

**Steps**

1. Calibrate: hang the 20.0 kg jug in place of the coupon; the cell must read 49 N. Write the reading.
2. Static (5 bars): put the turnbuckle in place of the spring; clamp an XY bar 35 mm deep in each grip; turn the turnbuckle about 1 turn every 10 s until the bar breaks; peak cell force x 4 / smallest gauge area = strength in MPa. 3 XY bars, then 2 Z bars (CA1_fatigue_rig.md 6 step 1).
3. UTS_meas = mean of the 3 XY strengths (cap 88 MPa). Work out your own peak: F_max = 0.4 x 0.8 x UTS_meas x area (at 88 MPa and 40 mm2: 1126 N; the cell then reads 282 N). F_min = 0.1 x F_max.
4. Set the crank throw and the turnbuckle so the cell swings between F_min / 4 and F_max / 4; check over 20 slow turns by hand before the motor runs.
5. R1 level 0.40, 3 XY bars one at a time: run to 100,000 cycles (27.8 h at 1 Hz). The rig stops itself if the bar breaks, its stiffness drops more than 10 % or the force drifts more than 5 %.
6. After each run-out look at the gauge and fillets with the loupe; then pull that bar to failure (step 2): it must keep at least 0.9 x UTS_meas.
7. Then 2 Z bars at 0.40 (0.40 x 0.8 x the Z strength).
8. Only if a 0.40 bar failed: 3 XY bars at the countersign table level for MT-1c's measured swing, at least 0.313 (881 N peak at 88 MPa at 0.313); see the R1 rule above.
9. 2 of 3 at a level is inconclusive: run 3 more; a level passes only with 5 of 6.
10. Send the rig's CSV log with the results sheet.

**Pass:** 3/3 run-outs to 1e5 at 0.40, stiffness loss <= 10 %, no crack; static XY >= 79.2 MPa, Z >= 42.3 MPa. R1 also needs MT-1c swing <= 117 % at mean <= 150 N. The 0.276 / 0.404 levels are retired (structural countersign 2026-11-13)

**Write in the results sheet** (`results_template.csv`, column `measured`):

- `CA-1-01 to CA-1-03` (XY#1 to XY#3): static XY UTS. MPa, pass if at least 79.2.
- `CA-1-04 to CA-1-05` (Z#1 to Z#2): static Z UTS. MPa, pass if at least 42.3.
- `CA-1-06 to CA-1-08` (RXY#1 to RXY#3): cycles at the R1 level 0.40 (run-out 100000). Cycles, pass if at least 100000.
- `CA-1-09 to CA-1-11` (SXY#1 to SXY#3): stiffness loss at run-out. %, pass if at most 10.
- `CA-1-12 to CA-1-13` (RZ#1 to RZ#2): Z cycles at 0.40. Cycles, pass if at least 100000.
- `CA-1@fallback-01 to CA-1@fallback-03` (FXY#1 to FXY#3): only if 0.40 failed: cycles at the table level for the MT-1c swing (run-out 100000). Cycles, pass if at least 100000.
- `CA-1@fallback-04` (level): fallback level used (table, >= 0.313). Type the value in -.

Note: BL-040: R1 rule from the adopted structural countersign (ca1_mt1c_confirm.countersign). BL-038: release level from h1_buckling.json (BL-027 body flip) and im3_body_simloads.json (28 mm cheek), replacing the 0.310 placeholder (CA1_fatigue_rig.md 1 asked for this). Grams: BL-037 F-1 (-9.9 g, an option that also needs a buckling re-run), not -50..-70 g. Machine: 8 runs x 27.8 h. CT-B1 merged here

## 5. CS-1: XLPE L200 compression set, 50 % for 22 h at 40 C

**Why:** BL-037 B-1 (-31.9 g) and H-1 (-22.3 g)  
**Specimen:** 3 discs 30 mm diameter of XLPE L200 6 mm (belt) and 3 of 5 mm (straps)

**Steps**

1. Measure each disc's thickness t0 with the caliper (light touch, 3 points, mean)
2. Put the discs between the two jig plates with 3.0 mm spacers (6 mm discs) or 2.5 mm spacers (5 mm discs)
3. Tighten until the plates sit on the spacers
4. 22 h in the 40 C box
5. Take out, unclamp, let them recover 24 h on a flat table
6. Measure tf the same way
7. Set % = (t0 - tf) / t0 x 100

**Pass:** every disc set <= 15 % (the EVA 45C record value)

**Write in the results sheet** (`results_template.csv`, column `measured`):

- `CS-1-01 to CS-1-03` (6mm#1 to 6mm#3): compression set, 6 mm disc. %, pass if at most 15.
- `CS-1-04 to CS-1-06` (5mm#1 to 5mm#3): compression set, 5 mm disc. %, pass if at most 15.
