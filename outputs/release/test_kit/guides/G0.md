### Print

3. Load the job's STLs from `designs/pack_5200/print/frame_h1/coupons_paht/` and its 15 x 15 mm prime pillar (`tower_A.stl` or `tower_B.stl`), pillar 10 mm (0.4 in) from the parts, parts spaced as in the files.
4. Layer time by a dwell, not a tower (BL-079): save a copy of the printer preset as 'PAHT coupons dwell' and, in Printer settings > Machine G-code > 'Layer change G-code', add at the end: a move that parks the nozzle clear of the parts (copy the park / wipe move your printer's own timelapse code uses) and `G4 S<dwell>`, with the dwell of the job from the table below (job A 14 s, job B 163 s). Do NOT use the filament's 'Slow printing down for better layer cooling' for this: it keeps the hot nozzle over the coupon and stops at the minimum speed. Everything else stays the frame profile.
5. Slice. Open the preview's layer-time view (it may not count the G4 dwell): every layer must show at least 175 s minus the dwell. On the printer, time 3 layers of each job with a watch: each >= 175 s. Write the shortest and the median layer time of each job in the results sheet. If the dwell leaves blobs or gaps at the restart, fall back to a dummy tower sized to the same layer time (one more filament, about USD 40, still inside the cap).
6. Print. Watch the first layer: every comb tie, bar and plate flat on the plate.
7. When cool: weigh each job's parts (pillar off), write it. Anneal everything as the frame (BUILD_H1 section 5: 90 C (194 F) 6 h, cool in the oven); weigh the traveller straight after (its dry mass).

### Conditioning to constant mass

1. Salt jar: 2 L sealed glass jar, a 1 cm (0.4 in) layer of non-iodised salt (NaCl) wetted to a wet slush (some undissolved salt must always show), a wire rack above it. Over saturated NaCl the air reads 74.4 % RH at 50 C (122 F) (Greenspan 1977, as bl077.json).
2. Heat: the jar goes in the kit's 40 C box with its thermostat set to 50 C (122 F) (ASSUMPTION BOX-COND). No box yet: the BUILD_H1 filament dryer set to 50 C, if the closed jar fits inside (ASSUMPTION DRYER-JAR). A thermometer beside the jar; write the reading each day.
3. Traveller: the 60 x 60 x 4 mm plate from job B (as thick as the thickest coupon, the bend bars). Weigh it on the 0.001 g scale before it goes in (dry, after the anneal) and every day at the same hour: take it out, wipe it, weigh within 1 min, put it back.
4. Done when the traveller changes by less than 0.1 % in 24 h (0.015 g on this plate). Expect 2 to 4 weeks (ASSUMPTION COND-TIME): the scale decides, not the calendar. Write the uptake % = (wet - dry) / dry x 100 (the TDS says 0.88 % at 25 C / 55 % RH; more is expected here).
5. Keep the conditioned parts in the jar until the hour they are tested; the jar rides in the 40 C box during the tests.

## 1. CA-3-PAHT: PAHT-CF Z tensile + SBS, hot/wet B-basis at n 10, dry set, layer time >= 175 s

**Why:** the frame decision (COUPONS_PAHT.md section 1: H1 as is, H1c or a redesign); the SAFETY HOLD on the released H1  
**Specimen:** hot/wet 10 Z + 10 SBS; dry 6 Z + 6 SBS; Z bars ISO 527-2 1BA from the combs (job B), SBS bars from the block (job A)

**Steps**

1. Print job A and job B (COUPONS_PAHT.md section 3; Load the job's STLs from `designs/pack_5200/print/frame_h1/coupons_paht/` and its 15 x 15 mm prime pillar (`tower_A.stl` or `tower_B.stl`), pillar 10 mm (0.4 in) from the parts, parts spaced as in the files.; Layer time by a dwell, not a tower (BL-079): save a copy of the printer preset as 'PAHT coupons dwell' and, in Printer settings > Machine G-code > 'Layer change G-code', add at the end: a move that parks the nozzle clear of the parts (copy the park / wipe move your printer's own timelapse code uses) and `G4 S<dwell>`, with the dwell of the job from the table below (job A 14 s, job B 163 s). Do NOT use the filament's 'Slow printing down for better layer cooling' for this: it keeps the hot nozzle over the coupon and stops at the minimum speed. Everything else stays the frame profile.).
2. SBS block: sand 0.3 mm off the top and the bottom on 240-grit paper glued to glass (3.6 -> 3 mm, 0.118 in), so the mid-plane stays on the line between layers 9 and 10. Trim the 5 mm edge strip, then saw the bars 18 x 6 mm on the pencil grid with a fine-tooth saw.
3. Z combs: snip each bar off its two ties with flush cutters and sand the tie stubs flat (they sit in the grips, not the gauge). The 5 mm wide gauge crosses the layers. Do not sand the gauge faces.
4. Label with a paint pen: CA3-Z-H1..H10 and CA3-S-H1..H10 (hot/wet), CA3-Z-D1..D6 and CA3-S-D1..D6 (dry). Measure each gauge width and thickness with the caliper at 3 points; write the smallest on the bar.
5. DRY set: in a zip bag with desiccant; test within 24 h at 23 C (73 F).
6. HOT/WET set: condition it (COUPONS_PAHT.md section 4) with the CA-1-PAHT and CA-4-PAHT bars.
7. Test at 40 +/- 2 C (104 F) inside the 40 C box round the lever rig's coupon station (link through a slot in the lid, ASSUMPTION BOX-ON-RIG of BL-074), 1 h soak first, thermometer touching the part.
8. Z tensile: lever rig static (turnbuckle in place of the spring), the bar's grips 10 mm deep. Strength = peak cell force x 4 / (width x thickness). Dry bars about 960 N (97.9 kg on the scale, 216 lbf) at the bar.
9. Short-beam shear: jig with the D6 loading nose on top and two D3 supports 12 mm (0.47 in) apart (ASTM D2344), bar flat as printed. Load slowly to the drop. Shear strength = 0.75 x F_max / (width x thickness).
10. Sheet: B-basis of the 10 = AVERAGE - 2.355 x STDEV (the sheet does it). Type every bar; send the sheet the same day.

**Pass:** (a) hot/wet Z B-basis >= 19.1 MPa (= 19.66 / 1.034 rounded up, the H1c body rows R1 and static fatigue); >= 26.8 MPa also lifts the open z 234 joint fatigue (0.735) to 1.0; (b) hot/wet SBS B-basis >= 11.0 MPa (= tau_hw 11.35 / 1.034 rounded up; >= 15.5 for the joint); (c) for the record: hot/wet / dry Z (booked 0.656) and the uptake; H1 AS IS (BL-079, h1_as_is): sleeved: no Z / SBS value clears it (static_fatigue, R1, joint_fatigue, lug_fatigue saturate below goal); inserts: no Z / SBS value clears it (static_fatigue, R1, joint_fatigue, lug_fatigue saturate below goal). ## 2. CA-1-PAHT: PAHT-CF short-beam-shear fatigue at 40 C, conditioned, 1.15 x Smax

**Why:** the frame decision (COUPONS_PAHT.md section 1: H1 as is, H1c or a redesign); the SAFETY HOLD on the released H1  
**Specimen:** 6 SBS fatigue + 3 SBS static from the job A block

**Steps**

1. Bars from the SBS block: CA1-F1..F6 (+1 spare) and CA1-S1..S3, faced, cut, labelled and conditioned with the CA-3-PAHT hot/wet set.
2. Fit the short-beam jig at the lever rig's coupon station and put the 40 C box round it. Test at 40 +/- 2 C (104 F) inside the 40 C box round the lever rig's coupon station (link through a slot in the lid, ASSUMPTION BOX-ON-RIG of BL-074), 1 h soak first, thermometer touching the part.
3. Static first: the 3 S bars to failure as CA-3-PAHT short-beam shear.
4. Fatigue peak for each bar: F_max = 5.43 MPa x width x thickness / 0.75 (about 131 N (13.4 kg on the scale, 29 lbf) at the bar, 3.3 kg on the cell); F_min = 0.1 x F_max (R 0.1).
5. Set the crank throw and the turnbuckle so the cell swings between F_min / 4 and F_max / 4; check over 20 slow turns by hand before the motor runs.
6. Run each bar to 100,000 cycles at 1 Hz (about 28 h; never above 5 Hz). The rig stops itself on a break, a stiffness drop over 10 % or a force drift over 5 %.
7. After each run-out: loupe on the bar's middle layer line, photograph, type the cycles. A bar that breaks: stop and send the sheet; never swap in the spare to make the count.

**Pass:** all 6 SBS bars run out to 100,000 cycles at Smax 5.43 MPa (peak 131 N on the jig), R 0.1, 40 +/- 2 C; the 3 static SBS >= the CA-3-PAHT SBS line 11 MPa

**Write in the results sheet** (`results_template.csv`, column `measured`):

- `CA-1-PAHT-01 to CA-1-PAHT-03` (static 1 to static 3): SBS strength, hot/wet 40 C (below 11: REDESIGN). MPa, pass if at least 11.
- `CA-1-PAHT-04 to CA-1-PAHT-09` (fatigue 1 to fatigue 6): cycles reached at Smax 5.43 MPa (a break before 100,000: REDESIGN). Cycles, pass if at least 100000.
- `CA-1-PAHT-10` (40 C box): thermometer during the runs. C, pass if 38 to 42.

## 3. CA-4-PAHT: PAHT-CF 3-point bend modulus at 23 C dry / 40 C conditioned / 60 C dry

**Why:** the frame decision (COUPONS_PAHT.md section 1: H1 as is, H1c or a redesign); the SAFETY HOLD on the released H1  
**Specimen:** 5 XY bend bars 80 x 10 x 4 per condition (job B)

**Steps**

1. Bend bars from job B: 5 for 23 C dry (CA4-D), 5 for 40 C conditioned (CA4-W), 5 for 60 C dry (CA4-H), +1 spare. Measure width and thickness (caliper, 3 points).
2. The 23 C and the hot dry sets stay sealed with desiccant. The 40 C set is conditioned with the CA-3-PAHT hot/wet set.
3. 3-point fixture: supports 64 mm (2.52 in) apart (ISO 178, 16 x thickness), bar flat as printed, dial indicator (0.01 mm) on top at mid-span.
4. Stiffness: hang 0.5 kg, zero the dial, add 2 kg (2.5 kg, 5.5 lb total), read the dial in mm. E = span^3 x 19.6 N / (4 x width x thickness^3 x reading); the sheet does the sum (ASSUMPTION E-LOAD: light weights so a hot bar is not bent past its linear range).
5. Strength: load to the break with the come-along and the crane scale. Strength = 3 x F x span / (2 x width x thickness^2).
6. 40 C set: Test at 40 +/- 2 C (104 F) inside the 40 C box round the lever rig's coupon station (link through a slot in the lid, ASSUMPTION BOX-ON-RIG of BL-074), 1 h soak first, thermometer touching the part. 60 C set: box thermostat at 60 C (140 F), thermometer 58-62 C for 30 min first (ASSUMPTION BOX-60, BL-074).
7. Sheet: E ratio = mean E (40 C conditioned) / mean E (23 C dry), and the flexural strength ratio (record).

**Pass:** E(40 C conditioned) / E(23 C dry), mean of 5 >= 0.37 (the H1c buckling need, eigen and record rule, rounded up); for the record the flexural strength ratio vs the booked 0.672; H1 AS IS (BL-079) would need >= 0.59 (its buckling rows need 0.449 eigen, 0.587 record rule)

**Write in the results sheet** (`results_template.csv`, column `measured`):

- `CA-4-PAHT-01` (23 C dry): E mean. Type the value in MPa.
- `CA-4-PAHT-02` (40 C conditioned): E(40 C conditioned) / E(23 C dry) (below 0.37: H1c buckling fails -> REDESIGN). Ratio, pass if at least 0.37.
- `CA-4-PAHT-03` (40 C conditioned): flexural strength ratio to 23 C dry (booked 0.672). Type the value in ratio.
- `CA-4-PAHT-04` (60 C dry): E mean (record). Type the value in MPa.
- `CA-4-PAHT-05` (40 C box): thermometer during the 40 C tests. C, pass if 38 to 42.
- `CA-4-PAHT-06` (60 C box): thermometer during the 60 C tests. C, pass if 58 to 62.

## 5. CLEVIS-SHARE-B-PAHT: US2 clevis outer-cheek share on the first frame (loading B), pass <= 0.6

**Why:** H1c first frame (only if the coupons point to H1c)  
**Specimen:** the first H1c frame, R stay, clevis hardware and the US2 gauge as CLEVIS-SHARE

**Steps**

1. Runs ONLY if the coupons point to H1c, on the first H1c frame (bl077.json), before any other frame test on it.
2. Fit the US2 gauge and the clevis as the kit's CLEVIS-SHARE (register row CLEVIS-SHARE); frame clamped on the bare R stay only (z 40-48 and 110-122), nothing on the US2 block.
3. Loading B: pull direction [0.0, 0.9216, -0.3881] (frame R), the real US2 pin set and a sewn US eye.
4. Reading 1 (share 1.0): 1 mm PTFE shim between the eye and the OUTER (clevis) cheek. Pull to 429 N (43.7 kg on the scale, 96 lbf), hold 10 s, read u1. Unload, read the zero.
5. Reading 0 (share 0): the same shim between the eye and the INNER cheek. Same 429 N, read u0.
6. Reading 2 (as built): shim out, eye centred as sewn. Same 429 N, read u.
7. Repeat readings 1, 0 and 2 three times (eye off and refitted each time). Share = (u - u0) / (u1 - u0); the sheet takes the largest.

**Pass:** measured share <= 0.60 (the share at which the clevis hole-bearing fatigue row reaches 1.15 on the hot/wet basis)

**Write in the results sheet** (`results_template.csv`, column `measured`):

- `CLEVIS-SHARE-B-PAHT-03` (set 1): u, as built, 429 N. Type the value in microstrain.
- `CLEVIS-SHARE-B-PAHT-04` (set 2): u1, shim on the outer cheek, 429 N. Type the value in microstrain.
- `CLEVIS-SHARE-B-PAHT-05` (set 2): u0, shim on the inner cheek, 429 N. Type the value in microstrain.
- `CLEVIS-SHARE-B-PAHT-06` (set 2): u, as built, 429 N. Type the value in microstrain.
