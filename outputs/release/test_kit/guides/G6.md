# G6: First-article frames on the stand (P1 then P2)

**When:** after G4's CA-1, IM3-EC and CA-5 pass (about week 5-6)  
**Rig:** kit C frame test stand (hip log, shoulder bar, sand bags, T5 crank) + kit A scale and come-along  
**Kits:** C, A, B (load cell), D

Frame release R1 completes here: IM3-EP on P1, then T9, T1, T2 and MT-1c on P2. Nobody carries the frame until T1 to T4 have passed. T2u destroys P1 and is always last.

About **52.2 h** hands-on and **37 h** of machine time.

## Safety (read before you start)

- Back-up sling under every load. Nobody under the load. T2u (P1, 204 kg) outdoors with a 3 m exclusion zone.
- Nobody carries the frame until T1, T3, T2 and T4 have passed. MT-1c stage 1 runs on the stand for that reason.
- T4c: -20 C parts are brittle; gloves and glasses.

## Rig sketch

```
        crown top bar --- shoulder bar (straps ratcheted to carry ~20 %)
              |
        [ P2 frame + bag, sand bags inside ]
              |
        hip log (foam 4x4) on the stand        come-along + crane scale pull down for T1 / T2 / T3
              |
        T5 crank: wiper motor -- crank -- bungee -- bag   (0.4 -> 1.6 g)

MT-1c:  IM2 strap free end -- [snap shackle] -- [100 kg S cell] -- [snap shackle] -- cam
        HX711 + Nano + SD logger taped to the frame, 80 Hz
```

## 11. MT-1c: meat-strap cinch readout: does the IM cinch cycle with the walking g (CINCH_STATIC)

**Why:** frame release R1: body fatigue 1.175 with the cinch static, 0.782 if it cycles fully; the measured swing sets the CA-1 level R1 needs (BL-039, designs/pack_5200/analysis/ca1_mt1c_confirm.md)  
**Specimen:** P2 frame + sewn bag, 30 kg sand meat on IM1-IM3 cinched by hand as in the field; kit B 100 kg S-type cell + HX711 inline in the IM2 strap's free end (two snap shackles), logging to an SD card

| load | N | lb | kg on the scale |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| meat on IM1-IM3 | 294 | 66 | 30.0 |
| pack fill | 667 | 150 | 68.0 |

**Steps**

1. Cinch IM1-IM3 by hand as in the field and lock the cams
2. Log the IM2 strap tension at 80 Hz
3. Stage 1 on the stand: run the T5 crank 600 cycles (10 min, 0.4 -> 1.6 g) with the meat in
4. Stage 2 after T1-T4 pass: a 10 min loaded walk on rough ground
5. For each stage compute the mean tension and the mean peak-to-peak swing per cycle / step
6. Send the log

**Pass:** record the larger peak-to-peak swing / mean of the two stages (%). R1 needs: swing <= 0.4 %: CA-1 at 0.276; swing <= 70 %: CA-1 also passed at 0.40; above: CA-1 at 0.404 (BL-039 table, ca1_mt1c_confirm.md section 2; replaces BL-038's 10 % pass bound, which keeps margin >= 1.0 only up to 11.6 % and without the 0.02 cushion)

**Write in the results sheet** (`results_template.csv`, column `measured`):

- `MT-1c-01` (stand): mean cinch tension, T5 crank 600 cycles. Type the value in N.
- `MT-1c-02` (stand): peak-to-peak swing / mean tension (sets the CA-1 level, ca1_mt1c_confirm.md). Type the value in %.
- `MT-1c-03` (walk): mean cinch tension, 10 min walk. Type the value in N.
- `MT-1c-04` (walk): peak-to-peak swing / mean tension (sets the CA-1 level, ca1_mt1c_confirm.md). Type the value in %.

Note: BL-038: added. The register's MT-1 is the box-X proof; the R1 hold named 'MT-1' in DECISIONS is this cinch readout (h1_buckling.md). Stage 1 replaces the walk for R1 (no person under an unreleased frame); stage 2 confirms
