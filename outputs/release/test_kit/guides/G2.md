# G2: Pull bench weekend: hooks, cams, bartacks, ladder locks

**When:** weekend 1 (week 1)  
**Rig:** kit A pull bench (come-along, 300 kg crane scale, 5 mm pin fixture, bar clamps)  
**Kits:** A, D (caliper, loupe, pocket scale), E

One bench, one weekend. MT-1 goes first because frame release R1 needs it; the rest are every sewn load point on the bag. After this weekend you may cut the bag (if G3's wash also passes).

About **22.5 h** hands-on and **5 h** of machine time.

## Safety (read before you start)

- Stand to the side of the load line, never in line with it. Moving blanket over every sample above 50 kg.
- Safety glasses and gloves on. Nobody near the line while it is loaded.
- Ratchet straps never in the load line above 151 kg (1480 N, ASSUMPTION: a third of the 1000 lb label).
- Crane scale stop: 280 kg (2750 N). PT-1 and PT-2 at 260.2 kg are the heaviest pulls: come-along only.
- LL-H hangs a dead load: nobody under it, 50 mm off the floor so a slip ends on the floor.
- If anything fails: stop, photograph, write it down, send the sheet. Do not repair and continue.
