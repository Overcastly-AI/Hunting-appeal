## 1. T9-R3b: T9 rev 3 (b): crown strap cinch force and retention (CROWN_PRE_N, MU_LID)

**Why:** CORRIE F rev 3 crown straps (bag batch release)  
**Specimen:** sewn rev 3 bag on P2, lid on, full top; 0-15 kg spring scale

| load | N | lb | kg on the scale |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| cinch | 80 | 18 | 8.2 |
| after the walk, at least | 40 | 9 | 4.1 |

**Steps**

1. Hook the spring scale on each B-28 tail
2. Pull to 8.2 kg (80 N) and lock
3. Walk 30 min loaded (only after T1-T4 pass)
4. Read the tension again (pull the tail until it just moves)
5. Set the pack down hard from 0.5 m on its base
6. Read again
7. Check the lid is still seated

**Pass:** after the walk >= 40 N, or the lid still seated on the full top (report both values)

**Write in the results sheet** (`results_template.csv`, column `measured`):

- `T9-R3b-01` (L): left strap tension after the walk. Type the value in N.
- `T9-R3b-02` (R): right strap tension after the walk. Type the value in N.
- `T9-R3b-03` (both): >= 40 N after the walk OR lid still seated. Type Y or N.

Note: BL-038: split out of T9 (item b)

Defined in: designs/pack_5200/analysis/corrie_f_body.md 14.9, 15.6

## 3. GT-1: capstan pull: guy webbing over the packed bag's corner (MU_WEB_FAB)

**Why:** guy friction share in the CG runs (bag batch release)  
**Specimen:** first sewn bag, packed full; a 25 mm guy webbing

| load | N | lb | kg on the scale |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| hanging weight | 49 | 11 | 5.0 |

**Steps**

1. Lay the guy webbing over the packed bag's top corner
2. Hang 5 kg (49 N) on one end
3. Measure the wrap angle with phyphox (tilt of the free leg minus tilt of the hanging leg, in degrees)
4. Pull the other end slowly with the spring scale until the weight starts to rise
5. Read the peak T
6. 3 pulls
7. Mu = ln(T / 49) / (angle x pi / 180)

**Pass:** mu inside 0.20-0.40 on each pull (record the value); outside: tell the pack-designer (CG runs re-checked)

**Write in the results sheet** (`results_template.csv`, column `measured`):

- `GT-1-01 to GT-1-03` (#1 to #3): mu = ln(T / 49 N) / wrap angle (rad). -, pass if 0.20 to 0.40.

Note: BL-038: added. Needs the first sewn bag

Defined in: designs/pack_5200/analysis/corrie_f_body.md 13.2, 13.8, 13.9; outputs/release/pack5200_corrie_f_rev3/manufacturing/release_tests.csv

## 7. HS-1: shoulder pressure: XLPE L200 vs EVA 45C J-strap load foam
