## 1. WP-2: TPU 840D lash-tab slot pull (C-6 / O-15)

**Why:** C-6 lash tabs Hypalon -> TPU 840D  
**Specimen:** 5 laser-cut B-11 tabs

**Steps**

1. Strap through the slot pulled in its working direction to 450 N
2. Hold 60 s

**Pass:** 5 of 5 hold 450 N, no slot tear

**Write in the results sheet** (`results_template.csv`, column `measured`):

- `WP-2-01` (1): passes the register criterion: 5 of 5 hold 450 N, no slot tear. Type Y or N.

Note: slot direction still to be confirmed on the tab drawing (materials-engineer flag)

Defined in: designs/pack_5200/analysis/corrie_body.md §12.4; designs/pack_5200/analysis/weight_pass_corrie.md C-6

## 2. AB-1: HyperD 300 vs 630D printable: Martindale 8000 rev, brush drag, print swatch

**Why:** BL-037 G-2 (-22.5 g)  
**Specimen:** 1 yd HyperD 300 printable with the owner's camo (custom print); a 630D printable offcut; 2 lab samples 300 x 300 mm

**Steps**

1. Order the HyperD print (an 8 x 8 in swatch first if offered)
2. Send one 300 x 300 mm sample of each fabric to a textile lab for Martindale abrasion (ASTM D4966 or ISO 12947-2) to 8000 rev, end point a hole or 2 broken yarns
3. At home tape a 50 x 300 mm strip of each to a board and drag a stiff nylon brush loaded with 2 kg along 200 mm, 50 passes
4. Look with the loupe
5. Judge the printed camo in daylight at 1 m and 10 m

**Pass:** HyperD: no hole at 8000 rev; brush strip no worse than the 630D strip; owner OK on the print in writing

**Write in the results sheet** (`results_template.csv`, column `measured`):

- `AB-1-01` (lab): HyperD 300: no hole at 8000 rev. Type Y or N.
- `AB-1-02` (lab): 630D: rev at end point (reference). Type the value in rev.
- `AB-1-03` (brush): HyperD strip no worse than 630D after 50 passes. Type Y or N.
- `AB-1-04` (print): owner OK on the camo print (in writing). Type Y or N.

Note: BL-038: added (BL-037). LONG LEAD: lab turnaround about 4 weeks after receipt; custom print lead time unknown

Defined in: designs/pack_5200/analysis/weight_push.md 2 (G-2), 7 HYPERD-ABRASION; data/materials/textiles/polyester_300d_printable.yaml

## 3. HB-4: hipbelt stitched-ear joint

**Why:** belt B wing joint  
**Specimen:** 1 ear stitched into a wing-root coupon (3 recommended)

**Steps**

**Pass:** no thread break; hole elongation < 0.5 mm

## 6. PS-1: pad-seat backer sag

**Why:** C-4 correction (pad seat)  
**Specimen:** pad seat on a printed pan offcut

## 7. BP-2: stay contact width on the backer strip (pressure film)

**Why:** BL-005 sandwich strip (A_back)  
**Specimen:** 100 mm printed H1 stay segment (z about 260) on a strip offcut, 10 mm foam under the strip

**Steps**

1. Carbon paper + white paper (or a Prescale LW sheet) between stay and strip
2. Dead load 25.5 kg (2.5 N/mm x 100 mm) for 2 min
3. Measure the imprint width at 5 stations

**Pass:** imprint width >= 8 mm at all 5 stations

**Write in the results sheet** (`results_template.csv`, column `measured`):

- `BP-2-01` (1): passes the register criterion: imprint width >= 8 mm at all 5 stations. Type Y or N.

Note: ASSUMPTION: a carbon-paper imprint stands in for pressure film; if the width is within 2 mm of 8 mm, buy one Prescale LW sheet and repeat. Fail: structural re-checks core crush on the measured width

Defined in: designs/pack_5200/analysis/back_system_integration.md §6

## 8. HB-2: belt face friction

**Why:** silicone grip print (A-1; OD-HB4)  
**Specimen:** belt face fabric with and without the grip print; polyester and merino base layers, dry and sweaty

**Steps**

1. Base layer taped to a board
2. 500 g weighted sled with the face fabric on it
3. Raise one end slowly until it slides
4. Read the angle in phyphox
5. Mu = tan(angle)
6. 5 runs each

**Pass:** mu >= 0.45 credits the grip print

**Write in the results sheet** (`results_template.csv`, column `measured`):

- `HB-2-01` (1): passes the register criterion: mu >= 0.45 credits the grip print. Type Y or N.

Note: ASSUMPTION: tilt-board static mu replaces the ASTM D1894-style sled pull (no low-range force gauge in the kits); grip print adds 4.9 g if adopted

Defined in: designs/pack_5200/analysis/hipbelt_fit.md §10
