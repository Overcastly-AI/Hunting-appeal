# G3: Wash and look: seams without binding

**When:** weekend 1, evenings  
**Rig:** washing machine, mesh laundry bag, 10x loupe  
**Kits:** E, D (loupe)

Both swatch sets go in the same wash. Three cold cycles, line dry, then look at every edge.

About **3.0 h** hands-on and **9 h** of machine time.

## Safety (read before you start)

- Nothing loaded. Keep the hot knife off unless a swatch fails.

## 1. FRAY-1: wash-swatch fray of the un-bound SSa2 body seam (rev 3 fabrics, as cut)

**Why:** un-bound body seams (bag release)  
**Specimen:** 6 SSa2 swatches 200 mm long, rotary-cut edges NOT seared (as the prototype cutting plan cuts them): ULTRA 200|330D, 330D|330D, 500D|330D, 630D printable|330D, HyperD 300|330D, 100D|330D; 301, 8 SPI, tex 90, Nm 110 round point

**Steps**

1. Sew each swatch as op 3.07 (SSa2)
2. Wash with the WP-1 swatches: 3 normal cold cycles in a mesh bag with a towel, line dry
3. Look at every edge with the loupe

**Pass:** no allowance frayed past the 2nd row on any swatch; a fail on one pairing: hot-knife that cut edge or bind that seam (20 mm grosgrain)

**Write in the results sheet** (`results_template.csv`, column `measured`):

- `FRAY-1-01 to FRAY-1-06` (ULTRA|330D to 100D|330D): no fray past the 2nd row after 3 washes. Type Y or N.

Note: BL-038: added; same wash load as WP-1 (WP-1 is the B.2 seared seam)

Defined in: outputs/release/pack5200_corrie_f_rev3/manufacturing/release_tests.csv (RD-17); designs/pack_5200/mfg_corrie_f_rev3.py ASSUMPTION NO-BIND; docs/DECISIONS.md 2026-11-13 orchestrator call (3)

## 2. WP-1: wash-swatch fray, seams without binding

**Why:** B-1 (no binding on body seams)  
**Specimen:** 4 seam pairings 330D|330D, 330D|ULTRA 200, 500D|330D, 100D|ULTRA; 301 + 2nd row 6 mm in, trimmed, hot-knife seared

**Steps**

1. Sew one 200 mm swatch per pairing
2. 3 machine-wash cycles (R-12), dry
3. Look at each edge

**Pass:** no fray past the second row on any swatch

**Write in the results sheet** (`results_template.csv`, column `measured`):

- `WP-1-01 to WP-1-04` (330D|330D to 100D|ULTRA): no fray past the 2nd row after 3 washes. Type Y or N.

Note: B-1 is already in B.2: a fail adds 36.4 g back. R-12 / S-03 gate is the same test (merged)

Defined in: designs/pack_5200/analysis/corrie_body.md §12.4; designs/pack_5200/analysis/weight_pass_corrie.md B-1; designs/pack_5200/analysis/build_simplicity.md S-03
