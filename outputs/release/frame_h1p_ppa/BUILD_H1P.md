## 5. Hip mount: trap-free boss, bearing plates, nut keeper (replaces BUILD_H1 section 12)

Hardware (bl070_h1p.json `h1p.hip_mount`): 8 x Ti gr5 M5 x 35 DIN 7984 + 8 x A2 M5 DIN 934 nuts (Loctite 243) on one two 6061-T6 bearing plates .063 in (1.6 mm), arcs r 13-24.2 (26 / 48.4 mm), 4 holes each (8 holes D5.5 on r 19, the mount pattern) and a printed PPA-CF nut keeper (3 mm, holds the nuts while the screws are turned from the mount side) replace 8 x M5 x 9.5 brass inserts + 8 x M5 x 12; torque 0.45 N m.

Stack: mount base 3.0 + boss 22 + plate 1.6 + nut 4.0 = 30.6 mm; the M5 x 35 screw stands 4.4 mm = 5.5 threads proud. **Rule: at least 2 full threads proud** (1.6 mm).

**Torque: 0.45 N m (4.0 in lbf)** with the Wera 7440 (0.3-1.2 N m, +/-6 %) and the 3 mm hex bit, star order, twice. Preload 346-643 N (nominal 450 N, nut factor 0.14-0.26, ASSUMPTION K-NUT).

**Cold:** at -20 C (-4 F) the boss shrinks 0.0496 mm more than the screws (706 N of preload lost, more than the 643 N put in), so **the preload is gone at -20 C**. No HA-LS-1 row relies on it: the BL-055 tipping model the hip rows use (strength 2.07, fatigue 4.31, creep 1.56) is preload-free; Loctite 243 keeps the nuts on, and T4b-PPA-COLD checks the paint marks after the cold drops.

Plate source: **SendCutSend (laser cut, 6061-T6, .063 in = 1.60 mm, +/-0.005 in)** from `designs/pack_5200/iter1/frame/h1p/hip_bearing_ring.dxf` (https://sendcutsend.com/materials/6061-aluminum); ASSUMPTION RING-PRICE: USD 8 per plate in an order of 4 (two sets: one spare), shipping in; no price is published, check the instant quote with hip_bearing_ring.dxf (BL-076: two arc plates per frame). Fallback: cut by hand from 1.6 mm (0.063 in) 6061-T6 sheet: print the DXF 1:1, glue it on, centre-punch, drill the D5.5 holes, saw and file the two arc outlines to the line (R1.5 corners), deburr.

1. CHECK the print: the D50 boss is solid (no nut traps); 8 D5.5 holes go straight through its 22 mm; the back is a flat D49 spot face. A 5.5 mm drill shank slides through each hole by hand; clear stringing by turning the drill in your fingers.
2. CHECK the 2 bearing plates (HW-HIP-RING): flat on glass (no rock), burr-free (file and 320 grit), 1.6 mm (0.063 in) thick. Lay them on the boss back, one under each group of 4 holes, curved edges following the spot face: every plate hole shows a boss hole.
3. CHECK the nuts: caliper each **HW-HIP-NUT-P** <= 4.1 mm thick (a thicker nut is ISO 4032: set it aside).
4. Keeper dry fit (BL-075): set the empty printed keeper in the D49 spot face on the plates. It has only about 0.24 mm radial play inside the printed lip: if it binds, sand its outside edge (240 grit on a block, turning it evenly) until it drops in and sits flat. If it still binds after light sanding, STOP and flag it to the engineer: do not force it.
5. Push the 8 nuts into the printed keeper's pockets, flats radial. Put the keeper on the plates (pockets over the holes), the plates on the boss back. Tape the keeper to the boss with masking tape at two edges.
6. Turn the frame wearer face up. Lay the Ti mount on the boss face: its 8 holes over the boss holes.
7. One drop of Loctite 243 on the last 5 mm of each **HW-HIP-SCR-P** (M5 x 35) thread. Keep it off the print.
8. Start every screw by hand through the mount, the boss and the plate into its nut, turning with the 3 mm hex bit; if one does not catch, back it out, press the keeper flat, try again. Never force it.
9. Set the Wera 7440 to **0.45 N m (4.0 in lbf)**. Tighten in a star order (1-5-3-7-2-6-4-8), then go round again at the same setting: stop at the first click each time.
10. CALIPER CHECK on all 8 screw tails (BL-075): the thread sticking out past each nut must read **3.1-5.2 mm** (0.122-0.205 in), at least 2 full threads (nominal 4.4 mm = 5.5 threads). Write all 8. Outside the band: wrong screw, a nut or the keeper not seated, or a thick nut: stop.
11. CHECK seating: push the mount's pin end with the spring scale to 20 N (2.0 kg, 4.5 lb) up, down, left and right: a 0.05 mm (0.002 in) feeler does not enter under the base edge.
12. Paint marks across every screw head and the mount, and across every nut, the keeper and the plates. Remove the tape. Let the Loctite cure 24 h before any load.
13. CORD RULE BL075-CORD: route the lumbar pad-seat bottom cord tie round the lower struts OUTBOARD of |x| 35 mm (1.4 in) each side of the frame centre. It must NEVER cross the back of the boss: there it would rub on the screw threads.
14. OPTIONAL: 8 TPU push-on caps on the screw tails (+1.45 g). Fit them only if the T1 load test leaves a witness mark on the bag (B-01 inside face) or the spreader over the boss. No other hip hardware is added (BL-075).
