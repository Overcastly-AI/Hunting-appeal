## 5. Hip mount: through-bolts (replaces BUILD_H1 section 12)

The Ti hip pivot mount (HW-HIP-MNT, unchanged, 3.0 mm base, D5.8 holes) sits on the D50 boss face as on H1. On H1-P its 8 screws go **straight through** the boss (D5.5 holes) into **A2 hex nuts** sitting in hex traps that open on the boss's back face; each nut seat is 13 mm behind the mount face. Hardware (bl070_h1p.json): 8 x Ti gr5 M5 x 20 DIN 7984 + 8 x A2 M5 DIN 934 nuts (Loctite 243) replace 8 x M5 x 9.5 brass inserts + 8 x M5 x 12.

Stack: mount base 3.0 + boss to the nut seat 13 + nut 4.0 = 20 mm = the screw length, so each screw tip ends flush with the nut's top.

**Torque: 0.15 N m (1.3 in lbf).** Source: calculation in `frame_h1p_build.hip()`: preload F = creep-rupture 0.55 x knocked design Z 32.85 MPa x nut ring 13.6 mm2 (dw 6.9 / D5.5) / 1.5 = 164 N; T = K 0.2 x 5 mm x F = 0.164 N m, rounded down. ASSUMPTIONS TB-TORQUE and K-NUT. This is light: snug, not tight. The threadlocker, not the torque, keeps the nut; the BL-070 hip rows book no preload. Do not use the PAHT path's 2.5 N m: that is the brass insert's limit, and on PPA-CF it would crush the layers under the nut.

1. CHECK the print: 8 through holes in the boss: a 5.5 mm drill shank slides through each by hand, end to end (hold the half to a light). Clear stringing with the drill turned in your fingers.
2. CHECK the nuts: caliper each **HW-HIP-NUT-P** <= 4.1 mm thick; a thicker nut is the wrong standard (ISO 4032), set it aside.
3. Dry-fit: drop a nut into each of the 8 hex traps from the back face: it falls to the seat and does not turn when you push a 3 mm hex key into it. Tight: file the trap walls lightly, never more than 0.2 mm.
4. With the nuts in, put a strip of masking tape over the trap openings so they cannot fall out. Turn the frame wearer face up.
5. Lay the Ti mount on the boss face: every one of its 8 holes shows a through hole under it.
6. One drop of Loctite 243 on the last 5 mm of each **HW-HIP-SCR-P** (Ti gr5 (Ti-6Al-4V) M5 x 20 low-head socket cap screw, DIN 7984) thread. Keep it off the print (wipe any drip with IPA at once).
7. Start every screw by hand through the mount into its nut; if one does not start, back it out, check the nut sits flat in its trap, try again. Never force it.
8. Tighten in a star order to **0.15 N m** with the 0.1-0.6 N m torque screwdriver and the 3 mm hex bit. Go round twice.
9. CHECK from the back face: each screw tip is level with the nut's top face (0 to 0.8 mm below it is OK: the nut still has 4 full threads). A tip more than 0.8 mm below: wrong screw length, stop.
10. CHECK seating (BUILD_H1 section 12 step 5): push the mount's pin end with the spring scale to 20 N (2.0 kg, 4.5 lb) up, down, left and right: a 0.05 mm (0.002 in) feeler does not enter under the base edge.
11. Paint mark across every screw head and the mount base, and across every nut and the boss back face. Remove the tape. Let the Loctite cure 24 h before any load.

## ASSUMPTIONS on this path

- **K-NUT**: Nut factor K = 0.2 for a Ti screw in an A2 nut with Loctite 243 (as frame_h1.Joint.torque_nm). Settled by: first article: the paint mark does not move in HB-6b.
- **TB-TORQUE**: Hip nut torque = K x d x F, with the preload F capped so the nut's bearing ring (dw 6.9 mm, D5.5 hole) stays at or below the PPA creep line / 1.5: F = creep-rupture 0.55 x knocked design Z x ring area / 1.5. The BL-070 hip rows check each nut seat against the external screw load (T_max per screw, tipping model, FLANGE-RIGID) with no preload term, so a low torque costs no booked margin; Loctite 243 holds the nut. Settled by: HB-6b on the first H1-P frame (paint marks, no turn).
- **SPOOL-PLAN**: Spool 1's left-over after the coupons is the dried swap reserve for both halves (BL-070 SPOOL-SWAP). Settled by: first article.
- The BL-070 assumptions (PPU=PPA, WET-PPA, FAT-PPA, INTERLAYER-0.6, NUT-BEAR, WARP, PPA-TIME, COLD, SPOOL-SWAP) are in `bl070_h1p.json` with what settles each.

## FOUND (for the structural-fea-engineer, BL-072)

- NUT-BEAR booked the nut bearing on a D10 ring (54.8 mm2). An 8 AF hex trap cannot hold a D10 washer, so the seat sees only the nut's own bearing ring (dw 6.9 mm: 13.6 mm2). On the same rule that reads strength 1.88 (goal 1.5), fatigue **1.06 (goal 1.15)**, creep 3.10 (goal 1.5). The rule takes Z TENSILE strength for compression across the layers, which is conservative; HB-6b settles it. Not changed here.
