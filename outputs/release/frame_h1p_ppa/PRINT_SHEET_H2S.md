## 3. Step by step, each half

1. Dry the spool you start on (a FULL spool: spool 2 for the lower half, spool 3 for the upper) at 120 C for 10 h. Dry spool 1's left-over at the same time: it is the swap spool.
2. Clean the textured PEI plate with soap and water, dry it, rub a thin even coat of glue stick over the whole footprint and the brim area.
3. Slicer: load the half's STL. Do NOT rotate it. Set the settings of section 1. Add the 15 mm brim. Add the mouse ears: 20 mm discs, 1 layer thick, under both stay feet (lower half) or both upper stay ends (upper half). Supports: tree, from the build plate, as BUILD_H1 section 3. Slice.
4. CHECK the preview: nothing red or outside the plate (brim and supports included); filament and time within 10 % of section 2; no support inside a bore, pin hole, bolt hole or nut trap; the 8 hip nut traps open UPWARD (on the lower half, on the top face). Screenshot and send it before the first half.
5. Plate in, door shut, chamber heater on to 65 C, bed to 110 C. **Wait 30 min** after the chamber reads 65 C.
6. Feed from the dried spool through the dry box. Start the print. Watch the first layer and the brim go down: every brim line stuck, no corner lifting.
7. Be at the printer at the swap time of section 2. When the printer pauses for run-out: take the empty spool off, put the dried swap spool on, feed it until clean filament comes out, resume. If it does not pause, the half is lost: stop and send a photo (ASSUMPTION RUNOUT).
8. When it finishes, leave it on the plate with the door shut until the chamber is below 40 C (warm PPA-CF bends and the plate can pull a corner). Then flex the plate off.
9. Supports off and clean-up exactly as BUILD_H1 section 4. **No anneal** (section 1).
10. CHECK: a corner or a stay foot lifted from the plate by more than 0.5 mm (feeler gauge under the brim edge before removal)? Write it down with a photo: the plate contact of the upper half is only 163 mm2 and warp is the main print risk (BL-070).
