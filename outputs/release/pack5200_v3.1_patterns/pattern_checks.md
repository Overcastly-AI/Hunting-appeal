## Flattening strain

Body panels are meshed in the (q, z) parameter plane and flattened by least squares on every edge (`huntkit.patterns.develop_region`). Strain is the principal stretch of each triangle, area-weighted 99th percentile; slivers (< 12 deg corner) at acute pattern corners are left out of max. Limit 2 %.

**Split decision.** Drafted on the smooth v3.1 surface, the frame panel needed 10.5 % and the outer face 8.5 % (p99): the 30 mm head setback is a smooth step over z 560-640 (bend radius ~36 mm) and the depth has a slope kink at T_PEAK (z 632). Two ring seams fix it: the SETBACK seam at z 560 and the SHOULDER seam at z 631.2 (= the zip's upper end, 0.8 mm under the kink). Between them the band is ruled ring to ring. The upper face gets two 110 mm darts from the collar seam. The side gores stay one piece (they crease at the two seam heights).

## Note for the pack-designer: geometry.yaml vs the patterns

**`designs/pack_5200/geometry.yaml` still describes the smooth 30 mm head-setback step (z 560-640, kink at T_PEAK z 632). The production patterns do not follow it: they take the setback with two ring seams, SETBACK at z 560 and SHOULDER at z 631.2 (= zip upper end), ruled between, so the frame panel (B-01 / B-01b / B-01u) and the outer face (B-02a-d) are 3 tiers each.** Volume rebuilt from the flats is 85.02 L vs 85.16 L design (see above). Action for the pack-designer: adopt the two seams in geometry.yaml / panels.yaml (seam z levels, tier piece ids) and in DESIGN.md, then rerun `python designs/pack_5200/patterns_v31.py --check`. Until then geometry.yaml and the cut pieces disagree above z 560 by design. Also: BUILD_PLAN.md step 9 still puts the B-12 ports at x +/-100, z 190/330/520; the patterns put them in the swell crests over the fairlead eyes (x +/-115), and step 6 still lists the retired frame stubs.
