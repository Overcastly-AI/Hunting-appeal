## Print settings - production (SLM metal)

- Order: Ti-6Al-4V (TC4) SLM, 30-60 um layers. **Specify: stress relief in the build plate, then HIP (920 C / 100 MPa / 2 h, ASTM F3001)**, support removal, bead blast. The fatigue allowable used here (200 MPa, as-built surface + HIP) is only valid with HIP.
- Orientation: as exported (the STL is already rotated so +Z is the build direction picked by the 45 deg overhang check; see `orientation` in `designs/pack_5200/analysis/gendesign_results.yaml`).
- **Socket bores:** STL bores are at final size (tube OD + 2 x 0.5 mm bondline). Ask for +0.2 mm stock on every bore and ream/bore to final (H9) after HIP; the bond surface must be machined or grit-blasted, never left with partially fused powder. Pin bores (pivot, shelf pivot) reamed to the pin size, H7.
- Surface prep for bonding: grit blast, solvent wipe, sol-gel or silane primer (Ti) within 4 h of bonding.
- AlSi10Mg alternative: same files, stress relieve 300 C/2 h. Lugs come out lighter per cm3 but the allowable is ~4x lower: run `huntkit.gendesign` with `alsi10mg_slm` before switching.

## Print settings - prototype (desktop FDM, PAHT-CF)

- Printer: an enclosed, heated-chamber machine with a hardened nozzle (recommended below).
- **Dry the filament 8-12 h at 80 C** (TDS) and print from a dry box (< 20 % RH).
- Nozzle 0.6 mm hardened steel, 280-290 C; bed 90-100 C (Engineering/High-Temp plate + PVP glue); chamber 45-60 C; cooling 0-40 %; <= 100 mm/s ([Bambu PAHT-CF TDS V2.0](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0380/5149/files/Bambu_PAHT-CF_Technical_Data_Sheet.pdf)).
- **Orientation: as exported.** The FDM STL is rotated so the layer normal is the lug axis with the LOWEST stress normal to the layers (Z strength is about half of XY). Do not re-orient in the slicer.
- Walls: >= 4 perimeters (>= 2.4 mm), 6 top/bottom layers, **100 % infill** for lugs and the pawl (the strength check assumes solid TDS specimens); 40 % gyroid for the webbing guides.
- Supports: Bambu Support for PA/PET interface, tree supports; PAHT-CF holds ~70 deg overhangs.
- **Anneal** 80-100 C for 6-8 h after printing (TDS: 80-130 C, 5-12 h), then let it re-condition 24 h.
- Bore fit: drill/ream sockets to final size; scuff and use the same MMA adhesive. Prototype lugs are for fit, assembly practice and static load tests; see the rating in generative_design.md.

## Recommended desktop printer

**Bambu Lab H2S** - single-nozzle, 340 x 320 x 340 mm, **active heated chamber to 65 C**, hardened-steel nozzle and extruder gears as standard, PAHT-CF on its compatible-materials list; launch price **$1,249** ([3DPrint.com: Bambu Lab launches H2S at $1,249](https://3dprint.com/320284/bambu-lab-launches-h2s-at-1249-exploring-the-strategy-behind-its-price-and-positioning/); [Bambu H2S spec summary (65 C heated chamber, hardened steel nozzle, 340x320x340)](https://www.bambuhub.net/printers/h2s)). The heated chamber is what makes PA-CF lugs print without warping or weak layers (PAHT-CF TDS asks for 45-60 C). Add a filament dryer that reaches 80 C (the AMS dries lower) and a 0.6 mm hardened nozzle. Budget option: P2S Combo ($649 on the Black Friday 2026 preview, [Makers101: Bambu Black Friday 2026, P2S Combo $649](https://makers101.com/bambu-lab-black-friday-2026/)) - enclosed with a hardened nozzle but no active chamber heater, so expect more warping on the large lugs. Confirm prices on the Bambu store before buying.
