## 4. CA-3-PPA: PPA-CF Z tensile + interlayer shear, dry and conditioned 14 d (H2S prints)

**Why:** WET-PPA (knock 0.73), design Z 45 MPa, INTERLAYER-0.6. **Holds:** H1-P full-frame print (BUILD_H1P gate): no H1-P half is printed before CA-3-PPA and CA-1-PPA pass.  
**You need:** 6 Z tensile + 6 SBS dry; 6 + 6 conditioned; same spool, same H2S profile as the frame; Z bars ISO 527-2 1A standing, short-beam blocks 24 x 8 x 4 flat.

**Steps**

1. Print job A and job B of the coupon plan (COUPONS_H1P.md section 2) from spool 1. Label each bar with a paint pen: CA3-Z-D1 to D6 (dry), CA3-Z-C1 to C6 (conditioned), the same for the short-beam blocks (CA3-S-D.., CA3-S-C..).
2. DRY set: as soon as the plate is cool, put the dry bars and blocks in a zip bag with a desiccant pack. Test them within 24 h.
3. CONDITIONED set: into the conditioning box (kit D, about 53 % RH) at room temperature (23 C / 73 F) for 14 days. Write the start date on the box.
4. Measure every bar's gauge width and thickness, and every block's width and thickness, with the caliper at 3 points. Write the smallest on the part.
5. Z tensile (each bar): lever rig static set-up (turnbuckle in place of the spring, as CA-1 step 2). Clamp the 20 mm grip ends 35 mm deep. Turn the turnbuckle about 1 turn every 10 s until the bar breaks. Strength = peak cell force x 4 / (width x thickness). Expect about 2,280 N (232.4 kg on the scale, 513 lbf) at the bar dry (58 kg on the cell).
6. Short-beam shear (each block): 3-point fixture with a D6 mm loading pin on top and two D3 mm support pins 16 mm apart (0.63 in); the block lies flat, as printed. Pull down on the loading pin with the come-along and crane scale (kit A) slowly until the load drops. Shear strength = F_max x 0.75 / (width x thickness). Expect up to about 1,459 N (148.7 kg on the scale, 328 lbf).
7. Look at every broken piece with the loupe: a Z bar should break flat along one layer line; a short-beam block should crack along the middle layer line. Photograph each.
8. For each set (dry Z, conditioned Z, dry SBS, conditioned SBS) type the 6 values into the results sheet. The sheet row 'mean - 2.4 SD' is a spreadsheet formula: =AVERAGE(the 6 values) - 2.4 * STDEV(the 6 values). Type its result.
9. Send the sheet the same day: the frame print waits on it.

**Pass:** (a) conditioned Z tensile, mean - 2.4 SD >= 31.3 MPa; (b) conditioned short-beam shear, mean - 2.4 SD >= 18.8 MPa; (c) record only: dry Z mean (booked >= 45 MPa) and conditioned / dry (booked >= 0.73). Between 31.3 and 0.9 x that: hold the PPA build; below: stay on PAHT-CF.

Note: BL-071: PPA path. The conditioned Z bars also give CA-1-PPA's batch Z strength. ASSUMPTION COND-BOX: the kit's ~53 % RH box stands in for 50 % RH.

## 5. CA-1-PPA: PPA-CF R 0.1 fatigue coupons, Z and XY, at 0.279 x conditioned UTS

**Why:** FAT-PPA (fatigue_1e5_ratio 0.30). **Holds:** H1-P full-frame print (BUILD_H1P gate): no H1-P half is printed before CA-3-PPA and CA-1-PPA pass.  
**You need:** 6 Z-built (load across the layers, the H1-P governing direction) + 6 XY-built, ISO 527-2 1A shape, plus 3 static of each from the same plate for the batch UTS; the Z statics are the CA-3-PPA conditioned Z bars.

**Steps**

1. Use the conditioned bars of job A (XY) and job B (Z) after their 14 days in the box, the same plate as CA-3-PPA.
2. Batch strength first. XY: pull the 3 conditioned XY static bars to failure as CA-3-PPA step 5. These bars need about 4,906 N (500.1 kg on the scale, 1,103 lbf) at the bar (125 kg at the cell; dry up to 171 kg): fit the 200 kg cell (shopping list) and bolt the second 40 x 10 lever bar beside the lever for these 3 pulls (ASSUMPTION LEVER-XY). Z: the batch Z strength is the CA-3-PPA conditioned Z mean (same bars, same plate).
3. UTS_xy = mean of the 3 XY strengths; UTS_z = the CA-3-PPA conditioned Z mean. Peak force for each fatigue bar: F_max = 0.279 x UTS x that bar's own gauge area (width x thickness). F_min = 0.1 x F_max (R 0.1). Booked values: XY about 1,369 N (139.5 kg on the scale, 308 lbf), Z about 464 N (47.3 kg on the scale, 104 lbf) at the bar; the cell reads F / 4.
4. Remove the second lever bar and put the 100 kg cell back. Set the crank throw and the turnbuckle so the cell swings between F_min / 4 and F_max / 4; check over 20 slow turns by hand before the motor runs.
5. Run each bar to 100,000 cycles at 1 Hz (about 28 h; never faster than 5 Hz). The rig stops itself if the bar breaks, its stiffness drops more than 10 % or the force drifts more than 5 %. Z bars first (6), then XY (6).
6. After each run-out look at the gauge with the loupe for a crack or whitening; photograph it. Type the cycles the bar reached in the results sheet (a broken bar: the counter reading when it broke).
7. Any bar that breaks before 100,000 cycles: stop, photograph, send the sheet. Do not replace it with the spare to make the count.
8. Send the rig's CSV log with the results sheet.

**Pass:** all 6 Z-built and all 6 XY-built bars run out to 100,000 cycles at Smax = 0.279 x the batch's conditioned mean UTS (R 0.1).

Note: BL-071: PPA path. 12 x 27.8 h at 1 Hz: about 2 weeks of rig time after the 14 d conditioning. ASSUMPTION LEVER-XY (static XY pulls only).

## 6. CA-4-PPA: PPA-CF 3-point bend modulus and strength at 23 C dry, 40 C wet, 60 C

**Why:** modulus_40c_wet_ratio 0.85, modulus_60c_ratio 0.75 (E-SCALE). **Holds:** H1-P first carry (stiffness and 60 C truck-cab rows); not a print gate.  
**You need:** 5 XY bend bars 80 x 10 x 4 per condition (job A).

**Steps**

1. Job A bend bars: 5 stay dry for 23 C, 5 go in the conditioning box for 14 days (the '40 C wet' set), 5 stay dry (sealed with desiccant) for 60 C.
2. Set the 3-point fixture's support pins 64 mm apart (2.52 in). Bar flat, as printed. Dial indicator (0.01 mm) on top of the bar at mid-span, against the loading pin.
3. Stiffness (each bar): hang 2.0 kg on the loading pin, zero the dial; add 5.0 kg more (7.0 kg total); read the dial (mm). Write the reading. E = span^3 x 49.0 N / (4 x width x thickness^3 x reading) in MPa (mm units). The sheet does this sum when you type the dial reading in the 'dial' row.
4. Strength (each bar, after its stiffness reading): pull down on the loading pin with the come-along and crane scale until it breaks. Strength = 3 x F x span / (2 x width x thickness^2). Expect about 347 N (35.3 kg on the scale, 78 lbf) dry at 23 C (73 F).
5. 23 C dry: the first set, at room temperature.
6. 40 C wet: take the conditioned set out of the box, put it with the fixture in the 40 C box for 1 h, test inside the box (lid open just enough for the hook), one bar at a time.
7. 60 C: set the 40 C box thermostat to 60 C (140 F); check with the thermometer that it holds 58-62 C for 30 min (ASSUMPTION BOX-60). Bars and fixture in for 1 h, test inside as above.
8. Ratios: E(40 C wet) / E(23 C) and E(60 C) / E(23 C), each the mean of 5 over the mean of 5. Type them in the sheet.

**Pass:** E(40 C wet) / E(23 C dry) >= 0.85; E(60 C) / E(23 C) >= 0.75; flexural strength at 60 C >= 152 MPa.

Note: BL-071: PPA path. ASSUMPTION BOX-60: the 40 C box holds 60 C.

## 7. T4c-PPA: -20 C drop of a printed H1-P stay, PPA-CF vs a PAHT-CF control

**Why:** COLD (impact_minus20c_ratio 0.5; Z Charpy 4.3 kJ/m2 vs PAHT 13.3). **Holds:** cold-weather use of a PPA-CF frame; not a print gate.  
**You need:** 3 PPA-CF + 1 PAHT-CF control; soaked >= 4 h at -20 C, dropped within 60 s of removal; stay file designs/pack_5200/print/frame_h1p/test_pieces/t4c_stay_R.stl.

**Steps**

1. Print job C: 3 stays `test_pieces/t4c_stay_R.stl` in PPA-CF with the frame settings (PRINT_SHEET_H2S.md), and 1 in PAHT-CF with the BUILD_H1 settings (the control). Clean the supports off as the frame. Label them T4c-P1..P3 and T4c-A1.
2. Pin a 2.0 kg (4.4 lb) steel slug through the hinge eye with a 5 mm pin (kit A dowel).
3. Freezer soak: put the stays and an oven thermometer in the freezer for at least 4 h. It must read -20 C (-4 F) or colder; a home freezer often reads only -18 C (ASSUMPTION FREEZER): use a chest freezer on its coldest setting and write the reading.
4. Lay a sheet of 19 mm (3/4 in) plywood flat on concrete. Mark 1220 mm (48 in) up a wall from the plywood face.
5. One stay at a time, within 60 s of taking it out: hold it by the slug with the foot down, bottom of the foot at the mark, and let go. Pick it up, back in the freezer for 30 min, then drop it again side-on (stay lying flat, wearer face down).
6. Look at the foot and the hinge eye with the 10x loupe: any crack, any white patch, any line along a layer? Then dye penetrant on the foot and the eye (spray cleaner, penetrant 10 min, wipe, developer): any red line is a crack. Tap the stay with a screwdriver handle: a dull note where it rang before is a crack.
7. Bend: when the engineer has posted the bend load (the 1.5 x C4 set-down stay moment; PENDING, see the note), load the stay in the 3-point fixture to that load and hold 1 min. Look again.
8. Type Y or N per stay and per drop in the results sheet. Photograph every stay, both faces, after each drop.

**Pass:** no crack or whitening (10x loupe, dye penetrant on the foot and the eye), tap test clean, and the coupon then carries 3-point bend at 1.5 x its C4 set-down stay moment without a crack; any Z crack (along a layer) is a fail.

Note: BL-071: PPA path. PENDING (structural-fea-engineer): the bend load in N for 1.5 x the C4 set-down stay moment is not in bl070_h1p.json; the drop part runs without it.
