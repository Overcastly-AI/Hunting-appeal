## 3. Adding the filament change in Bambu Studio (H2S, no AMS)

1. Slice the half. Open the **Preview** tab.
2. Drag the vertical **layer slider** on the right to the layer number in section 2 (lower **134**, upper **101** at 0.3 mm layers). The slider label shows the layer number and its height; check the height reads the change z of section 2 within one layer.
3. **Right-click** the slider handle and pick **Add Pause** (not 'Change Filament': that one is for an AMS). A marker appears on the slider. The printer pauses BEFORE it prints that layer.
4. Slide off and back to the marker to check it sits on the right layer; re-slice if Bambu Studio asks.
5. Send to the printer. Write the predicted time to the pause from the slicer's layer list.
Source: Bambu Lab wiki 'View slicing information' (custom operations per layer: pause, filament change; https://wiki.bambulab.com/en/software/bambu-studio/view-slicing-information); Bambu forum 'Filament change without AMS mid-print' and 'Is there a way to Pause at layer X to manually change filament?' (right-click the layer slider in Preview, Add Pause; without an AMS use Add Pause, not Change Filament; https://forum.bambulab.com/t/filament-change-without-ams-mid-print/2589 , https://forum.bambulab.com/t/is-there-a-way-to-pause-at-layer-x-to-manually-change-filament/3005).

At the pause (be there before it): open the external spool holder only, not the door; cut the filament at the extruder inlet, pull it back, load the next dried spool through the dry-box tube until clean PPA-CF purges, check the chamber reads 65 C (149 F; ASSUMPTION PAUSE-HEAT), press Resume. **Within 10 minutes** (BL-073: CA-3-HW tests a 20 min pause layer, so 10 min has margin).

## 4. Step by step, each half (after the gate) and the first-article upper half (now)

1. Dry the two spools of SPOOL_PLAN for the half at 120 C for 10 h: the start spool and a FULL change spool.
2. Clean the textured PEI plate with soap and water, dry it, glue stick over the whole footprint, the pad feet and the brim area.
3. Slicer: load the half's `_flat_print.stl`. Do NOT rotate it. Settings of section 1, 15 mm brim. Supports: tree, from the plate. Do not add mouse ears: the pads are in the file. Slice.
4. CHECK the preview: nothing outside the plate; every pad foot is on layer 1; filament and time within 10 % of section 2; no support inside a bore, pin hole or the 8 hip holes. Add the pause (section 3). Screenshot.
5. Plate in, door shut, chamber heater on to 65 C, bed 110 C. **Wait 30 min** after the chamber reads 65 C.
6. Feed from the start spool through the dry box. Start. Watch the first layer: every pad foot and the brim stuck.
7. The change: section 3.
8. When it finishes, leave it on the plate with the door shut until the chamber is below 40 C (104 F). Feeler gauge under each pad foot and the brim edge: write the largest gap. Then flex the plate off.
9. **Pads off:** hold the part, put the flush cutters flat against the part at each neck (the D3 waist) and snip; or rock the column sideways until the neck snaps. Never lever against the part. Sand each scar flush with 180 then 320 grit on a block; no dent deeper than 0.6 mm (0.024 in).
10. Supports off and clean-up as BUILD_H1 section 4. **No anneal**.
11. CHECK the change layer with the 10x loupe all round: no gap, no blob you can catch a fingernail on. Photograph it.
