# BUILD_H1: how to build the H1 frame (Pack 5200)

Generated by `designs/pack_5200/frame_h1_build.py` (BL-054, manufacturing-engineer). **Do not edit by hand: re-run the script.** Every number is read from the STL files, the material records and the frame code when the script runs; lines marked ASSUMPTION are listed at the end with the test that settles each.

Read this file top to bottom, once, before you buy anything. Do the steps in order. Each step says what you need, what to do, and how you know it worked (CHECK). Metric first, inches in brackets. If a CHECK fails, stop and send a photo; do not work round it.

3. **Plate preview with supports on** (review 5 m-10). Load each half into Bambu Studio with the profile of section 3, supports on, brim on. Slice. CHECK, for each half: (a) nothing is red or outside the plate, including the support base, the brim and the purge / skirt keep-out; (b) the preview's filament and time are within 10 % of section 3; (c) no support is drawn inside a bore, pin hole, bolt hole or nut trap. Screenshot each preview and send it. ## 4. Take the supports off and clean up

1. Let the plate cool to room temperature before you lift the part (warm PAHT-CF bends).
2. Break tree supports off from their tips toward the trunk with pliers; cut stubborn ones with flush cutters. Work from the middle of each stay outward.
3. Sand support scars on the wearer face of the stays flush with 120 grit, then 240, sanding ALONG the stay (never across it). Take off no more than the scar. **[PLACEHOLDER BL-053]** BL-053 says whether the wearer face of the stays is fatigue-critical and whether a sanding allowance is needed (review 5 B-1 fix 4).
4. Pick every bore, pin hole and nut trap clean with a pick and a 3 mm drill bit turned by hand.
5. CHECK: no white (stress-whitened) patch on a stay; no layer split you can catch with a fingernail; every hole open end to end (hold it to a light).

## 5. Anneal

1. Put each half on a flat plate in the oven, a 16 mm rod through each crown bore so the bores stay straight.
2. 90 C (194 F) for 6 h. Turn the oven off and let the parts cool inside it, door shut.
3. If the parts sit out for more than a day afterwards, dry them again: 80 C, 4 h.

## 6. Weigh (before reaming)

Weigh each half after support removal and annealing. Expected (STL x density): lower 425.6 g (15.01 oz), upper 543.5 g (19.17 oz), together 969.1 g (34.18 oz) (2.14 lb).

CHECK: the two halves together read **940 to 1008 g** (33.2-35.6 oz; ASSUMPTION MASS-BAND). Heavier: over-extrusion, check the flow ratio before the next print. Lighter: gaps or wet filament; look at a broken support stub for voids. Write both weights on the build sheet.

## 7. Bolt the halves together (the z 234 joint)

1. Ream the 4 bolt holes in each half to **5.1 mm (0.201 in)**, from the split face outward, slowly, square to the face (a drill guide helps).
2. Dry-fit an M5 nut (HW-M5-NUT) in each of the 4 side traps of the lower half: it slides in from the side and does not turn. Tight: file the slot lightly, never more than 0.2 mm.
3. Sand both split faces flat on 120 grit taped to the flat board, about 10 strokes, until they touch over the whole stay section with no rocking.
4. Into each side trap: a 12 mm washer (**HW-J234-WSH**) then a nut; the washer goes on the split-face side, under the ledge.
5. Stand the halves on the board with a 16 mm rod through both crown bores.
6. A 12 mm washer (HW-J234-WSH) under each head; drop the **M5 x 45** screws (**HW-J234-SCR**) into the upper half's counterbores, through the split, into the nuts; one drop of blue threadlocker on the threads.
7. Tighten to **1.0 N m** (9 in lbf: snug, two fingers on a short key), cross pattern, twice. Do not over-torque: the joint takes no credit for clamping, and the nylon creeps.
8. CHECK: no step you can feel with a fingernail across the split on any face.
9. Ream each crown bore to **18.0 mm (0.709 in) in one pass** from the top, through the split. Scribe a line across the split on each stay: these two halves are now a matched pair. Press the two stay-top UHMWPE bushings (HW-BUSH) into the tops of the bores. CHECK: a crown leg with its 70 mm bushing slides the full travel and does not catch at z 234.

## 8. US2 clevises

> **[PLACEHOLDER BL-053]** review 5 M-2: the clevis joint's nut traps in the current print (x +/-145.8 to 151) are square slots sized for the 10 mm washer, so the 8 mm nut spins in them. BL-053 re-cuts them as a hex pocket. Until the re-cut STL is in this folder, use the fallback in step 2.

1. Ream the two bolt holes in each clevis and in the lower half's US2 block (printed 4.8) to **5.1 mm (0.201 in)**.
2. Into each side trap, from the wearer face: a 10 mm DIN 433 washer (**HW-US2-WSH**) then an M5 nut. If the nut turns in the trap (old print), hold it with a thin 8 mm open spanner slid in from the wearer face while you tighten.
3. Put the clevis on its block, flat face to flat face at x +/-163, throat floors flush. Push the **M5 x 35** screws (**HW-US2-SCR**, not the z 234 M5 x 45) into the counterbores in the clevis's outer face, **no washer under the head**. Blue threadlocker, snug (about 1 N m).
4. CHECK: a steel rule laid across the clevis outer face does not touch the heads (design 0.5 mm below the face); no step in the throat floor across the joint line (break the edges 0.3 mm if the eye catches).
5. Ream the US2 eye through both cheeks, assembled, to 7.2 mm so they line up (step 9).

## 9. Fairlead eyes, sleeves and pins (12 stations)

Stations, both stays: hinge z 60, US1 z 95 (lane x +/-70), US2 z 142 (lane x +/-165), IM1 z 190, IM2 z 330, IM3 z 520.

1. Ream every eye hole to **7.2 mm (0.283 in)** from both sides (24 cheeks).
2. Slide one UHMWPE sleeve (**HW-PIN-SLV**, OD 7.2 / ID 5.2) into each cheek, flush with both faces: 10 mm long at the hinge, 8 mm at the other 10 stations (ASSUMPTION PIN-SLEEVE). 24 sleeves per frame.
3. Pins: hinge (z 60) = **Ti-6Al-4V, 5 x 44 mm** (**HW-PIN-TI**, 2; not 7075: the 1019 N hinge row fails a 7075 pin). The other 10 = 7075-T6, 5 mm (**HW-PIN-AL**).
4. **[PLACEHOLDER BL-053]** pin retention (review 5 M-5): BL-053 designs one retention scheme for all 12 pins (head, retainer, tether, removal by hand for bag-off). The pins go in at bag fitting (bag op list), not at frame build; buy them when the BL-053 part list is in.
5. CHECK: a 5 mm pin goes through cheek, sleeve, sleeve, cheek at every station by hand, without forcing.

## 11. Pawl

1. Ream the pivot bore (printed 7.6, at z 500 through both cheeks of the pawl housing) to **8.0 mm (0.315 in)** from each side.
2. Bond one 304 sleeve (**HW-PAWL-SLV**, OD 8.0, ID 4.2, 8 mm long) into each cheek with a thin film of MMA, flush with the cheek's outer face (ASSUMPTION SLEEVE-BOND: a reamed 8.0 bore gives no press fit). Wipe the bore of the sleeve clean. Cure 24 h.
3. Fit the **low-profile** pawl (`crown_pawl_lp_fdm_pacf` for P1; the SLM Ti one for production; never `../crown_pawl_*`) on the 4 x 50 mm pin (**HW-PAWL-PIN**) with the 0.5 mm music-wire torsion spring (**HW-PAWL-SPR**) on the pin beside it: one leg on the pawl's tang, the other on the window's lower edge. E-clip each pin end (**HW-PAWL-CLIP**, ASSUMPTION PAWL-PIN).
4. Tie the release cord through the pawl's arm-end hole; lead it out through the fore-wall window.
5. CHECK (after the crown is in, step 13): pull the cord to the stop, the rack slides both ways; let go, the pawl snaps back into the teeth. Do it 100 times.

## 12. Hip mount

1. Heat-set 4 M5 brass inserts (**HW-HIP-INS**) into the printed hip boss, square to the face, flush (ASSUMPTION HIP-INSERTS). Practise on the HB-6 insert blocks first.
2. Fit the SLM Ti pivot mount with its M5 screws, blue threadlocker, 2 N m.
3. **OPEN (found by BL-054):** the H1 print's hip-boss pilot holes are not in the H1 records; check the boss on the print against the Ti mount's hole pattern before heat-setting; send a photo if the holes are not there.

### 13a. Spine clamp coupon first (T6(c))

Make 3 coupons exactly as the frame: the clamp (drawing `drawings/spine_clamp_7075.pdf`) on a 100 mm top-bar offcut and a 100 mm spine offcut, same prep, same beads, same MMA. Pull the spine out of the socket along its axis to failure. PASS: mean >= 1.5 x 384 N = 576 N (59 kg), lowest >= 1.25 x = 480 N, failure cohesive or in the laminate (not a clean peel). The limit axial force is the largest spine end force over every rev C case on kits S / M / L (`crown_spine_clamp.joint_loads`). Make 3 more and TWIST them: clamp the bar offcut in V-blocks, lever on the spine offcut, torque the saddle about the bar to failure: PASS mean >= 1.5 x 35.6 N m = 53.4 N m (473 in lbf), lowest >= 1.25 x (the largest limit torque jump in the bar across the clamp, same source). The saddle's bond peak is 0.81 with the transverse shear added (sensitivity), so this coupon is the evidence for the clamp (load_standard.md T6 rule).

### 13b. Order (do not change it)

1. **Dry fit** the whole crown on the frame with tape, no glue: legs with bushings in the bores at the kit's shortest setting, the clamp's saddle on the bar centre, the spine (rack on) in its channel, socket over the spine top. CHECK: the spine slides its channel over the kit's whole range; the pawl engages the rack.
2. **B-25 gate (bag op 7.19, B25-FIT):** with the crown dry-fitted at kit S's shortest setting, the sewer dry-fits the B-25 scrap strip. The bag op says what passes.
3. Take the crown apart. Cut the rack to the spine length minus 15 mm and bond it to the spine's fore face (ASSUMPTION RACK-CUT). Bond one 70 mm UHMWPE bushing (HW-BUSH) on the bottom end of each leg (frame.yaml 'low bushing'). Cure 24 h.
4. Slide the clamp's saddle onto the top bar to the bar centre (it is a closed ring: it cannot go on later).
5. **Slide both B-25 bar sleeves onto the top bar now**, one from each free end, outboard of the clamp (bag op 7.19: the sleeves are sewn closed off the bar, round an 18 mm dowel). They sit 26.5-64.5 mm (1.04-2.54 in) each side of the bar centre. **Once the corners are bonded they cannot go on.** Thread the spine through the B-25 spine-wrap loop.
6. Bond the two corners (leg top to bar end): cope, sand 120 grit, IPA wipe, MMA with 0.8 mm beads, carbon wrap 3 plies 30 mm onto each tube, ply-drop 8 mm, peel ply, shrink tape, RT cure + 65 C post-cure (`../frame/PRODUCTION.md` 'Corner wrap' is the source of this recipe; it is the only part of that file you use). Keep the B-25 sleeves and the clamp away from the wrap with PTFE tape.
7. Bond the clamp (ASSUMPTION FRAME-AS-JIG): crown seated in the frame at the kit's shortest setting, PTFE tape over the print round the channel. Saddle ID 19.6 on the 18 mm bar, socket ID 21.6 on the 20 mm spine: both 0.8 mm bondlines set by the beads. Sand both tubes and the fitting bores 120 grit, IPA. Butter, assemble, spine fully home in the socket, 24 h.
8. CHECK: crown slides through the kit's range with the pawl clicking; release cord frees it; the B-25 sleeves turn on the bar by hand.
