# BUILD_H1: how to build the H1 frame (Pack 5200)

Generated by `designs/pack_5200/frame_h1_build.py` (BL-054, manufacturing-engineer). **Do not edit by hand: re-run the script.** Every number is read from the STL files, the material records and the frame code when the script runs; lines marked ASSUMPTION are listed at the end with the test that settles each.

Read this file top to bottom, once, before you buy anything. Do the steps in order. Each step says what you need, what to do, and how you know it worked (CHECK). Metric first, inches in brackets. If a CHECK fails, stop and send a photo; do not work round it.

> BL-053 data is in use (`designs/pack_5200/print/frame_h1/print_settings_bl053.json, designs/pack_5200/analysis/h1_rev5.json`): supports, the US2 hex nut traps, the stronger US1 / US2 lugs and the pin retention below come from it. BL-055 Part A data is in use (`designs/pack_5200/analysis/h1_closeout.json`): the Ti US1 pins (section 9) and the hip insert pattern with its printed pilot holes (section 12).

## 4. Take the supports off and clean up

1. Let the plate cool to room temperature before you lift the part (warm PAHT-CF bends).
2. Break tree supports off from their tips toward the trunk with pliers; cut stubborn ones with flush cutters. Work from the middle of each stay outward.
3. Support scars: support contact lands on wearer-facing (-y) surfaces only: the stays' wearer faces (2224 + 8755 mm2) and the undersides of the lugs, fairlead roots and lane blocks; the lugs' governing spots (US1 outer cheek / floor corner, US2 arm root bag face, hinge inboard cheek corner) face up or sideways and carry no scar. **These faces are fatigue-critical.** sand the support witness on the stays' wearer faces and the lug undersides flat with 240 then 400 grit, removing at most 0.2 mm (SAND-ALLOW); deburr every bore edge Measure with the caliper before and after on one stay: at most 0.2 mm (0.008 in) off. Sand ALONG the stay, never across it (BL-053, ASSUMPTION SAND-ALLOW).
4. Pick every bore, pin hole and nut trap clean with a pick and a 3 mm drill bit turned by hand.
5. CHECK: no white (stress-whitened) patch on a stay; no layer split you can catch with a fingernail; every hole open end to end (hold it to a light).

## 5. Anneal

1. Put each half on a flat plate in the oven, a 16 mm rod through each crown bore so the bores stay straight.
2. 90 C (194 F) for 6 h. Turn the oven off and let the parts cool inside it, door shut.
3. If the parts sit out for more than a day afterwards, dry them again: 80 C, 4 h.

## 6. Weigh (before reaming)

Weigh each half after support removal and annealing. Expected (STL x density): lower 494.6 g (17.45 oz), upper 543.4 g (19.17 oz), together 1038.0 g (36.61 oz) (2.29 lb).

CHECK: the two halves together read **1007 to 1080 g** (35.5-38.1 oz; ASSUMPTION MASS-BAND). Heavier: over-extrusion, check the flow ratio before the next print. Lighter: gaps or wet filament; look at a broken support stub for voids. Write both weights on the build sheet.

## 7. Bolt the halves together (the z 234 joint)

1. Ream the 4 bolt holes in each half to **5.1 mm (0.201 in)**, from the split face outward, slowly, square to the face (a drill guide helps).
2. CHECK each nut with the caliper before it goes in: thickness <= 4.1 mm (0.161 in); a thicker nut is the wrong standard, set it aside. CHECK each M5 x 45 screw with the caliper too, under the head to the tip: **44.5-45.5 mm** (1.752-1.791 in; ASSUMPTION BOLT-LEN). The nominal screw leaves 4.0 mm to the end of the tail hole and ends 0.9 mm past the nut (bl058.json nut_check); outside the window set it aside. Dry-fit an M5 nut (HW-M5-NUT, DIN 934) in each of the 4 side traps of the lower half: it slides in from the side and does not turn. Tight: file the slot lightly, never more than 0.2 mm.
3. Sand both split faces flat on 120 grit taped to the flat board, about 10 strokes, until they touch over the whole stay section with no rocking.
4. Into each side trap: a 12 mm washer (**HW-J234-WSH**) then a nut; the washer goes on the split-face side, under the ledge.
5. Stand the halves on the board with a 16 mm rod through both crown bores.
6. A 12 mm washer (HW-J234-WSH) under each head; drop the **M5 x 45** screws (**HW-J234-SCR**) into the upper half's counterbores, through the split, into the nuts; one drop of blue threadlocker on the threads.
7. Tighten to **1.0 N m** (9 in lbf: snug, two fingers on a short key), cross pattern, twice. Do not over-torque: the joint takes no credit for clamping, and the nylon creeps.
8. CHECK: no step you can feel with a fingernail across the split on any face.
9. Ream each crown bore to **18.0 mm (0.709 in) in one pass** from the top, through the split. Scribe a line across the split on each stay: these two halves are now a matched pair. Press the two stay-top UHMWPE bushings (HW-BUSH) into the tops of the bores. CHECK: a crown leg with its 70 mm bushing slides the full travel and does not catch at z 234.

## 8. US2 clevises

BL-053 re-cut the nut traps (review 5 M-2): a 10 mm washer slot at the nut face, then a hex pocket that holds the nut by its flats; thinnest wall 2.3 mm (>= 2 mm). Bolt axes at z +/-7 mm about the pin. BL-056 (CLEVIS-SHARE B at share 1.0): the joint blocks grew upward to z pin +19 mm (was +14.5) and the nut face moved 1 mm (0.04 in) INBOARD: each nut trap now runs x +/-144.8 to 150 (was to 151), so the nut sits 1 mm deeper; the 35 mm screw still reaches through it (hip_mount_bl056.md 7). Use only the clevises and lower half printed from the files in this folder (dated 2026-11-21 or later): a clevis from an older print does not mate with the grown block.

1. Ream the two bolt holes in each clevis and in the lower half's US2 block (printed 4.8) to **5.1 mm (0.201 in)**.
2. CHECK each nut with the caliper before it goes in: thickness <= 4.1 mm (0.161 in); a thicker nut is the wrong standard, set it aside. CHECK each M5 x 35 screw with the caliper too, under the head to the tip: **34.7-35.3 mm** (1.366-1.390 in; ASSUMPTION BOLT-LEN). The nominal screw leaves 0.5 mm to the end of the tail hole and ends 1.0 mm past the nut (bl058.json nut_check); outside the window set it aside. Into each side trap, from the wearer face: a 10 mm DIN 433 washer (**HW-US2-WSH**) then an M5 nut (DIN 934). CHECK: the nut slides into its hex pocket and does not turn with a finger.
3. Put the clevis on its block, flat face to flat face at x +/-163, throat floors flush. Push the **M5 x 35** screws (**HW-US2-SCR**, not the z 234 M5 x 45) into the counterbores in the clevis's outer face, **no washer under the head**. Blue threadlocker, snug (about 1 N m).
4. CHECK: a steel rule laid across the clevis outer face does not touch the heads (design 0.5 mm below the face); no step in the throat floor across the joint line (break the edges 0.3 mm if the eye catches).
5. Ream the US2 eye through both cheeks, assembled, to 7.2 mm so they line up (step 9).

## 9. Fairlead eyes, sleeves and pins (12 stations)

Stations, both stays: hinge z 60, US1 z 95 (lane x +/-70), US2 z 142 (lane x +/-165), IM1 z 190, IM2 z 330, IM3 z 520.

1. Ream every eye hole to **7.2 mm (0.283 in)** from both sides (24 cheeks).
2. Slide one UHMWPE sleeve (**HW-PIN-SLV**, OD 7.2 / ID 5.2) into each cheek, flush with the faces (ASSUMPTION PIN-SLEEVE): 10 mm long x 4 (hinge cheeks); 8 mm long x 18 (US1, IM1-IM3 and the US2 inner cheeks); 6.5 mm long x 2 (US2 OUTER (clevis) cheeks: the pin head's counterbore takes the rest). 24 sleeves per frame. The US2 outer sleeve ends flush with the counterbore floor, under the pin head (BL-058: bearing SF 1.06 at the bounding corner, goal 1.0). CHECK: caliper each US2 outer sleeve 6.5 mm before it goes in.
3. Pins, all 5 mm, headed: **Ti-6Al-4V** (**HW-PIN-TI**, 4): hinge (shank 45.1 mm, 4.26 g), us1 (shank 41.1 mm, 3.91 g); **7075-T6** (**HW-PIN-AL**, 8): us2 (shank 39.6 mm, 2.31 g), im1 (shank 41.1 mm, 2.48 g), im2 (shank 41.1 mm, 2.48 g), im3 (shank 41.1 mm, 2.48 g) (source `designs/pack_5200/analysis/h1_closeout.json`). Keep the two kinds apart: a Ti pin weighs 1.58 x a 7075 pin of the same size (4.43 vs 2.81 g/cm3, material records), so CHECK each pin on a 0.01 g scale against its weight above (within 0.1 g) before it goes in.
