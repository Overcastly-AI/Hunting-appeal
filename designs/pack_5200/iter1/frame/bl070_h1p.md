## Design steps

## ASSUMPTIONS

- ASSUMPTION PPU=PPA: 'PPU' is Bambu PPA-CF (BL-069). Settled by: owner confirmation.
- ASSUMPTION WET-PPA: Design Z 45 MPa (57 - 2.4 SD) x wet knock 0.73 (Raise3D conditioned / Bambu dry). Settled by: CA-3-PPA.
- ASSUMPTION FAT-PPA: fatigue_1e5_ratio 0.30 (PA12-CF lower bound), on the layer rule's FI. Settled by: CA-1-PPA.
- ASSUMPTION INTERLAYER-0.6: Interlayer shear strength = 0.6 x Z tensile (frame_topopt layer rule, as PAHT). Settled by: CA-3-PPA SBS.
- ASSUMPTION LINEAR-Z: Where interlayer shear governs (92 % of the R1 top 1 %), a margin scales with knock x Z. Settled by: re-run check_body with the coupon values.
- ASSUMPTION E-ISO / E-SCALE: Voxel FE isotropic at E_xy (the stress fields are unit-modulus, so strength checks do not depend on it); buckling at E_xy x 0.85 hot x 0.85 creep = 8526 MPa. Layer-normal bound x E_z / E_xy = 0.364. Settled by: CA-4-PPA (40 C wet, 60 C); T1 deflection at 150 lb.
- ASSUMPTION JOINT-Z: z 234 joint and US2 clevis capacities on the PPA joint dict: washer / contact bearing on knock x XY, plug shear-out and clevis hole bearing on knock x Z; contact stiffness E x 0.85. Settled by: CA-3-PPA.
- ASSUMPTION INS-PPA: Insert pull-out on PPA either by the record rule (hot x Z/XY TDS ratio 0.339: 297 N) or by the PAHT HB-6 basis scaled by knocked Z (448 N); both fail the 8-insert fatigue row (needs 488 N). Settled by: HB-6-PPA only if inserts are kept.
- ASSUMPTION NUT-BEAR: H1-P hip mount through-bolts: nut / D10 washer bearing on the layers in compression taken at knock x design Z (32.9 MPa) on the ring D10 / D5.5; tipping model and rigid flange as BL-055 (FLANGE-RIGID). Settled by: HB-6b on the frame (hip mount proof) with the through-bolts.
- ASSUMPTION H1P-RULES: H1-P moves material only on the 2 mm voxel model of the released print: interfaces, crown sleeves, member floors, lash points and the wearer (plate) face frozen; thinning one surface layer at a time where the PPA utilisation (6 mm max filter) is low, growth where it is high. Settled by: the checks on the export.
- ASSUMPTION PPA-TIME: Print time by the BL-053 rule (8 mm3/s x 1.08) at the PAHT volume rate; TDS speed < 100 mm/s. Settled by: slicer preview.
- ASSUMPTION WARP: PPA-CF (E x 3, crystallises at 178 C) warps more than PAHT on a small plate contact; chamber pre-heat 30 min at 65 C, glue, brim 15 mm, mouse ears at the stay feet. Settled by: first article.
- ASSUMPTION COLD: impact_minus20c_ratio 0.5; Z Charpy 4.3 kJ/m2. Settled by: T4c-PPA.
- ASSUMPTION SPOOL-SWAP: A half that needs > 0.75 kg is started on a full dried spool with a second dried spool staged (AMS feed of PPA-CF on the H2S not verified). Settled by: first article.
