### 3. SHARE-GAUGE

The US2 gauge (arm root, bag face, x 126.96) was read on the exported H1-P mesh with loading B's pin load on the inner cheek only and on the outer cheek only (`h1_rev5.sub_fea(cheek=...)`): -6.18 and -14.43 MPa per kN; both cheeks -10.20 (the mean of the two to 1.0 %). The reading is **linear** in the share but **not proportional**: at share 0 it reads r = 0.43 of the share-1 reading (placement band 0.41-0.44; a beam read on the lever arms gives 0.46). The BL-074 rule share = u / u1 reads s + (1 - s) r: the design share 0.6 would read 0.77 and the line 0.79 would read 0.88, so it over-reads (safe, but it fails a sound clevis).

**Corrected:** add reading 0 (shim on the INNER cheek, share 0) = u0; share = (u - u0) / (u1 - u0); pass <= **0.79** (unchanged in share terms). u0 / u1 must read 0.41-0.44, else the gauge is off its site. Without u0 the pass line in gauge terms is **u / u1 <= 0.87** (r at the low end of the band). One unit of u / u1 is 1.75 units of share, so gauge scatter counts 1.75 x.
