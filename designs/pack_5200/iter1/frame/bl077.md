| H1c h1_upper_flat | 322 | 174.1 | 29.5 |

Floor = the slowest half's median, rounded up: **175 s** (H1c h1_upper_flat); it is longer than both released H1 medians, so the coupons are as cold or colder than either frame.

Method: park-and-dwell: the coupon job's printer preset gets a per-layer dwell in 'Layer change G-code' (park the nozzle clear of the parts, G4 S<dwell>), so each layer is printed at the frame profile's own speed and then cools with the nozzle away, as a frame interface does while the nozzle works elsewhere on the part; a 15 x 15 mm prime pillar beside the parts takes the ooze after each dwell. Not the slicer slow-down: it keeps the hot nozzle over the small coupon for the whole layer (Vaes 2021: slower printing did not lengthen the cooling that sets the weld) and it stops at 'Min print speed'. Dummy towers only as the fallback if the dwell oozes.

Why time, not volume: The weld between layers is set by the interface temperature history (Seppala 2017; Vaes 2021): the time the layer below cools before the next road lands, with the nozzle away. A tower only buys that time; its volume does nothing else, so a dwell with the nozzle parked gives the same history without the filament.

Sources: Seppala, Han, Hillgartner, Davis, Migler, 'Weld formation during material extrusion additive manufacturing', Soft Matter 13 (2017) 6761-6769, doi:10.1039/C7SM00950J (NIST): the interface temperature history (time above Tg, via the rheological shift factor) sets interdiffusion and the weld strength; Vaes, Coppens, Goderis, Zoetelief, Van Puyvelde, 'The extent of interlayer bond strength during fused filament fabrication of nylon copolymers', Polymers 13 (2021) 2677, doi:10.3390/polym13162677: weld time from the measured thermal history predicts the PA bond; halving the print speed (11 -> 5.5 mm/s) doubled the build time but gave no clear change in weld time or bond strength; Bambu Lab wiki 'auto cooling' (https://wiki.bambulab.com/en/software/bambu-studio/auto-cooling) and OrcaSlicer wiki 'Material cooling' (https://www.orcaslicer.com/wiki/material_settings/cooling/material_cooling): 'Slow printing down for better layer cooling' lowers the print speed until the layer takes the 'Layer time' threshold, never below 'Min print speed'; forum request 'Park hotend for minimum layer time' (https://forum.bambulab.com/t/park-hotend-for-minimum-layer-time/70222): Bambu Studio cannot park the nozzle to make up the time; OrcaSlicer wiki 'Machine G-code' (https://www.orcaslicer.com/wiki/printer_settings/machine%20gcode/printer_machine_gcode): the 'Layer change G-code' is inserted at every layer change after the Z lift (same field in Bambu Studio, Printer settings > Machine G-code; forum 'Custom G-code for Pause - Wipe - Resume', https://forum.bambulab.com/t/solved-custom-g-code-for-pause-wipe-resume/40620).

## 9. Assumptions

- ASSUMPTION B-BASIS-N5: The Bambu TDS +/- values are standard deviations of an ISO 527 set of n 5 (the ISO 527-1 minimum; the TDS gives no n): B-basis k 3.4066 on the lower published mean of each property. Settled by: CA-3-PAHT at n 10.
- ASSUMPTION WET-PAHT: Wet / dry = 0.82 XY (Bambu's 12-18 % decline, the 18 % floor; the XY bending pair reads 0.92) and 0.80 Z (the Z bending pair 49 / 61): bending pairs stand in for tensile. Settled by: CA-3-PAHT hot/wet vs dry.
- ASSUMPTION HOT-40-PAHT: 40 C / 23 C = 0.82 from the Fiberlogy PA12 CF15 UTS curve (Ciganas 2026), the lowest of the direct and two Tg-shift readings; a PA12-CF15 grade stands in for PAHT-CF. Settled by: CA-3-PAHT at 40 C.
- ASSUMPTION TAU-VM: Interlayer shear allowable = Z / sqrt 3 (von Mises), was 0.6 x Z with no source; the frame code's 0.6 x s_z rule is fed s_z = tau / 0.6, so the layer-normal term reads 3.8 % harsh. Settled by: CA-3-PAHT SBS.
- ASSUMPTION E-WET: Hot/wet modulus = 0.716 (Ciganas E 40 / 23 C) x 0.82 (wet strength ratio) = 0.587; buckling rows are scaled by 0.587 / 0.6 from the booked basis. Settled by: CA-4-PAHT.
- ASSUMPTION FAT-0.30: PAHT fatigue at 1e5 = 0.30 x the hot/wet B-basis static strength (record ratio, unchanged). Settled by: CA-1-PAHT.
- ASSUMPTION PERFECT-LOCAL: The bound on any local fix removes the hot-spot regions from the utilisation population and assumes nothing else changes (a real doubler also attracts load: the bound is optimistic for it). Settled by: not needed: the bound already fails.
- ASSUMPTION SLEEVE-PROUD: Each 304 sleeve is cut to the measured boss depth + 0.05-0.15 mm, so the 0.6 N m preload sits on the steel and the PAHT under the plates sees only the hip tipping load (BL-055 model, FLANGE-RIGID). Settled by: HB-6b-SLV (no plate witness mark before load, paint marks after T5).
- ASSUMPTION SLEEVE-TUBE: 304 tube 1/4 in OD x 0.010 in wall (ID 5.84 mm) is bought as cold-drawn tube; the check uses the annealed record yield 215 MPa (sleeve SF 1.22 at the K 0.14 preload). Settled by: caliper on receipt; HB-6b-SLV.
- ASSUMPTION COST-SCOPE: The cap covers PAHT filament + the fasteners / metal parts that join or clamp the printed body; crown, pins, bushings and the SLM Ti hip mount carry over and are outside it. Settled by: orchestrator / owner.
- ASSUMPTION LAYER-DWELL: BL-079 (replaces LAYER-TIME): the coupon jobs hold every layer >= the slowest frame half's median layer time at the real 0.20 mm profile with a per-layer park-and-dwell in 'Layer change G-code' (normal print speed, then the nozzle parked clear for the dwell); matching the cooling time, not the tower volume, is what matches the interlayer weld (Seppala 2017, Vaes 2021). The slicer slow-down is not used: it keeps the nozzle on the part and stops at the minimum speed. Settled by: slicer preview (layer-time view) and the job's logged shortest layer time.
- ASSUMPTION XY-HELD: BL-079 thresholds for H1 as is hold the in-plane (XY) allowable at the booked hot/wet B-basis 43.14 MPa: no coupon here measures the XY tensile B-basis (CA-4-PAHT flexural strength is for the record). Settled by: not settled by these coupons (a later XY coupon if H1 as is comes within reach).
- ASSUMPTION FAT-COMMON: BL-079: the fatigue ratio measured in shear (CA-1-PAHT) is booked to every term of the layer rule (the record's single ratio 0.30); the thresholds are given at the booked 0.30, so a better measured ratio is not credited to the in-plane term. Settled by: CA-1-PAHT.
- ASSUMPTION A2-45: The A2 M5 x 45 DIN 7984 costs the same as the cited A2 M5 x 35 (Zoro 50-pack); the Ti lines are the kit's USD 2.50 shop-price ASSUMPTION. Settled by: price check at purchase.
