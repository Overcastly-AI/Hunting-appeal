# Pack 5200: system design

Owner: pack-designer. Status: **concept v3.1 "SPINE" (2026-11-01)**. v3.1 applies the owner's answers to the v3
open questions and two new owner directions:
1. **Volume**: TRUE 85.2 L in the main bag alone (no collar, pocket or lid counted), with a deeper bag
   (11.5–12 in at the widest) so the fill height drops to ~42 in. Slim teardrop kept: depth tapers from the hips to
   the shoulder blades.
2. **One diagonal zip** (no side zips) confirmed.
3. **The rain fly counts in the base weight.**
4. **Frame v2 integrated** (`frame.yaml`, 577 g, HA-LS-1): straight stays, feet 20 mm inboard, crown axis leans toward
   the head. Hard points from `analysis/frame_hardpoint_changes.yaml` are now in `geometry.yaml`.
5. **The shelf lip is DELETED** (owner: modern bags don't use one). Meat is held by an **internal meat harness**
   (§5) that works inside the bag and with the bag pulled away.
6. **Head clearance** checked with ANSUR II anthropometry (§4.7). It initially FAILED; two design changes fix it.

v3 (2026-11-01, superseded) is kept as Appendix B; v2 as Appendix A.

Parameters: `spec.yaml`. Computed geometry: `geometry.yaml`. Bill of panels: `panels.yaml`. Both are generated by
`build_geometry.py` (`python designs/pack_5200/build_geometry.py`), so every number below traces to that script, a
material record in `data/materials/`, an input file in `analysis/`, or a line tagged `ASSUMPTION:`.
Research basis: `docs/research/pack_market_2026.md` (cited as **[R §n]**, pain points as **P1..P10**); anthropometry
`analysis/anthropometry_ansur2.yaml` (ANSUR II public data, cited inside).

Visuals: concept sketch `outputs/screens/20261101_packv31_concept.png`; head clearance
`outputs/screens/20261101_packv31_headclear.png`; 3D renders `outputs/screens/20261101_packv31_{hero,full,side,haul,fit}.png`;
offline viewer `outputs/pack3d/pack5200_viewer.html`.

**One line:** an 85.2 L (5197 cu in) teardrop roll-top, 11.9 in deep and 15.5 in wide at the shoulder blades,
42.0 in from the bag bottom to the fill line, on the 577 g carbon frame v2. **4.90 lb (2224 g)** complete with
fly, size M belt and the internal meat harness. ONE diagonal zip, no shelf: three 38 mm internal straps cinch meat to
the frame.

## 4. Bag design

### 4.1 Shape (computed, `geometry.yaml → bag.profile`)
Loft of **mixed superellipse rings** along z: outer (aft) half n = 2.5, frame half n = 5.0. Each ring has a half-width
a(t), depth d(t) and a frame-side face y_f(z) = stay centre line + 20 mm **+ the 30 mm head setback above z 560–640**.
t = 0..1 runs from the bag bottom (z 35) to the fill line.

**Solve (owner rule):** D_PEAK = 302 mm is fixed; the half-width control curve is scaled by k and k is bisected so the
fill height that holds exactly 85.2 L equals 42.0 in (1067 mm). Result k = 1.064, fill height **1066 mm (41.97 in)**.

| Station | Width | Depth | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bottom (z 35) | 272 mm (10.7 in) | 190 mm (7.5 in) | narrow at the belt: arm swing, slung rifle butt |
| Shoulder blades (z 635) | **394 mm (15.5 in)** max | **302 mm (11.9 in)** max | fullest section, t = 0.56 (C7 at the 19 in setting is z 603) |
| Collar seam (z 781) | 391 mm | 290 mm | body gores end; roll-top collar above |
| Rolled-down top | — | 62 mm | daily state, **61.2 L (3,737 cu in)**, 804 mm (31.7 in) tall |
| Fill line (z 1101) | 319 mm | 62 mm | full state, **85.2 L**, 1066 mm (42.0 in) tall; open collar +175 mm |

Uniform-fill CG: **y 148 mm** aft of the stay line, z 593 (v3: y 128 / z 691; v2 box 180). Deeper bag and setback move
it 20 mm aft, the shorter bag drops it 98 mm: net the load sits lower and still 32 mm closer than v2 (FEA hand-off).

Honest trade: the owner's 42 in at ≤ 12 in deep needs +0.9 in of width at the shoulder blades (14.6 → 15.5 in). The
alternative (keep 14.6 in) gives a 45.5 in fill height at 12 in deep. Open question Q1.

### 4.7 Head clearance (R-28; `geometry.yaml → head_clearance`, `outputs/screens/20261101_packv31_headclear.png`)

**Inputs (cited, `analysis/anthropometry_ansur2.yaml`):** ANSUR II public data (US Army 2012, n 4082 men / 1986 women,
Penn State OpenLab release; percentiles per sex): vertex above C7 (stature − cervicale height) 214 / 239 / 260 mm,
tragion-to-top 116 / 131 / 141 mm, head length 178 / 200 / 211 mm for 5th F / 50th M / 95th M. Craniovertebral angle
48.8° ± 6.8° (Zárate-Tejero 2024, n 122); cervical extension ROM 59° (Apti 2023); O–C2 share 0.39 (23° of 59°).

**Model (2D, mid-plane):** cranial-vault ellipse from head length and tragion-to-top; neutral posture from the CVA;
extension split 39 % about the occipital condyles and 61 % about C7/T1; C7 sits at torso + iliac crest (z 120), 55 mm in
front of the crown axis (40 mm back pad + 15 mm). Obstacles: crown top bar (OD 18 + wrap), yoke/back pad (to C7 − 10),
bag frame-side face above the crown with a 15 mm stuffed bulge, and, unrolled, the open collar leaning 25 mm toward the
head. Checked at 15/19/23 in × 5th F / 50th M / 95th M × rolled / collar unrolled (18 cases). ASSUMPTIONs are listed in
the YAML and in `build_geometry.py` (BACK_PAD, C7_IN, BULGE, COLLAR_LEAN, vault centre, pivot locations).

**Result.** v3.1 as first solved (frame v2 crown, face on the stay line): **FAIL**, contact at 13–16° (crown top bar
and bag face equally; both sit ~30 mm behind the occiput). Two fixes, adopted (DECISION D-21):
1. **Head setback**: B-01 steps 30 mm aft over z 560–640 (above the shoulders of the smallest torso). Zero volume cost.
2. **Swept crown top bar**: the bar sits 25 mm aft of the leg axis (offset corner wraps); lifter lugs move to the corner
   wraps so the lifter angle stays 40.6°.

| Case (all 3 torsos give the same angles: the crown and C7 move together) | Gap neutral | Gap at 30° | First contact | at CVA 55° (+1 SD) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5th pct female head | 67 mm | 10 mm | **36°** | 27° |
| 50th pct male head | 68 mm | 7 mm | **34°** | 25° |
| 95th pct male head | 75 mm | 11 mm | **36°** | 26° |

**PASS** at the design posture (≥ 34° vs 30° required), rolled and unrolled; the unrolled collar does not govern (it is
above the head at every setting), the swept top bar and the bag face just above it do. **Sensitivity:** a user with an
upright head (CVA +1 SD) touches at 25–27°, i.e. a soft bag contact on a sharp look-up. Loaded hunters lean forward and
carry the head forward (lower CVA), so the mean is the right design case, but the prototype must confirm it (side photos
of 3 testers at the 15/19/23 settings). The orange-flag accessory posts are not in this check (lash points, behind the bar).

### 4.8 Bag-to-frame attachment (branch fairlead)

The production frame is the printed branch body. The toggles and the girth-hitched stubs in the
v3.1 notes were drawn for a carbon-tube stay. On this frame the bag hangs on **eight printed
fairleads**, one part, grown on the bag face of the stays. Record:
`analysis/bag_attachment.md`. Parameters: `attachment.yaml`.

| Station | z | What uses it |
|---|---:|---|
| Hinge | 60 | Sewn 38 mm eye on a tethered pull-pin. Leave the pins in and the bag swings for a bay load. Pull them and the bag comes off. |
| IM1 | 190 | Meat strap only. The bag does not clip here. |
| IM2 | 330 | Bag hook and meat strap, beside the open branch window. |
| IM3 | 520 | Bag hook and meat strap. |

The pin is 5 mm 7075-T6 in double shear (yield SF 14.4 on the 726 N hinge load) inside a UHMWPE
sleeve (bearing SF 2.32). The printed cheek, 1 mm mesh, is strength SF 2.97 and fatigue SF 1.67
at the hinge, which governs. IM3, the heavy aft pull (554 N), is strength SF 3.91 and fatigue
SF 2.20. The bag hook is the 38 mm sliding-gate hook already assumed for the meat harness
(1500 N), SF 2.71 at IM3. The bartack on the hinge eye is the same 2700 N assumption, SF 3.72.

Nothing is added on the wearer side. The branch window stays open. B-01, which sits 20 mm aft of
the stay centre, gets a feathered 16 mm swell, 90 mm long, over each fairlead so the cleat has
about 2 mm of air and does not rub the cloth. The spreader is notched 22 mm in height at each
fairlead, at the edge landing.

Load lifters stay on the carbon crown corner wraps, with a tri-glide keeper behind each ladder
lock. They do not move onto the printed body or the extension posts. The hipbelt stays on the Ti
pivot (checked SF/required 8.6, fatigue 19.7).

Bag-off is still in the design and it is optional. Four hooks and two hinge pins, about 34 s
with gloves. The meat straps then hook eye-to-eye on the same fairleads. Leaving the pins in
keeps a normal bag. Net mass **+113 g**. Pack **2979 g (6.57 lb)**. Marginal cost about **$48**.

A sleeve, a rail, a magnetic buckle, a cam, and a printed clip tongue were compared and set aside.
The clip tongue is fatigue SF 0.28 even with an 18 mm root. The comparison is in the analysis note.

## 5. Meat hauling v3.1: internal meat harness, no shelf (DECISION D-19, owner 2026-11-01)

### 5.1 Research and IP
Leaders moved the meat inside or into a sling: SG Terminus has a 2,200 cu in internal load cell next to the frame;
KUIU ICON 6 has an internal meat sleeve and Load Sling Mode; Exo and Eberlestock clamp the load to the frame instead of
standing it on a shelf [R §2, §4 "sling or V-compression ... users increasingly prefer"]. IP [R §6]: Mystery Ranch
US 8,579,171 (sling between frame and bag) **expired**; US 2014/0263519 (slotted frame receiving a sling) is avoided
because our stubs girth-hitch at nodes, nothing passes through a frame slot; deleting the shelf removes the "adjustable
shelf" element of MR US 10,952,523 claim 1; no SG patents found; KUIU's patent-pending covers its corrugated frame. Get
the attorney FTO before tooling.

### 5.2 Architecture

**Branch-frame update.** The six girth-hitched stubs below are eyes on the fairlead pins (§4.8).
Same heights, same 38 mm straps, same hooks. A cracked cleat is bypassed in the field by hitching
the webbing around the stay, which puts the old path back without a printer. The paragraphs under
this line are the v3.1 tube-frame writeup and stay as the load and hardware record.

- **3 straps, IM1–IM3**, 38 mm `poly_webbing_38mm` (bone/stone colour: blood shows, so it gets washed), at **z 190**
  (lumbar-pan top land on the stay), **z 330** (mid-bar node, the same node the v2 shelf straps loaded) and **z 520**
  (upper-bar node). Never mid-span. Cut lengths 973 / 1023 / 1083 mm.
- **6 frame stubs**: 200 mm of 38 mm webbing girth-hitched round the stay right at the node wrap (cannot slide), sewn
  eye, box-X bartack (tex 135). Hard points `meat_anchor_z190/330/520_R/L` (they replace `shelf_pivot` and
  `shelf_strap_anchor_fixed`).
- **6 ports** in B-01 at x ±100: 45 × 6 mm slot through a 40 × 60 Hypalon patch on the inside face (B-12). The stub eye
  pokes through; the port carries no load and its lips close when empty.
- **Hardware (MEAT family)**: plain webbing strap with an aluminium sliding-gate hook at each end + one 38 mm metal cam
  with a glove pull-tab. To release: flip the cam (one motion). To remove: unhook.
- **Bag on (default meat mode)**: meat in game bags goes in through the roll-top, high and tight against B-01; each strap
  crosses the load and hooks to the two stub eyes; cam tight. The load path is meat → strap → stub → frame node; the bag
  fabric only carries the meat's weight down to the bottom and the hinge pins.
- **Bag pulled back (bay mode, bulk meat)**: pop the 4 LINK toggles, let out W1–W3, the bag hinges back 250 mm at the
  crown on its bottom pins; the stub eyes slide out of the ports. The SAME straps hook stub-to-stub round the meat in the
  V; re-cinch W1–W3 round the bag to close the V. ≤ 90 s.
- **Frame-only**: bag off; IM1–IM3 stub-to-stub + W1/W2 round a bone-in quarter, W3 lashes the top to the crown.
  Without a shelf the vertical hold is friction (5 clamping straps), checked in §5.3; the hock can also hook over IM1.
- **Stow flat**: hook each strap across B-01 port-to-port and cam it flat (it doubles as internal compression for a small
  load), or unhook and roll all three into the fly pocket.
- **Wash**: unhook, machine-wash cold (polyester + anodised Al, no foam, no hook-and-loop); stubs rinse on the frame;
  Hypalon port edges don't fray or wick.
- **External wraps W1–W3 KEPT** (not merged): carry-mode compression, bay closure and antler/cape lashing. An internal
  strap cannot compress the outside of the bag or close the bay; a wrap cannot reach inside it.

### 4.1 Shape (computed, `geometry.yaml → bag.profile`)
The bag is a loft of **mixed superellipse rings** along z: outer (aft) half exponent n = 2.5 (rounded),
frame half n = 5.0 (flat on the frame). Each ring has a half-width a(t), a depth d(t) and a frame-side
face y_f(z) = stay centre line + 20 mm, so the frame-side panel follows the stay S-curve [R §5.3].
t = 0..1 runs from the bag bottom (z 35) to the fill line.

| Station | Width | Depth | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bottom (z 35) | 256 mm (10.1 in) | 178 mm (7.0 in) | narrow at the belt for arm swing and a slung rifle butt; lowest 6 in slightly flared for a sleeping bag [R §8 A risk] |
| Shoulder blades (z 790) | **370 mm (14.6 in)** max | **264 mm (10.4 in)** max | fullest section, t = 0.60 |
| Collar seam (z 916) | 362 mm | 254 mm | body gores end; roll-top collar above |

**Honest note on height.** 85.2 L at ≤ 10.5 in deep and a tapered 10–14.6 in width needs a

cannot hold 85 L at that section: it holds ~64 L. Leaders' published volumes are not measured the same way
(Exo 5000 is quoted at 39 × 10.5 × 9.5 in expanded, a 64 L envelope [R §3.2]). Open question Q1.

### 4.3 Access: why one diagonal zip beats two side zips (DECISION, owner to confirm, Q2)

### 4. Load path

1. **Carry mode.** Load goes from the bag to the frame-side panel, then through the tabs to the
   stays at three heights (z 60, 330 and the crown). The bag bottom sits on the shelf and its
   pivots at z 25. From the stays, ~80 % runs down through the lumbar elbows into the **lumbar
   plate**, then the Ti hipbelt pivot (z 120), the centre plate, the HDPE wing stiffeners and the
   iliac crest. The other ~20 % runs up the stays, through the crown legs and spine, the rack lock
   and the yoke, and into the shoulders. The lifters pull the crown top toward the shoulders and
   keep the load's centre of mass close to the spine.
2. **Meat mode (bag pulled back).** Meat in breathable game bags sits on the shelf, against the
   frame-side panel and liner. The shelf straps run from the aft shelf corners to
   `shelf_strap_anchor_fixed` at the **mid-bar node** (z 330), which turns the shelf load into a
   node load. The demo FEA failed with mid-span shelf loads (`meat_shelf_heavy_low` SF 0.66–0.78).
   The load wings wrap the meat and the bag together. The heaviest item goes lowest and closest to
   the frame.
3. **Frame-only haul.** The bag comes off. The quarter sits on the shelf, held by 3 × 38 mm wing
   straps per side (wide straps, so they don't cut into game bags). The top of the load lashes to
   the crown top bar. The demo frame-only case failed (SF 0.56 at 2.5 g). The FEA engineer must
   size for this case explicitly.
4. **Set-down / drop.** One stay foot hits the ground and the lumbar plate closes the torsion box.
   The demo failed this case at the lumbar tube (SF 0.83).
5. **Bonded joints in shear, never peel.** The elbow fitting is a sleeve with ≥ 25 mm overlap each
   side (Volkersen peak SF 0.79 at the demo elbow). Inserts carry every pin.

### 5. Suspension geometry

#### 5.1 Torso-adjust mechanism: the telescoping crown (DECISION)

**Owner question (v2): "why holes and not a pulley buckle system?"** The answer is to use both, for
different jobs. A pulley is the right **adjuster**: it's continuous and you can pull it while
wearing the pack. It isn't a trustworthy **lock**, because webbing creeps through buckles under
thousands of gait cycles and cam teeth ice up. So v2 keeps the sliding crown (which preserves the
constant 41° lifter geometry) and changes how its height is held:

- **Adjuster: 3:1 webbing pulley.** The webbing dead-ends on the crown spine bottom, runs down to
  fixed guide A on the lumbar-plate top (z 190), back up to moving guide B on the crown spine
  bottom, down to fixed guide C, and out to a metal **cam buckle** at the right lumbar corner (a
  left-side mount exists for left-handers). The guides are UHMWPE-lined. Webbing is 25 mm
  polyester (`poly_webbing_25mm`), 1.4 m long; tail travel is 610 mm for the full 8 in.
- **Lock: two-way toothed rack + spring pawl.** A 20 mm wide PA66-GF30 rack (`pa66_gf30`) with a
  4 mm pitch is screwed into bonded inserts in the carbon spine plate. **No holes go in tubes.** A
  Ti pawl in a POM housing on the upper bar (z 520) has symmetric teeth, so it locks both
  directions. A 2 mm Dyneema cord runs from the pawl to a release toggle on the left shoulder
  strap.
- **Adjusting while worn:** pull the toggle and the pawl lifts.
  - To shorten the torso, pull the pulley tail forward at the hip.
  - To lengthen it, lift the cam tab and shrug: the shoulders push the yoke up.
  - Release the toggle and the pawl snaps into the nearest tooth (≤ 4 mm resolution, effectively
    continuous).
  - Off the back, the crown slides by hand.

**Retention loads** (`geometry.yaml → frame.crown_retention`). Extension means the crown is pushed
up out of the stays: the shoulders lift the yoke while the frame and load hang below it.

### 8. Universal-fit strategy (summary)
1. One frame. The crown sets torso and lifter geometry together, from 15 to 23 in, continuously
   (3:1 pulley, adjustable while worn) with a positive 4 mm rack lock.
2. Four hipbelt sizes on one Ti pivot, plus a 3-position wing cant for hip shape.
3. Two strap shapes (J / S) on one yoke quick-connect, plus a long sternum rail.
4. Lower strap anchors on the frame, so belt swaps don't change the harness.
5. A torso scale in inches is printed beside the rack on the crown spine, so the user reads the
   setting at the pawl, then fine-tunes it while wearing the pack.
6. One bag height for now (owner decision, v2). For a 15 in torso the bag top stands ~220 mm above
   the crown top. The top straps and lid compression stabilise it. **Future option:** a ~70 L
   short bag on the same frame.

### 9. Quiet hunt mode
- No hook-and-loop anywhere (R-13). Fidlock magnetic buckles on the sternum strap and lid front
  instead of side-release clicks.
- Coil zips (quieter than molded teeth), Dyneema cord pulls with silicone sleeves, and garages
  that park the sliders.
- No film laminates (X-Pac/DCF) on exposed faces. 500D Cordura has quietness 3/5. **Future
  option:** a brushed-face archery lid/pocket set (quieter, but picks up grime).
- UHMWPE bushings on the crown legs mean no carbon-on-carbon squeak. The pawl is POM-housed,
  and the pulley guides are UHMWPE-lined. Make crown adjustments before the stalk: the pawl
  clicks once.
- Elastic keepers on every webbing tail. Ladder locks have a soft-touch overmold (vendor option).
- Polyester webbing for the lifters and compression, because it doesn't sag or squeak when wet.

#### Camo-accent panels as functional reinforcement
The accents are angular "shard" polygons (5 shapes, `SH-01..05`) in the owner's base camo. Each is
a **second layer sewn over the solid base panel** exactly where abrasion happens. The edgy look
comes from straight, angled seam lines that are also easy to sew: shards have no curves, so a
beginner can topstitch them.

| Shard | Where | Wear it takes |
|---|---|---|
| SH-01 (L+R) | Lower bag corners, wrapping 120 mm onto the gussets | Set-down on rock, deadfall drag |
| SH-02 | Upper outer-face diagonal | Antler, bow and rifle rub. Lash tabs sew through both layers, so it also reinforces the load points |
| SH-03 | Lid top | Thrown-down impacts |
| SH-04 (L+R) | Side-pocket faces | Brush |
| SH-05 (L+R) | Hipbelt wing tips | Arm swing, brush |

The shards add 99 g in total. NIR and colour matching between the printed polyester and the solid
nylon goes to the camo-designer.

### 12. Decisions, assumptions, open questions

**DECISIONS**
- D-1 (v2, owner question): telescoping crown, **3:1 webbing pulley + cam buckle** as the
  continuous, while-worn adjuster, with a **two-way PA66-GF30 toothed rack + Ti spring pawl** as
  the positive lock. That combination won the §5.1 trade study. The rack holds 5328 N (SF 4.0 on
  the 1334 N requirement). There are no holes in carbon. It meets spec.yaml's "continuously
  adjustable" requirement (lock resolution 4 mm). The v1 pin/holes are superseded.
- D-2 Lifter anchors ride on the crown at yoke + 150 mm, which gives a constant 40.6° lifter angle.
- D-3 Upper stays are parallel (230 mm c-c). All splay happens in the lower segment (270 mm c-c at
  the feet).
- D-4 A lumbar pm45 plate replaces the lumbar tube. Shelf straps anchor at the mid-bar node (from
  the FEA demo failures).
- D-5 The main bag is a box bag, 340 × 788 × 320 mm, computed to 85.24 L. Lid, collar and pockets
  aren't counted.
- D-6 (owner): **ambidextrous**. Full-length side zips on both sides. One rifle boot clips to
  either hipbelt wing, with muzzle loops on both sides. The pulley cam mounts right, with a left
  mount available.
- D-7 Camo appears only as double-layer shard reinforcements (5 shapes).
- D-8 (owner): **one bag height for now**. Short (~70 L) bag is a future option. Hipbelts S–XL,
  J and S straps.
- D-9 (owner): **extension posts are an accessory**. Sockets stay on every crown top bar. Posts are
  excluded from the base weight.
- D-10 (owner): the load shelf stays as recommended (double-layer TPU 840D wings, 1.5 mm HDPE
  stiffener, 532 g group).
- Future options: short bag; brushed-face archery lid/pocket set; staggered double-pawl for 2 mm
  lock resolution.
