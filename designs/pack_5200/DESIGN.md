# Pack 5200: system design

Owner: pack-designer. Status: **concept v1 (2026-11-01)**. Parameters: `spec.yaml`. Computed geometry:
`geometry.yaml`, bill of panels: `panels.yaml`. Both are generated by `build_geometry.py` (run
`python designs/pack_5200/build_geometry.py`), so every number below traces to that script, a
material record in `data/materials/`, or a line tagged `ASSUMPTION:`.
Concept sketch: `outputs/screens/20261101_packconcept_1.png`.

**One line:** an 85.2 L (5200 cu in) meat hauler on a bonded carbon frame. A **telescoping torso
crown** carries the yoke and the load lifters, so one pin adjustment fits 15–23 in torsos and keeps
the lifter angle at 41° for everyone. The bag pulls back 250 mm (10 in) off a fabric load shelf
to make a 40 L meat bay. The frame also works alone in frame-only haul mode.

## 3. System architecture

```
                       ┌── extension posts (accessory, +220 mm, orange flag, antlers)
   CROWN (telescopes) ─┼── top bar: load-lifter anchors x=±70, bag-top buckles
   in upper stays      ├── centre spine: yoke (J or S straps) + index rack (17 holes, 12.7 mm)
                       └── 2 legs, 430 mm, slide in UHMWPE bushings inside the upper stays
   FIXED FRAME ────────┬── 2 segmented stays: foot → lumbar elbow (z 130) → top socket (z 530)
                       ├── upper bar z 520: spring index-latch (POM) engages the crown rack
                       ├── mid bar z 330: shelf-strap + bag-mid anchors (at the nodes)
                       ├── lumbar plate (pm45 shear web) z 50–190: Ti hipbelt pivot z 120
                       └── foot boots: shelf pivots, bag-bottom hinge, bumpers
   SOFT GOODS ─────────┬── harness: J/S straps on the yoke; sternum rail; 38 mm lifters
                       ├── hipbelt S/M/L/XL: centre plate on the pivot; wing cant 0/5/10°
                       ├── load shelf (840D TPU + HDPE) + 2 frame-mounted load wings
                       ├── main bag 340×788×320 box bag; full right side zip; collar; lid
                       └── pockets: lid, under-lid, 2 side, 2 hipbelt; hydration sleeve
```

The bag is fully removable. It attaches with 8 Hypalon tabs: 4 low tabs to the foot boots /
`bag_anchor_low`, 2 mid tabs to `bag_anchor_mid` at the mid-bar nodes, and 2 top tabs that buckle to
the crown top bar with 25 mm webbing. Those top straps are length-adjustable because the crown
moves and the bag doesn't.

## 4. Load path

1. **Carry mode.** Load goes from the bag to the frame-side panel, then through the tabs to the
   stays at three heights (z 60, 330 and the crown). The bag bottom sits on the shelf and its
   pivots at z 25. From the stays, ~80 % runs down through the lumbar elbows into the **lumbar
   plate**, then the Ti hipbelt pivot (z 120), the centre plate, the HDPE wing stiffeners and the
   iliac crest. The other ~20 % runs up the stays, through the crown legs and spine, the index pin
   and the yoke, and into the shoulders. The lifters pull the crown top toward the shoulders and
   keep the load's centre of mass close to the spine.
2. **Meat mode (bag pulled back).** Meat in breathable game bags sits on the shelf, against the
   frame-side panel and liner. The shelf straps run from the aft shelf corners to
   `shelf_strap_anchor_fixed` at the **mid-bar node** (z 330), which turns the shelf load into a
   node load. The demo FEA failed with mid-span shelf loads (`meat_shelf_heavy_low` SF 0.66–0.78).
   The load wings wrap the meat and the bag together. The heaviest item goes lowest and closest to
   the frame.
3. **Frame-only haul.** The bag comes off. The quarter sits on the shelf, held by 3 × 38 mm wing
   straps per side (wide straps, so they don't cut into game bags). The top of the load lashes to
   the crown top bar. The demo frame-only case failed (SF 0.56 at 2.5 g). The FEA engineer must
   size for this case explicitly.
4. **Set-down / drop.** One stay foot hits the ground and the lumbar plate closes the torsion box.
   The demo failed this case at the lumbar tube (SF 0.83).
5. **Bonded joints in shear, never peel.** The elbow fitting is a sleeve with ≥ 25 mm overlap each
   side (Volkersen peak SF 0.79 at the demo elbow). Inserts carry every pin.

## 5. Suspension geometry

### 5.1 Torso-adjust mechanism: the telescoping crown (DECISION)

| Option | For | Against | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook-and-loop yoke (common) | Continuous, cheap | Noisy (banned by R-13), creeps under 2.5 g meat loads | No |
| Sliding yoke on a rail on a fixed-height frame, with pin-lock | Simple, proven | On ONE frame spanning 8 in, the frame top sits 300 mm above a 15 in user's C7. Lifter angle goes from ~70° (useless) to ~40°. A frame short enough for small users can't reach the 23 in yoke height (demo FEA: frame ≥ 634 mm needed) | No for a universal frame |
| Sized frames | Optimal per size | Violates the owner's "universal" decision | No |
| **Telescoping crown: yoke + lifter anchors + top bar on one carriage, pin-indexed** | One adjustment sets torso AND lifter geometry. Lifter angle is 40.6° at every setting. Frame height follows the user (601–804 mm top), so short users get no frame above their head. No holes in tubes. The crown doubles as the tall-load mount | More parts. Leg/socket bending must be checked. Discrete 12.7 mm steps | **Chosen** |

How it works: two 430 mm crown legs slide inside the parallel upper stays on UHMWPE bushings (one
at the stay top, one at z ≈ 380). Positive engagement comes from a spring-loaded POM-housed
**Ti index pin** in the upper-bar latch (z 520). It drops into one of 17 holes (pitch 12.7 mm /
0.5 in) in the carbon centre spine. To adjust: pull the latch ring, slide the crown, and let the
pin click. DECISION: the 0.5 in steps replace "continuous" adjustment, because the positive lock
matters more under meat loads and 0.5 in is below the harness fit sensitivity (shoulder-strap
webbing absorbs ±10 mm). Upgrade option: a second, staggered row gives 6.35 mm steps.

Geometry (from `geometry.yaml → frame.crown_settings`):

| Torso in (mm) | Yoke z | Crown top z (in) | Leg insertion mm | Lifter angle |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 15 (381) | 451 | 601 (23.7) | 359 | 40.6° |
| 17 (432) | 502 | 652 (25.7) | 308 | 40.6° |
| 19 (483) | 553 | 703 (27.7) | 257 | 40.6° |
| 21 (533) | 603 | 753 (29.7) | 207 | 40.6° |
| 23 (584) | 654 | 804 (31.7) | 156 (≥ 150 required) | 40.6° |

ASSUMPTIONs: the yoke anchor is 50 mm below C7 (same as the FEA model). The lifter point on the
strap is 20 mm below C7 and 140 mm forward of the anchor. The iliac crest sits at frame z 120.

### 5.2 Hipbelt pivot and universal hipbelt
- A **single centre pivot** (Ti pin in a bonded Ti insert on the lumbar plate, `hipbelt_pivot`
  (0, −48, 120)) gives free roll of ±8° with an elastomer centring bushing. The hips rock on
  side-hills and in deadfall without the frame twisting the belt.
- **Interchangeable belts S/M/L/XL** (hip 26–30 / 30–34 / 34–38 / 38–42 in). Each belt has a
  PA66-GF30 centre plate that slots over the pivot and locks with a POM latch, tool-free. Wing
  lengths (computed, ASSUMPTION padded = 72 % of hip circumference): S 186, M 223, L 259, XL 296 mm.
- **Wing cant 0/5/10°**: a 3-position snap where each wing joins the centre plate. It fits
  conical hips (more common in women) and straighter hips.
- Stabilizer straps run to `hip_stabilizer_anchor` (z 60) on the lower stays.

### 5.3 Shoulder harness
- The yoke is a 1.5 mm carbon plate on the crown spine. Strap tops attach with a quick-connect
  (webbing loop + Ti pin), so J- or S-straps swap in 2 minutes. Padded length is 440 mm, tapering
  from 72 to 58 mm wide. Foam is EVA 45C load + perforated EVA comfort, with a spacer-mesh face.
- **S-strap**: 35 mm lateral S-offset, which clears the chest. This is the "works for women"
  option, and it suits many men with broad chests too.
- The sternum strap is a Fidlock magnetic buckle on POM rails with 120 mm travel.
- The lower strap ends anchor to `shoulder_strap_lower_anchor` (z 110) on the lumbar plate
  corners, not the hipbelt, so a hipbelt swap doesn't disturb the harness.

## 6. Load shelf and meat-haul system
- **Shelf**: two layers of 840D ether-TPU nylon (`tpu_coated_nylon_840d`) around a 1.5 mm HDPE
  stiffener, 270 × 260 mm (10.6 × 10.2 in). It hinges on the shelf pivots at the stay feet
  (z 25), has 2 drain grommets, and a removable silicone-coated U liner (`S-02`) that washes
  separately.
- **Meat bay**: release the bag-mid and bag-top straps and the bag slides aft up to 250 mm on its
  lengthened straps. The shelf becomes the floor of a ~40 L bay between the frame and the bag.
  The load wings close the sides. The frame-side panel is TPU 840D and the game bags breathe:
  nothing airtight touches the meat.
- **Quarters**: a bone-in hindquarter rides hock-up on the shelf, tight to the frame. Two
  38 mm shelf straps run to the mid-bar nodes and the 3 + 3 wing straps cinch it. Boned meat
  (2–4 game bags) is stacked heavy-low-close.
- **Frame-only mode**: the bag comes off (8 tabs). Shelf + wings + crown lash points carry the
  load.
- **Tall loads**: 220 mm plug-in extension posts with a bar go into the crown top sockets. The
  lifters move up to the extension bar for tall loads (R-10). The FEA must add a tall-load case
  with the posts fitted.
- **Head / antlers / cape**: the cape goes in a game bag in the bay. The skull sits on top of the
  bag, under the lid (the lid detaches and re-buckles over the skull plate on 4 long straps), with
  the antlers pointing up and aft. Twelve Hypalon lash tabs (no daisy loops to snag brush) carry
  the lashing. The **orange flag sleeve** stows in the lid and slides over the crown top bar or
  extension posts for hunter safety when packing out antlers.
- Both rifle and bow carry mount to things that don't move when the bag pulls back. The rifle
  carry is a butt boot on the left hipbelt wing plus a muzzle strap to the left upper compression
  strap. The bow carry is a riser held by two 25 mm straps on the outer face, with a cam/limb boot
  (`B-12`) low on the outer face.

## 7. Bag design
- **Panel layout (box bag, `huntkit.patterns.box_bag`)**: W 340 × H 788 × D 320 mm
  (13.4 × 31.0 × 12.6 in), bottom corners R 60.
  - Frame-side panel `B-01`: TPU 840D (meat/blood side).
  - Outer face `B-02`: 500D Cordura, solid color.
  - Left gusset `B-03`.
  - Right gusset split into fore/aft strips `B-04a/b` with a full-length #10 coil zip (835 mm).
  - Bottom `B-05`: TPU 840D, drain grommets.
- **Access**: a top opening with a 150 mm extension collar (drawcord) under the lid, plus the full
  right-side zip in a garage (`B-08`) so it can't be heard or snagged.
- **Lid**: 320 × 280 × 80 mm. The top is 210D with a camo shard, the underside is TPU 840D. It has
  an AquaGuard #8 front zip and an under-lid flat pocket (#5 zip). It's removable, holds the
  orange flag sleeve, and converts to a skull cap.
- **Pockets**: 2 bellowed side pockets (Ultragrid 210D, 3.99 L each) for a tripod, trekking poles
  or a bottle. 2 hipbelt pockets (0.97 L each, rangefinder-sized). Under-lid pocket 1.29 L.
  Hydration sleeve inside.
- **Compression**: 3 × 25 mm side straps per side, plus the load wings (38 mm) as the main
  load-spreading compression. They wrap the whole bag in carry mode and the meat in haul mode.

## 8. Universal-fit strategy (summary)
1. One frame. The crown sets torso and lifter geometry together, from 15 to 23 in, in 17 positive
   steps.
2. Four hipbelt sizes on one Ti pivot, plus a 3-position wing cant for hip shape.
3. Two strap shapes (J / S) on one yoke quick-connect, plus a long sternum rail.
4. Lower strap anchors on the frame, so belt swaps don't change the harness.
5. A fit card printed on the crown spine shows hole number ↔ torso inches, so the user measures
   once and sets it.
6. ASSUMPTION: one bag height works for everyone. For a 15 in torso the bag top stands ~220 mm
   above the crown top. The top straps and lid compression stabilise it. A short-bag variant is an
   open question (§12).

## 9. Quiet hunt mode
- No hook-and-loop anywhere (R-13). Fidlock magnetic buckles on the sternum strap and lid front
  instead of side-release clicks.
- Coil zips (quieter than molded teeth), Dyneema cord pulls with silicone sleeves, and garages
  that park the sliders.
- No film laminates (X-Pac/DCF) on exposed faces. 500D Cordura has quietness 3/5. A brushed-face
  option on the lid is open (§12).
- UHMWPE bushings on the crown legs mean no carbon-on-carbon squeak, and the POM latch closes with
  a soft detent.
- Elastic keepers on every webbing tail. Ladder locks have a soft-touch overmold (vendor option).
- Polyester webbing for the lifters and compression, because it doesn't sag or squeak when wet.

### Camo-accent panels as functional reinforcement
The accents are angular "shard" polygons (5 shapes, `SH-01..05`) in the owner's base camo. Each is
a **second layer sewn over the solid base panel** exactly where abrasion happens. The edgy look
comes from straight, angled seam lines that are also easy to sew: shards have no curves, so a
beginner can topstitch them.

| Shard | Where | Wear it takes |
|---|---|---|
| SH-01 (L+R) | Lower bag corners, wrapping 120 mm onto the gussets | Set-down on rock, deadfall drag |
| SH-02 | Upper outer-face diagonal | Antler, bow and rifle rub. Lash tabs sew through both layers, so it also reinforces the load points |
| SH-03 | Lid top | Thrown-down impacts |
| SH-04 (L+R) | Side-pocket faces | Brush |
| SH-05 (L+R) | Hipbelt wing tips | Arm swing, brush |

The shards add 99 g in total. NIR and colour matching between the printed polyester and the solid
nylon goes to the camo-designer.

## 12. Decisions, assumptions, open questions

**DECISIONS (pack-designer)**
- D-1 Telescoping crown torso adjust with a Ti index pin in a 17-hole rack (12.7 mm steps). It
  replaces "continuously adjustable" (spec.yaml) in favour of positive engagement.
- D-2 Lifter anchors ride on the crown at yoke + 150 mm, which gives a constant 40.6° lifter angle.
- D-3 Upper stays are parallel (230 mm c-c). All splay happens in the lower segment (270 mm c-c at
  the feet).
- D-4 A lumbar pm45 plate replaces the lumbar tube. Shelf straps anchor at the mid-bar node.
  These respond directly to the FEA demo failures.
- D-5 The main bag is a box bag, 340 × 788 × 320 mm, computed to 85.24 L. Lid, collar and pockets
  aren't counted.
- D-6 The full-length side zip goes on the right. Rifle carry goes on the left (hipbelt boot).
- D-7 Camo appears only as double-layer shard reinforcements (5 shapes).
- D-8 One bag size. Hipbelts are graded S–XL. J and S straps.

**ASSUMPTIONS** (marked in the text): yoke 50 mm below C7. Lifter strap point 140 mm forward of the
anchor. Iliac crest at z 120. Hipbelt padded length is 72 % of hip circumference. Frame mass
allocation is 525 g. Geometric volume matches filled volume within ±5 %. Benchmark ranges are
approximate. Blaze-orange colourway of robic 210D for the flag sleeve (no record yet). Slider and
grommet masses.

**Open questions for the owner**
1. Does the 0.5 in (12.7 mm) torso step work for you, or do you want the staggered 0.25 in rack
   (+10 g, a bit more machining)?
2. Rifle on the left and side zip on the right: are you right-handed and is that your carry
   preference? It's mirror-able.
3. One bag height means a 15 in user has the bag ~220 mm above the frame top. Accept that, or plan
   a 70 L short bag later on the same frame?
4. The load shelf group is 532 g. That's the price of meat-zone durability. Keep double-layer
   wings, or go single-layer (−75 g)?
5. Extension posts: standard in the box or an accessory?
6. A brushed-face (fleece-like, ultra-quiet) lid/pocket option for archery, at a cost of grime
