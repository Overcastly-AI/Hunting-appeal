Why each choice: the global run (section 3) puts the stay's walk beam stress at **72.6 MPa at z 330** (meat walk) and only **9.7 MPa at z 520**. At z 330 a metal clamp edge (Kt 2, fretting 0.6, design curve) fails whatever the preload, so the mid tee carries no moment and has no metal on the stay; the frame loses <= 2.5 % on any member row by it (bars 1.32 -> 1.29). At z 520 the beam stress is low, so a clamp works if its preload hoop stays moderate: the 100 mm shells roughly halve the hoop per newton of the 50 mm B81 shells, and the friction need is set by the side-hill torque about the stay (12.6 N m), not by the pawl. The end effect is gone with 15 mm of tube above the shells (solid FE: hoop per kN 23.4 at 15 mm vs 26.7 flush vs 23.3 continuous; couple 68.5 / 99.0 / 65.8).

## 6. Findings beyond review 9, and the design-curve sensitivity

- **F1 (BL-081 code, not edited):** `bl081_local.clamp_rows` and `bushing_rows` take the stay beam stress as Fy / sf, which is the stress at the case's LIMIT load, and then treat it as factored (walk = / 3 x 1.6). For the governing meat / tall cases that is 1.5 x low. It also uses the worst stay stress in the frame at the upper tee (91 MPa vs 20-89 MPa real at z 520, 9.7 MPa walk). BL-085 reads the stay forces at the tees from the global model (`bl085_global`) and corrects the bushing rows.
- **F2:** the BL-081 tee clamp bolts are M5 x 45 for a 44.5 mm grip (shell OD + two 1/4 backers): no thread for the nut. B85 uses M5 x 50 on a 41.3 mm grip.
- **F3:** Speedy lists no 1-1/4 x .125 tube; its 1-1/4 x .120 (p-4573) has a 1.010 in bore. B85 books that stock, bounds the unconformed fit by a crown-bearing (cos) jaw field in every row (SHELL-FIT), and adds the shell bending to close the 0.13 mm gap. The BL-081 SHELL-PRICE (USD 15) is replaced by the read price.
- **F4:** the BL-081 fatigue walk was the bag carry only; the meat walk (meat_straps x 1.6 / 2.0) gives 7 x higher stay stress at z 330 and is the mission load. B85 takes the envelope (ASSUMPTION WALK-ENV).
