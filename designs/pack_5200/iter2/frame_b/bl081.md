## 0. Answer

- **Strong: PASS, min margin 1.02** (stay wall under the upper tee: fatigue (preload mean + walking cycle)); fatigue minimum 1.02 on the BL-082 basis (Yahr mean curve, Goodman, Kf = Kt, no preload credit). Every row of section 8 is checked on real joints; nothing is rigid or idealised except where an ASSUMPTION says so.
- **It is not the BL-080 concept B any more.** The first-order B passed only because its joints were rigid and its printed tees carried everything for free. On the honest basis three of its joints could not be made to work, so B81 changes them (section 2): twin lumbar rungs (z 85 / 155) instead of one cross tube; a hip post of two angles tied to both rungs instead of a bracket bolted through one tube; 6061 half-shell clamps instead of printed clamp tees; 1 x .065 tube for stays, rungs and upper bar; crush sleeves in every through-bolt.
- **Mass 1991 g (4.39 lb) body**, +978 g against the BL-080 booking (1013.3 g), +928 g vs H1 and +573 g vs H1c (frame body + hip fixings).
- **Cost USD 176.28 per frame: OVER the USD 150 cap** (proving USD 66.64).
- Recommendation to the orchestrator: B81 is the first version of B that is strong on an honest basis, but it loses the rubric's mass axis to H1c and misses the cost cap; see section 7 for the levers and the decision.

## 4. Holes, bushings, clamps (items 2, 3, 5)

- Solid FE hole Kt, t1000_065 with D5.3 through-hole (gross): tension 3.35, bending with the hole on the extreme fibre 3.12, on the neutral axis 0.43, torsion (on tau) 5.38, pin bearing along / across the tube 1.28 / 1.78 (on P/dt).

## 7. Mass and cost: where B81 stands and the levers

- B81 is +586 g against H1c (1404.7 g) and +922 g against H1. The BL-080 1013 g relied on rigid printed joints; the metal that makes the joints honest (gussets, half-shells, 1/4 backers, post, sleeves, 36 bolts) is about 978 g.
- Lighter levers that keep the margins (not taken, each needs its own check): hose-band clamps on the half-shells instead of 1/4 backers and bolts (about -150 g, needs CL-1); .049 stays with an internal 7/8 x .049 doubler only at z 95-145 (about -60 g); Ti screws (-130 g, about +USD 90: over cap); a skeletonised foot cup (-50 g).
- Cost levers: pick up the metal at a local supplier (-USD 18 shipping); share the M5 50-pack over two frames; the cap itself is an orchestrator ASSUMPTION the owner may override.
- Decision for the orchestrator: (a) accept B81 as the strong-but-heavy metal reference and run the lighter levers as BL-083, or (b) go back to H1c / concept D (formed pan: the pan carries the hip torque as a plate, which is the load path B lacks).

## 10. Assumptions (each with the test that settles it)

- ASSUMPTION GAP-K: Huth fastener stiffness x 0.5 for the shim / fit gap. Settled by BB-1 (load-displacement).
- ASSUMPTION CLAMP-K: clamp tees as 2e4 N/mm and 2e8 N mm/rad links. Settled by OV-1 / CL-1 (stiffness), T1 deflection.
- ASSUMPTION GUSSET-J: gusset pair torsion as differential in-plane plate bending, GJ = 6 E I_p d^2 / L^2. Settled by HB-P.
- ASSUMPTION PLUG-K: saddle plug 3e4 N/mm in compression, no tension. Settled by PL-1.
- ASSUMPTION BRACKET-EQUIV: the post's front legs between the mount and the ties use the 2-angle section. Settled by HB-P.
- ASSUMPTION RADIAL-HOLE: holes modelled radial in the solid submodel (< 5 % edge-angle difference at d/D 0.21).
- ASSUMPTION CLAMP-UNIFORM: conforming 6061 half-shells give uniform pressure over +/- 86.5 deg (1.5 mm kerf gaps). OV-1, CL-1.
- ASSUMPTION EMBED: preload retention 0.9 in the all-metal clamp stack (no PAHT in the preload path). CL-1 wet after 24 h at 40 C.
- ASSUMPTION FIT: crown bushing bore = leg OD + 1.0 mm (0.5 mm radial), >= 0.3 mm must remain. OV-1.
- ASSUMPTION FRET-50: cyclic tangential load <= 0.5 x slip capacity gives no fretting. SC-1.
- ASSUMPTION CINCH-ANGLE: 30 % of the 150 N cinch acts along the stay. SC-1.
- ASSUMPTION PAD / DROP-MASS / COMP-Z: T4c drop estimate (1.6 kg rigid, 6 mm TPU pad, PAHT crush 2.4 x Z tension). CF-1, T4c.
- ASSUMPTION SHELL-PRICE, FLAT14-PRICE, SLEEVE-PRICE, SHIP-BASE: prices without a read listing. Quotes at order.
- ASSUMPTION H-PIN: the lumbar push H (1239 N C2, 661 N walk) on the hip pin (BL-055 bound) for the mount tipping rows.
- ASSUMPTION Hinge eye between the outer cup cheek and the stay wall, load spread over 18 mm (BL-041 eye). Fit-up trial.
- ASSUMPTION BL-082 (used as recorded): Yahr mean curve + Goodman (Su 290) R 0 S_max(1e5) 190.9 MPa (Goodman chosen over Gerber because the curve is a mean curve with no scatter factor; Gerber would give about +25 %), Kf = Kt, BJ-NOCREDIT, ANODISE-FAT (holes and clamp zones left bare: no knock-down applied), CRUSH-SLEEVE, wet friction 0.15, ring crush / hoop limits. KF-1 settles the fatigue basis because the fatigue minimum is under 1.2.
