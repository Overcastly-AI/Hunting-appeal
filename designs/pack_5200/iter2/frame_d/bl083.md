## 0. Answer

- **D83 is strong: min margin 1.02** (stay wall under the upper tee: fatigue (preload mean + walking cycle)); fatigue minimum 1.02. Same basis as BL-081 (Yahr / Goodman, Kf = Kt from tube_solid, no preload credit, wet mu 0.15, crush sleeves, PAHT in bearing / compression only).
- **But D does not beat H1c on mass, and no D variant meets the USD 150 cap.** D83 body **1850 g (4.08 lb)**, USD **262.06**; D83-L (hose-band tees, local metal; .049 stays were tried and fail the upper tee at 0.67) 1674 g, USD 242.54, strong PASS 1.02. H1c is 1404.7 g / USD 164.59; the 1300 g stretch goal is out of reach for any metal-stay variant checked here.
- **Why D is heavy and dear:** SendCutSend does not bend 6061-T6 (their page), so the formed pan is 5052-H32, whose minimum yield (159 MPa) is 0.66 x 6061's. On real joints a 2 mm pan was not enough: the Ti mount couple needs a formed spine channel, the face needs .100 in for the lumbar push, and the open tray let the frame sway (global buckling 0.99) until a strut on an M5 rod tied the walls at z 155. The pan set is 479 g and USD 126 at qty 1 (SCS price ASSUMPTION; band USD 90-126); the rest of the frame (stays, tees, bars, foot, printed parts) is B81's and already costs about USD 136.
- **Hip pivot offset:** the belt sets it (pin through belt B's centre plate, pad seated on the pan face). D keeps the BL-055 / 056 Ti mount and its 21 mm stand-off on a pan face at y -48.55, so the hipbelt interface is unchanged; the 67 mm to the stay line is carried as in-plane bending of the pan walls. Moving the pin aft (section 5) saves little pan and costs belt pad EVA: the offset is not worth changing.
- **B81 levers as briefed** (B81-L, comparison row): 1775 g (-216 g), USD 199.88 (+23.60), strong FAIL 0.70 (stay wall under the upper tee: fatigue (preload mean + walking cycle)).

## 3. What D83 is

- **Stays, bars, tees, foot, crown bushings: B81** (1 x .065 stays, 1 x .065 upper bar, 3/4 x .035 mid bar, 6061 half-shell clamp tees, printed foot cups). Only the lumbar / hip node changes.
- **Hip pan tray:** 5052-H32 .100 in (2.54 mm), SendCutSend laser-cut + 4 bends: face x +/-102.3 (OML) z 50-190 with its body side at y -48.55 (the BL-055 boss face: Ti mount, pin stand-off 21 mm, belt B pad seat 190 x 150 and the 25 mm top return for the Hypalon sleeve all unchanged), walls aft along the stays' inboard faces (z 78-190, beside the stay z 78-170 so the IM1 loop wraps the stay above), return flanges top 25 / bottom 20 mm. Flat 312 x 176 mm, hole-to-bend 7.1 mm (>= 6.0, half the 0.472 in die).
- **Mount spine:** 5052-H32 .100 channel, web 64 mm, legs 25 mm aft, z 50.5-189.5 behind the face, clamped by the 8 Ti-mount screws (all BL-055 positions now land) and 4 screws near the flanges. It carries the mount couple (V x 21 mm + pad offset) as beam bending to the flanges.
- **Pan bolts:** per side M5 A2 at z 85 along x through wall + stay (crush sleeve); at z 155 an M5 A2 threaded rod runs through both stays and a 3/4 x .035 spacer tube between the walls (the lumbar strut). The stay holes are lateral, on the neutral axis of the stays' fore-aft bending (solid-FE Kt on the neutral axis 0.43 vs 3.12 on the extreme fibre, BL-081).
- **Global stiffness:** torsion 16.1 N m/deg = 5.9 x H1 (B81 19.9). T1 crown elastic deflection at 150 lb: 15 in -2.2 mm, 19 in -3.6 mm, 23 in -5.6 mm (band +/-30 %).

Design path (each step forced by a failing row on the BL-081 basis):

| step | change | row it fixed |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5052 .080 tray, Ti mount on the face only | face strip at the mount: SF 0.99 at 1 g (mount couple into a 2 mm plate) |
| 2 | + spine channel (web 64, legs 25) | face strips at the mount; spine margin > 3 |
| 3 | .080 -> .100 tray | face out of plane under the lumbar pad push 0.89 -> 1.31; wall at the z 155 bolt 0.95 -> 1.02 |
| 4 | + strut on an M5 rod at z 155 | global buckling 0.99 -> 1.51 (side-hill): the open tray let the stays yaw / sway against the pan through wall out-of-plane bending |
| 5 | spine 50 -> 64 mm, spine screws z 62 / 178 | SendCutSend hole-to-bend rule (>= 1/2 die width) |

## 5. Hip pivot offset (free variable)

What the belt needs (`hipbelt_fit.md` 4.1, `hipbelt_structural.md` 4, `concepts/corrie.py pad_seat`): the Ti pin at x 0, z 120 through belt B's centre plate (roll +/-8 deg), a D36 POM thrust face for the lumbar push, and a pan face z 50-190, >= 190 mm wide, for the pad seat (EVA rails either side of a 70 mm channel, HDPE backer, 25 mm top return). The pin's fore-aft position is fixed by the body, not the frame: moving it aft by d moves the pad seat aft by d, so the pad stack must grow by d (or the whole frame and bag move d toward the back, which the spreader / back-panel clearance would have to absorb). D83 keeps d = 0.

(15 / 23 in torsos, global rows only.) Shortening the offset lowers the pan-bolt force and lifts the stays, but the shorter walls lose margin (the bolt couple then works on a squat wall whose aft strip at the z 155 bolt carries it in bending: 0.92 at d 15, 0.41 at d 30 in the grillage), the tray saves only 13 g per 15 mm while the belt pad gains 37 g, and the belt's fit moves. With the pan the 67 mm offset is carried in the walls' own plane, so the BL-055 / 056 mount and its offset stay (d = 0).
