Why these fixes (from the B86 global run):

- **The stays bend fore-aft, and B81 / B85 drilled the gusset bolts along y, so every stay hole sat on the extreme fibre.** The walk stress at the hole fibre is 54 MPa at z 135, 32 at z 105, 26 at z 95, 15 at z 78; above the lumbar node the stay runs a 70 MPa plateau from z 155 to z 380. An open hole (Kt 3.3-4.7) passes the design curve (R 0 S_max 114 MPa) only below about 30 MPa, so the holes move to z 78 / 95. The hinge pin z 60 is 18 mm (3.4 D) from the new z 78 hole, on a different axis.
- **The rung's central span is a constant-moment zone** (two tie forces at x +/-30): 23 N m fore-aft at walk, 36 MPa at the hole fibre, plus the tie's bearing patch. No hole position along the rung helps. The tie force is one-signed: the post pushes the upper rung aft and pulls the lower rung forward in every case (the only reversal is 33 N in the single-stay set-down). So the upper tie becomes a printed compression saddle with no hole, and the lower tie keeps its bolt, with the nut-side patch spread by a printed saddle washer on a snug bolt (no preload credited; a 6061 saddle washer was tried first and its edge failed the metal-fretting row at 0.94).
- **A row B81 / B85 never checked:** the printed rung-end plug bears on the stay at z 155, right at the top of the node, at 69 MPa walk. With the B81 20 mm head it is about 0.95 on the design curve with polymer contact. B86 flares the upper plug head to 50 mm (25 mm wide, between the gusset plates), and the IM1 collar moves up to z 181-189.
- **Front leg:** two 1 x 1/8 doubler strips under the Ti mount, credited only by bending-stiffness share (no composite action). The strips put the hip pin 3.2 mm further forward. The global model carries that, and the tipping rows are scaled by (21 + 3.2) / 21.
- **Leg-end bushing 70 -> 110 mm** (it grows up the leg, so the leg tip is unchanged; it now spans z 171-414 over the torso range, 61 mm clear of the stay-top bushing). That lowers the hoop at the 73 MPa stay section. The B85 stay holes at z 105 / 135 would have blocked a longer bushing, and the z 95 hole does not.
- **Bonded sleeve doublers were studied and rejected.** A 1-1/4 x .120 sleeve bonded on the stay (Volkersen shear lag, toughened epoxy G 700 MPa, 0.13 mm bondline) has a bond peak of 20 MPa at walk and 37 MPa factored at the sleeve end. That is above any cited epoxy fatigue strength. Through-hole doublers in the stay's bending peak still carry the hole.
