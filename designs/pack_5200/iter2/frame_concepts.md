Why B:
1. **Strong:** every metal row, buckling and joint row clears 1.0 on the HA-LS-1 rev C envelope (14 case sets x 3 torsos),
   and **no printed part is a bending member**: the tees, foot cups and pawl housing carry load in bearing or in a
   metal-backed clamp. A fails outright; C passes only on an idealised solid printed hub (see §3) and inherits the H1
   lower-half lug rows that BL-077 left open.
2. **Cost:** USD 116.86 a frame, inside the cap with USD 33 to spare; the only one of the light passing concepts inside it
   (D needs a laser-cut and bent pan: USD 160.42 on the ASSUMPTION PAN-PRICE).
3. **Mass:** 1013.3 g, 56 g under H1 and 391 g under H1c, level with D (0.9 g apart, inside the estimate band).
4. **Buildable by a beginner with hand tools:** one 72 in length of 1 in x .049 in tube, a 12 in mid bar, an 18 in angle
   and an 18 in flat; cut with a tubing cutter, drilled on printed jigs, no laser order, no bending, no bonding.
5. **Looks:** a clean ladder of round tubes and small printed nodes, the language of a bike or a premium external frame;
   it reads as intentional, not as an FE result (RUBRIC 3). D's pan would look better at the lumbar; the owner may prefer
   D at USD 160 (see §6).

## 1. Scope and basis

- **Comparison basis = the frame body** as BL-077 §8 books it (H1 1069.3 g = printed halves + US2 clevises; H1c 1404.7
  g): everything that replaces the printed body, i.e. stays, bars, hub, printed nodes and joint hardware, plus the
  growth of the carried-over crown bushings when the bore changes (booked: +41.3 g for a 22.9 mm bore). The Ti hip
  mount fixings are a separate row (BL-077 basis 28.8 / 48.2 g; B / D 41.8 g for 8 x M5 x 12 A2 and nuts).
- **Carried over unchanged:** crown kit (OD 16 carbon legs, top bar, spine, rack + pawl, 3 kits), Ti hip mount, spreader,
  CORRIE F rev 3 bag and attachment, hipbelt, harness. Cost scope as BL-077 ASSUMPTION COST-SCOPE.
- **Checks are first order** (`bl080_metal_frame` module docstring): the pack5200 beam model with each concept's
  sections and materials, all 14 HA-LS-1 rev C case sets at torsos 15 / 19 / 23 in; metal strength on the MMPDS
  S-basis, buckling at 1.5 x case factor, metal fatigue 1e5 x (0 -> 1.6 g) on Basquin with Kt 2 at clamps and Kt 3 at
  through-holes; printed parts on the BL-077 honest hot/wet B-basis. Joint forces come from the solved model. Detailed FE
  comes next (§8).

## 2. Concepts (sketch: `outputs/screens/20261125_bl080_1.png`)

- **A. Twin 7075 flat-bar stays + printed PAHT hub and crown sleeves.** Kifaru-style 1 in flat bar (sized to 1 x 1/4 in),
  printed lower hub carrying the Ti mount, crown legs in printed sleeves clamped beside the bars.
- **B. 6061 tube ladder + printed tees (pick).** Stays 1 in x .049 in 6061-T6 drawn tube on the H1 stay axis (x +/-115,
  straight z 0 -> 530), lumbar cross tube 1 in x .049 in at z 120, mid bar 3/4 x .035 in at z 330, upper bar 1 x .049 in
  at z 520 (pawl). Tees through-bolted (M5) below the crown-leg travel (z <= 160), metal-backed clamps above it. Ti hip
  mount on two hand-cut 1-1/2 x 1-1/2 x 1/8 in 6061 angle brackets through-bolted to the cross tube. Crown legs slide
  inside the stays on thicker UHMWPE bushings (bore 22.9 mm).
- **C. Hybrid.** The H1 lower half re-cut with through-bores for 7/8 x .049 in stays, upper half replaced by tube bars
  and printed tees (the brief's example c).
- **D. Own idea: tube stays + formed 6061 hip pan.** 7/8 x .049 in stays, a SendCutSend laser-cut and press-brake-formed
  .063 in hat pan with a .125 in doubler under the Ti mount (no holes within 25 mm of the bends, DECISION D-FLANGE), bolted
  through the stays at z 70 / 140.

Reading:
- **Flat stays (A) fail as stays and as crown guides.** A 1 in x 1/4 in 7075 bar reaches 253 MPa at 1.6 g in the C1 IM3
  couple (fatigue 0.77), and the crown-leg bushing load (1.34 kN factored) bends a printed sleeve ring to 0.32 in fatigue.
  A flat stay cannot carry a 190-205 mm aft CG at 150 lb without a bore for the crown; Kifaru's own note that flexible
  stays fail a real pack load agrees ([Kifaru](https://kifaru.net/en-ca/products/composite-stay-kits)).
- **C's hub passes only as an idealisation.** The grillage is a 10 mm solid PAHT plate across the whole lumbar (8 mm
  would do, 305 g); the real H1 lower half is a branched body whose lumbar struts and hip boss were among the
  over-utilised regions in BL-077, and it carries the US1 / hinge lugs and hip inserts that BL-077 left at 0.61-0.83 in
  fatigue (sleeved through-bolts are H1c's fix). C is not adopted on this evidence; it is the fallback if the owner wants
  to reuse the H1 lower print.
- **B and D are metal wherever a member bends.** B's two thinnest rows are the cross-tube and stay fatigue at the bolt
  holes (1.02 / 1.05) on a blanket Kt 3; the FEA must replace that with the real hole Kt (§8). Upper bar strength (1.06,
  pawl in the hipbelt-off abuse case) governs the bar size for every concept.
- The crown carry-over members stay at margin >= 1.12 on the new stays (info, `crown_info`).

## 8. Next structural brief (structural-fea-engineer)

Frame: concept B as sized in `frame_concepts.json` (`concepts.B`), built by `bl080_metal_frame.build_model` (do not edit
`pack5200.py`; add joint flexibility as links). HA-LS-1 rev C `ls1_envelope()`, torsos 15 / 19 / 23 in, both couple
variants. Check exactly:

1. **Global model with real joints:** tees as bolts (below z 160) and slip springs (above), hip bracket as two angle
   legs. Re-run every case: stay, bar, cross-tube, bracket and Ti-pivot margins; buckling eigenvalue >= 1.5 x case
   factor; T1 crown deflection at 150 lb for the +/-30 % test band; torsion stiffness vs H1.
2. **Lumbar cross tube and its bolts:** shell submodel of the 1 in x .049 in tube with the bracket bolt holes and the
   lumbar tee holes; real Kt; fatigue at 1e5 x (0 -> 1.6 g) with a cited notched 6061-T6 S-N (materials-engineer).
   First-order 1.02 / 1.05 today.
3. **Stays at the crown-leg bushings** (leg end z 171-374, stay top z 530): bushing contact on the tube wall, ring
   bending, fretting at the bushing ends (first-order fatigue 1.05-1.09 at z 377 in C / D).
4. **Pan-free load path:** lumbar pad and C5 meat face pressure into the spreader and stays; re-run `spreader.evaluate`
   with the round-tube clips at z 166 / 260 / 400.
5. **Upper tee clamps and upper bar:** clamp pressure vs tube ovalisation with the crown leg inside (radial clearance
   >= 0.3 mm at preload and at limit), slip at mu 0.2 wet with the factored bar-end forces (1.28 kN first order), the
   pawl loads 1001 N (C8) and 1334 N (tall-load compression), fatigue at the pawl-housing bolt holes.
6. **Hip bracket:** two 1/8 in angles carrying the Ti mount 45 mm forward of the cross tube; tipping load from BL-055 /
   BL-076; bolt bearing in the angle and the tube; fatigue.
7. **Foot cups:** hinge pin through the tube at z 60 (bag-on hinge 1019 N factored), US1 cradle 489 N, stabilizer
   anchor; C4 single-stay set-down and the T4c 122 cm drop on the cup; printed cup in compression only.
8. **IM1-IM3 and the 8 fairleads on metal:** every attachment case of `load_standard.md` §4.3 (float 559 / 1142 N, IM3
   hooks 1415 N); load into the tube by a pin (z <= 160) or a metal-backed clamp (above); printed parts in bearing on the
   PAHT Z hot/wet B-basis 19.66 MPa.
9. **Static-cinch cycles (R1 analogue):** 150 N per anchor x 1e5 on clamps and pins: fretting and preload loss.
10. Deliverables: pass / fail per row with margins, the coupons that settle each assumption (CL-1 clamp slip, BB-1 bolt
    bearing, bracket proof), and the parts-list deltas for the manufacturing-engineer.

## 10. Assumptions (each with the test that settles it)

- ASSUMPTION KT-HOLE: Kt 3 on the gross stress at a transverse hole in a tube in bending (Peterson, rounded up). Settled by the FEA hole submodel + BB-1.
- ASSUMPTION PRINT-MASS: printed node masses +/-30 %. Settled by node CAD + slicer.
- ASSUMPTION SOFT-PANEL: in B (no lumbar plate) the lumbar loads reach the stays through a 1 GPa back-sheet stand-in, kept out of the buckling solve. Settled by `spreader.evaluate` on B.
- ASSUMPTION BRACKET: B's hip bracket in the beam model is a 20 x 3 mm 6061 tube 45 mm long. Settled by the bracket submodel (§8.6).
- ASSUMPTION MU / CLAMP-PRELOAD: mu 0.2 wet Al/Al and 2.6 kN per M5 A2 on the clamp backers. Settled by CL-1 clamp-slip coupon (wet).
- ASSUMPTION BRG-ED: bearing yield 386 MPa (AA typical at e/D 2) x 0.9 at e/D 1.5. Settled by BB-1.
- ASSUMPTION BEAR-PAHT: printed parts in bearing on the PAHT Z hot/wet B-basis 19.66 MPa. Settled by the BL-079 G0 coupons.
- ASSUMPTION FLAT-PRICE, PAN-PRICE, SHIP, TOOL-PRICE: prices without a cited listing (7075 flat 1 x 1/4 in, SendCutSend pan, shipping, small tools). Settled by quotes at order.
- DECISION D-FLANGE (concept D only): no holes within 25 mm of the pan bends, so the flange strips take Kt 2.
- The H1 / H1c baselines are BL-077's numbers (body 1069.3 / 1404.7 g; USD 138.52 / 164.59).
