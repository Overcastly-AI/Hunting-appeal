- **Longer crown legs** (ticket idea: keep more leg in the stay): at the top setting of every kit the leg bottom only moves from z 374 to 247, still above the governing band; the extra leg stiffness draws load and the margin drops slightly. Rejected.
- **Bonded liner** (ASSUMPTION LINER): helps the z 160-240 band, nothing below it; +75 g for +0.10. A second bushing or a longer bushing seat changes only how the leg loads the bore above z 374, which is not in the governing population (0-2 %): not run separately for that reason. Rejected.
- **Moving the hot spot by where the bushing bears:** the bushing forces at 23 in static carry are 50-95 N (factored); the governing population is not under them. No lever.
- **Load-path growth:** H1's own grow-only fully-stressed offset (frame_comp_d.offset_step, 6 mm max-filter, cube-root growth, at most 3 mm per step, keep-outs and passive voids re-imposed, frame_h1.assemble) from H1's shift, driven by the per-voxel utilisation of every kit setting on fine_sm8 at target 1.15 x 1.02. One step (+38.8 g) gives 1.33; the step is then scaled (alpha) to find the least mass that passes. The lumbar p99 rises with it, so the gain is a stress reduction, not dilution of the percentile.

## 7. Found, not fixed: branch / mid-bar buckling on the sim loads

Team A's Euler rule (K 1, E x 0.6 hot x 0.7 creep, P_cr >= 1.5 x factored compression), evaluated on the same solves: H1 **0.43** on the sim loads (mid bar x -115..115, A 72 mm2, L 230, P_cr 125 N against 188 N factored, hipbelt-off abuse at 23 in meat; branch upper R 0.71, side-hill 23 in meat), **1.31** on the beam loads. BL-021 printed 0.43 and did not flag it. Hand sizing (P_cr ~ A^2): the mid bar needs A >= 72 / sqrt(0.44) = 108 mm2 (r_eq 5.9 mm, about +9 g) and the upper branches about 128 mm2 (+8 g a pair), before the stiffer bars draw more load. Not part of BL-024; it needs its own ticket and a check that the sim's lateral load on the stays (spreader contact net, IM strap pulls) is real.

## 8. Assumptions

- ASSUMPTION SIM_TORSO: A crown-kit setting between the simulated torsos (15 / 19 / 23 in) takes the bag forces of the nearest simulated torso (17.5, 18, 20, 20.5 -> 19 in); the other neighbour is run as a check and the worse of the two is reported. The crown (legs, bushings, top bar) is the kit's own at the setting's torso. Settled by: A CORRIE F fine meat run at 18 and 20.5 in (corrie_f_sim.py finerun F --torso 18 / 20.5).
- ASSUMPTION SIM_BODY: As BL-021 (im3_body_simloads ASSUMPTION SIM_BODY): the sim's 1 g frame-side forces scaled by g x load / 68 kg, closed on the payload weight at the sim CG. Settled by: T9 loaded CG + frame strain gauges at the stays and the lumbar struts in the T1 carry test.
- ASSUMPTION CINCH_STATIC: As BL-021: the meat-strap cinch is a static preload, in every strength and creep set but not in the static-carry set that gives fatigue; the cycled bound is reported beside it. Settled by: MT-1 strap tension readout over a 10 min loaded walk in the T2 meat test.
- ASSUMPTION PAHT_FAT: PAHT-CF fatigue_1e5_ratio 0.30 (record ASSUMPTION). Settled by: CA-1 coupon test (flip point in section 6).
- ASSUMPTION FI99: The body margin is H1's FI99 rule (99th percentile of the failure index over the non-load-patch voxels, 2 mm grid, frame_comp_d.evaluate). A change that adds material is credited only through the stresses it lowers; the governing region's own percentile is reported beside it so the body number is not raised by diluting the population. Settled by: T1 frame strain gauges at the lumbar struts / stay feet (z 100-200) against the voxel model's strain.
- ASSUMPTION LINER: Sleeve liner trial: a bonded tube in the bore is modelled as the bore ring r 9-11 mm with its Young's modulus scaled so the ring's bending stiffness EI equals the tube's (6061-T6 20 x 1 mm; voxel ring I from the 2 mm grid). Bond assumed perfect; the liner's own stress is not part of the body FI. Settled by: Only needed if a liner is adopted: a bonded-liner coupon (push-out) and a stay 3-point bend.
- ASSUMPTION LEG_ENGAGE: Longer-leg trial: each kit's legs are lengthened so that at the kit's lowest setting the leg bottom sits at H1's 15 in depth (z 171): +127 mm each (crown_diet deepest leg bottom z 298). Settled by: Only needed if adopted: crown legs FPF re-run (crown_diet.crown_margins) at the new length.
- ASSUMPTION PRINT_TIME: print time scales with printed mass from H1's 27 h. Settled by: slicer estimate of the new halves.
- ASSUMPTION MESH: the body grid is H1's 2 mm voxel (the FI99 rule and KEEP 1.02 are calibrated on it); no finer body grid was run. Settled by: a 1.0 mm sub-model of the lumbar struts (or T1 strain gauges).
