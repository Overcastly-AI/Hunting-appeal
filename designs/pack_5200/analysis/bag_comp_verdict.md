## 0. Verdict in short

- **Winner: A, CORRIE E (EVOLVE).** It is the only entry that passes the gate. It has the best access: 0 hourly misses
  on every loadout and belt, and the door is 294 to 334 mm clear at IM1 to IM3. Its 15 in meat CG margin is 6.4 mm with
  the bias. It costs +24.0 g over CORRIE B.2 and has the most build hours (38.3 h).
- **Runner-up: B, FELL (LEAN).** It has the highest weighted score under every weighting (7.67 with the RULES weights).
  It **fails the gate** on one hourly item: the 2nd bottle on the 8-day sheep load, at every belt. It also has the
  weakest access and durability.
- **Gate failures:** B, C, D and E are named in section 2 with the failing number. CORRIE B.2 itself would also fail
  the hourly gate.
- **Owner call that flips the result:** the owner could rule the sheep-load 2nd bottle a reserve item rather than an
  hourly one. Then B passes and wins on points. C still fails (volume, missing render) and E still fails (flat-bias CG).

Screens: `outputs/screens/20261110_BL002_1.png` is the contact sheet. `outputs/screens/20261110_BL002_2.png` has the

## 5. Verdict and best idea from each (task 5)

- **Winner: A, CORRIE E.** It is the only gate pass. Main risks to carry forward: hip-pocket reach is an ASSUMPTION
  (gate EV-3), elbow and rifle-butt clearance at the 320 mm base (EV-1), a full bottle loading the wing root (EV-4), and
  +2.7 h of build.
- **Runner-up: B, FELL.** Leader on points, gate fail on the sheep 2nd bottle.
- **Keep from each:**
  - A: the hip-reach wing pockets with the pole quiver (E-4/E-5). They fix snacks, GPS, bottle #2 and the poles on
    every belt.
  - B: seam hard points for G-hook pods that stay home; the single straight centre zip; the 3 % shallower shell (15 in
    meat 175.4 mm, the best margin).
  - C: the floor-hinged full-face lid with no sill. Also its sim finding that a box zip carries ≤ 0.45 N/mm (SF 79), so
    the zip is not the hoop-load path.
  - D: the SWAP slab, a sleep-kit module that becomes the wipeable meat liner under IM1–IM3.
  - E: panel line = camo break = door seam, with two-tone solid blocking and camo only on the outline. This is the
    strongest "mostly solid, camo accent" look.

## 6. Mass basis notes (ledger, not edited)

- The ledger moves strap-mounted pockets to `harness` for D (SP, 19.7 g) and E (IMP-6R, 17.0 g), but not for B's A4
  strap pod (28.0 g, `lean/fell.py:226-230`, right J-strap) or C's IMP-C1 (15.8 g, `C/tailgate.py:190-191`, which the
  code itself labels "harness part"). Applied the same way to all five, B would be 722.6 g and C 916.3 g. The ranking
  does not change. The scores use the ledger numbers as published.
- `ledger.teams()` `pack_M_h1_g` = H1 pack − CORRIE bag + entry bag (`ledger.py:443`). The strap pockets it moved to
  harness drop out of the pack total, so D and E pack masses are 19.7 and 17.0 g low.
