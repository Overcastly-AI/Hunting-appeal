## Summary

- Production pack before this plan: **3462 g (7.63 lb)**. After the applied item: **3441 g (7.59 lb)**. Target 2268 g (5.0 lb). Gap **1173 g**.
- The bag side (bag body, fly, pockets, attachment, under-straps, meat harness, wraps) is **1307 g**. The other 2134 g is the printed frame, spreader, back panel, hipbelt and harness.
- Applied (no owner choice): W-01, **-22 g**.
- Owner choices I recommend: O-4, O-3, O-6, O-5, O-15, **-54 g** more. With them the pack is about **3386 g (7.47 lb)**.
- Every bag-side item on the list together, recommended or not, is **-465 g**. **The bag cannot close the gap.** The 5.0 lb target was set on the 569 g tube frame; the printed branch frame plus spreader is 1225 g. Closing it needs a frame-side decision or a new target (owner choice OC-0 below).

Cost is the material delta per pack (record cost per yard over the record roll width; ASSUMPTION 1.5 m roll when a record has none). Thread follows the 3 % rule of the budget.

## Owner choices

**OC-0. The target.** Bag-side cuts are worth about 76 g with no loss of function. The rest of the gap (~1118 g) is in the printed frame and back system, which the owner chose over the lighter tube frame (DECISIONS 2026-11-01). **Recommendation:** stop chasing 5.0 lb in the bag. Publish the honest number for the printed frame (about 3386 g, 7.47 lb with the recommended cuts), keep 5.0 lb as the target of a later lighter-frame variant, and if 5.0 lb must hold now, order a frame-side mass study from the structural-fea-engineer. More bag cuts would cost meat-haul function, quiet or durability.

**O-2. Meat straps 38 mm -> 25 mm (webbing, cams, hooks)** (-84.8 g, -0.92 $/pack). Trade: 25 mm bites game bags and the 25 mm cam/buckle ratings were already too low for the cradle (meat_haul_modes.md); breaks the one-width MEAT family (R-25). **Recommendation: NO. Load rating and glove use beat 80 g.** Patterns if accepted: none.

**O-9. Delete the fly: waterproof body (ULTRA 200 face + gores, taped seams)** (-82.2 g, +23.94 $/pack). Trade: ULTRA's film backing is louder (quietness 2, R-13 quiet requirement), UHMWPE takes camo print poorly (record ir_notes), taped seams in a 3D teardrop are hard for a first-time builder. **Recommendation: NO. Quiet hunting and the owner's camo direction win; keep the fly.** Patterns if accepted: all face/gore pieces and the fly set.

**O-1. Meat straps 3 -> 2 (drop IM1, keep its fairlead eyes)** (-72.1 g, -12.97 $/pack). Trade: bag-off quarter loses its lowest clamp: friction hold drops from 5 to 4 straps and the hock can no longer hook over IM1; bag-on loads move to IM2/IM3 (IM3 hook already the heaviest, SF 2.71). **Recommendation: NO. Packing meat out is the primary mission; IM1 is the low clamp in bag-off. Keep 3.** Patterns if accepted: B-01: delete the 2 IM1 ports (B-12 x2).

**O-11. Delete both floating side pockets** (-62.1 g, -5.64 $/pack). Trade: loses tripod / rifle-stock / bottle carry and 5.8 L; wraps alone carry long items. **Recommendation: NO. Tripod and stock carry are requirements R-18..22.** Patterns if accepted: delete P-01..P-03.

**O-16. Delete haul wrap W3 (crown wrap)** (-38.9 g, -2.00 $/pack). Trade: the roll-top clips still compress the top; frame-only loses the top lash to the crown and antler carry. **Recommendation: NO. Antler/cape lash is a field use case; keep W3.** Patterns if accepted: none.

**O-4. Body face, side gores, hem, storm strip 330D Cordura -> 210D UHMWPE grid** (-17.9 g, +3.70 $/pack). Trade: tear 90 -> 180 N (2x); abrasion of the grid fabric is not in its record; visible grid lines change the solid-colour look; same quietness (3). **Recommendation: YES if a supplier abrasion figure is >= the 330D (800 Taber cycles) and the owner likes the swatch.** Patterns if accepted: B-02a/b/c/d, B-03, B-07, B-08 material id; re-nest markers.

**O-3. Roll-top collar B-06 100D Robic -> 70D ripstop (40D silpoly shown for scale)** (-12.8 g, +0.94 $/pack, 40D silpoly: -28.8 g). Trade: tear 45 -> 35 N (40D: 25 N). The collar carries an over-stuffed load and the roll; silpoly is too slick to hold a roll. **Recommendation: YES to 70D once the materials-engineer adds a record with a real tear value; NO to 40D silpoly.** Patterns if accepted: B-06 material id only (marker re-nest at the 70D roll width).

**O-8. Fly integration: stow the fly in the SPINE band, delete fly pocket F-02 + #5 zip** (-11.0 g, -1.19 $/pack). Trade: the fly no longer deploys from the bottom without unpacking; the band then holds fly + bow/poles. **Recommendation: NO. The base-pocket fly is a researched answer to P7.** Patterns if accepted: delete F-02; D-01 unchanged.

**O-6. Camo shards SH-02..04 on a 300D printable base (SH-01 corner stays 630D)** (-9.7 g, -0.09 $/pack). Trade: SH-02/03/04 are trim on low-wear spots; SH-01 wraps the bottom corner and stays the abrasion doubler (DECISIONS: accents as functional reinforcement). **Recommendation: YES, after a print test on the 300D base (camo-designer colour match).** Patterns if accepted: SH-02, SH-03, SH-04 material id.

**O-7. Rain fly F-01 40D -> 20D silpoly** (-8.6 g, +1.89 $/pack). Trade: tear 25 -> 15 N; the fly is also the brush cover in rain. **Recommendation: NO. 10 g does not pay for a fly that snags open on the first deadfall.** Patterns if accepted: F-01a/b/c material id.

**O-10. Side-pocket gussets P-02 210D grid -> 100D Robic** (-8.5 g, -0.50 $/pack). Trade: tear 180 -> 45 N on the brush-facing gusset. **Recommendation: NO. This is the pain point we were built to fix.** Patterns if accepted: P-02 material id.

**O-5. Bag bottom B-05 500D Cordura -> Challenge ULTRA 200 (B-01 fabric)** (-7.6 g, +1.56 $/pack). Trade: better: Taber 1500 -> 2000, tear 130 -> 250 N, waterproof floor (blood/wet ground); louder (quietness 2) but the floor is not brushed; stock colours only. **Recommendation: YES. A durability gain that also saves grams; pick the closest stock ULTRA colour (black/coyote).** Patterns if accepted: B-05 material id (and B-16 floor channels stay 500D).

**O-12. Hydration: replace the sleeve B-09 with 2 hanger loops** (-7.6 g, -1.40 $/pack). Trade: the bladder then sits against the meat stack: blood on the bladder and no separation. **Recommendation: NO. Meat rides in this bag; keep the sleeve.** Patterns if accepted: delete B-09.

**O-13. Diagonal zip #10 -> #8 coil (2 sliders kept)** (-6.8 g, -0.57 $/pack). Trade: #8 under a meat load and gloved hands; the leaders use #10 on the main zip (SG, research 3.1). **Recommendation: NO. The main zip is the last place to save 7 g.** Patterns if accepted: none (tape width differs 1 mm: B-08 notes).

**O-15. Lash tabs B-11 Hypalon -> laser-cut TPU-coated 840D** (-6.4 g, -0.14 $/pack). Trade: lighter and stiffer; laser fuses the cut edge; pull-test the 25 x 5 slot to the 450 N buckle class first. **Recommendation: YES, pending a 5-sample slot pull test.** Patterns if accepted: B-11 material id.

**O-14. Diagonal zip: one slider instead of two** (-6.0 g, -1.00 $/pack). Trade: lose the open-from-either-end access that makes one zip ambidextrous. **Recommendation: NO. Ambidextrous access is a decision.** Patterns if accepted: none.
