## 6. Assumptions and decisions that govern

- ASSUMPTION: parts outside a team's metric (haul wraps for A/B/C/E, B-11 tabs, bow riser carry, keeper loops, roll-top buckles for A/B/C/E) are unchanged in its entry, as each write-up states. Settled by BL-002 re-booking each entry part by part in the ledger.
- ASSUMPTION (inherited): thread + labels ~3 % of soft goods (geometry.yaml). Settled by weighing the first prototype's thread cones before and after.
- DECISION: IMP-6 (and any strap pocket) is `harness`: it is sewn to the J-strap in B.2 and stays with the harness when the bag comes off.
- DECISION: wings and wing straps, roll-top buckles and pouch straps are `straps_compression` (ticket), so the bag system books them; the fairlead swells B-13x are `attachment`.
- DECISION: C-9 is not applied on H1 (0 g row): it is a ligament offset of today's body raster; H1 replaces that body. A C-9-style offset on H1 needs its own FEA (frame_h1.md section 6 lists CA-1 as the next H1 cut).
- `base` is True on every row: CORRIE B.2 books every part in base weight. BL-008 proposes base vs add-on.
