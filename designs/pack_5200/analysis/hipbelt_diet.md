## 3. Item notes

### 3a. Front closure

- **a1, 38 mm buckle: REJECT.** `buckle_side_release_38mm` breaks at 900 N (estimate record). Rev C C2 puts T = 1000 N through the buckle (`hipbelt_structure.load_sets`), so SF/req 0.90; the 50 mm buckle is 1.50. It would save 17 g. Only a 38 mm buckle with a tested break of at least 1000 N could come back (materials-engineer).
- **a2, book the closure as belt B builds it.** Routing (hipbelt_structural.md section 5): anchor on the buckle half, round the D-ring on the wing tip, back through the heavy ladder lock on the buckle half, then the tail. So the only 50 mm webbing is two buckle tabs. Webbing actually needed per size (clothing +40 mm on the circumference, lock 62 mm from the front centre, anchor 60 mm, D-ring tab 110 mm, tail >= 150 mm at the largest waist):

| Size | Leg at max / min waist mm | Tail at min waist mm | 25 mm needed m | 50 mm needed m | Booked g | Needed g | Delta g |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| S | 120 / 69 | 252 | 1.21 | 0.28 | 45.0 | 32.4 | -12.6 |
| M | 118 / 67 | 252 | 1.20 | 0.28 | 45.0 | 32.3 | -12.7 |
| L | 121 / 70 | 252 | 1.21 | 0.28 | 45.0 | 32.5 | -12.5 |
| XL | 125 / 74 | 252 | 1.23 | 0.28 | 45.0 | 32.8 | -12.2 |
| XXL | 138 / 74 | 277 | 1.28 | 0.28 | 45.0 | 33.7 | -11.3 |

  The booked 0.6 m of 25 mm is about half of what the 2:1 needs (1.20 m at M), so this row also corrects an under-booking. Net it still saves, because 0.9 m of 50 mm strap goes.
- **Comfort of a narrower front strap.** Belt B's front is the 2:1's two 25 mm legs side by side (50 mm), not a single strap. Line pressure T_req x curvature / width at the sharpest curvature between the wing tip and the front centre: 50 mm 79 / 103 kPa (median / worst), 38 mm 104 / 135, one 25 mm width (legs stacked) 158 / 206. A narrower front strap raises it in proportion (38 mm: x1.32). Build rule kept: **the two legs run side by side, never stacked**. The figure is a watch item only: the fit model has no front strap, the abdomen is soft and webbing bridges the sharpest curvature; HB-1 should put a sensor just in front of the wing tip.

### 3b. Stabilizer straps

- **b, delete both (22.4 g).** With belt B the wing is stitched to the plate (fixed joint, the cone is in the pattern), the plate is held on the Ti pin in x, z and pitch, the lumbar pad bears on the frame, and the only intended freedom is roll +/-8 deg on the pin. A stabilizer pulling the wing root toward the lower stay (z 60) about 100 mm off the pin can only resist that roll, which is the motion the pivot is there to allow. No rev C case, no frame model spring and no fit-model term uses the stabilizers, so the pressure and hold numbers do not move. What it can lose: damping of belt roll on a side-hill. Gate **HB-D1**. The printed anchor pads on the frame stay, so the straps can be refitted without a frame change.

### 3c. Wing stiffener

- Booked HDPE 41.0 g; the developed outline gives 39.0 g (belt_bom ignores the 15 mm soft-tip gap; deltas here use the developed figure on both sides). The model is insensitive to thickness (hoop stiffness is a small share against the foam), as `hipbelt_fit.md` section 3.2 found. The fit model cannot see wing roll-over, which goes as t^3: 0.75 mm keeps 42 % of the bending stiffness, 0.5 mm 12.5 %, the perforated sheet an estimated ~49 % (ASSUMPTION) at more labour.
- **c1 (shorter)** moves the HDPE end 20 mm further behind the ASIS (the padded tip, which sets the ASIS result, does not move) but shrinks the pocket room on the stiffened zone by 20 mm (IMP-5 is already short at M). Not taken.
- **c2 0.75 mm taken**, gated by **HB-D2** (wing roll-over).

### 3d. Foam

- Code note: `hipbelt_concepts.B` inherits C0's wing stack EVA 45C 6 + EVA 30C 5; `hipbelt_fit.md` section 4.1 says 45C 5 + 30C 6. The ledger books the code (load foam 30.1 g), so the variants start from the code.
- d5 models the softer wedge as a taper loss: x0.843 (records: EVA 45C secant 480 kPa, XLPE L200 192 kPa, at belt B's worst lumbar peak). ASSUMPTION; HB-1 settles it.
- XLPE L200 (Minicel class) has a lower compression set (12 %) than EVA 45C (15 %) in the records, so creep is not the objection; stiffness is. d2 and d4 pass and even lower the peaks (a softer layer spreads the load), d5 fails on hold (the wedge loses its taper), d1 and d3 pass but save little.
- **No foam change is in the package (DECISION).** The package meets the stretch without them, and a 2.5x softer load layer changes how firm the belt feels under 68 kg, which the linear Winkler model cannot judge (strains near 30 % at the peaks, past the record's 25 % secant point). d2 + d4 are the reserve: build one prototype wing pair in XLPE for HB-1 if b or c2 fails its test.

### 3e. Bottle holster

- Holster 38.8 g = add-on 36.6 g (tube, base, back, straps, G-hooks, cinch) + 2.2 g of wing tabs bartacked to the shell, which stay in base. It is outside the fit model (hipbelt_fit.md section 7: under 2 % of the hip load). Moves base, not the as-hunted mass.

### 3f. Pad seat

- Pad seat today: rails 10.2, backer (CFRP 0.8 after C-4) 35.3, slip liner 4.7, sleeve 7.6, bottom strap 11.2 g. The backer, rails and liner set the channel depth play and the C-4 margins (load path in effect): untouched. The liner also keeps the CFRP backer off the aluminium spine (galvanic couple), a second reason to keep it.
- **f1** bottom retention: 25 mm strap + ladder lock 11.2 g -> 2 mm Dyneema cord 0.45 m + spring cord lock 3.6 g. The pad is pressed onto the pan by the lumbar push in every carry case; the bottom tie only keeps it in place with the belt off. **f2** top sleeve Hypalon (1000 g/m2) -> 1000D Cordura (340 g/m2, already in the belt BOM), 2.6 g; hot-cut edges. Gate **HB-D3**.

## 6. Assumptions and the tests that settle them

- ASSUMPTION closure geometry: clothing +40 mm, lock station 62 mm, anchor 60 mm, ring wrap 15, lock wrap 30, buckle tab 140 mm each. Settled by cutting the first prototype's webbing to these lengths and fitting it on the smallest and largest tester of each size (cut list check, no new test ID).
- ASSUMPTION stabilizers carry no needed function with the fixed joint and pivot. **HB-D1:** 68 kg sandbag, 20 min side-hill traverse (about 25 deg) and a 10 min step-down set, stabilizers cinched vs fully slack, same tester, belt tension set to T_req with the luggage scale. Pass: belt creep within HB-3's 15 mm both ways and no tester-reported roll or sway difference. Fail: refit the straps (+22 g).
- ASSUMPTION a 0.75 mm HDPE keeps the wing from rolling over at the crest. **HB-D2:** one wing with 0.75 mm, one with 1.0 mm on the same belt, 68 kg, 30 min hike; mark the crest line on both wings and photograph the top edge at the crest every 10 min. Pass: top-edge fold under 10 mm and creep within HB-3, equal to the 1.0 mm side; plus HB-1 pressure within 10 % of the 1.0 mm side. Fail: back to 1.0 mm.
- ASSUMPTION perforated-sheet stiffness (1 - phi)^2, phi 0.3: only used for c4 (not taken).
- ASSUMPTION a softer wedge loses taper by its extra compression (d5). HB-1 settles it if d5 is ever taken.
