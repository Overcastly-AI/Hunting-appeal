## 6. Assumptions

- ASSUMPTION STAY_LOCAL: The stay is vertical at z 520, so the sim's global (x, y, z) force is the fairlead's local (pin, aft, up) force, as bag_attach / float_fea / loadpath_check use it. Settled by: H1 STL: stay tangent at z 520 within 5 deg of vertical (read off the print file before R1).
- ASSUMPTION COMBINE: Worst rev C combination on one eye: the sim bag-hook vector and the rev C C5 IM3 meat-strap vector both at full value and added as vectors. This counts the meat weight twice (the sim hangs all of it on the bag, rev C puts 30 % of it and the overturning on the straps): an upper bound. Settled by: IM3 eye pull with both carabiners in the throat, the strap pulled aft and the hook pulled down at the same time (first frame).
- ASSUMPTION LATERAL: The lateral (pin-axis) part of the hook pull bears on the inner face of one cheek, spread over the face within one hole diameter of the hole centre. Settled by: Same eye pull as COMBINE, with the hook 10 deg off the stay plane.
- ASSUMPTION DIR_SCATTER: The combined hook + strap pull on the eye may sit up to 10 deg further aft than computed (the 18 sim hook vectors spread 2-8 deg aft; the strap angle at the eye is rev C's, not measured). The cheek must keep fatigue >= 1.15 at the computed angle + 10 deg. Settled by: IM3 eye pull test with the strap led aft at the measured hunting angle (first frame), or a protractor reading of the strap and hook angles in the T2 meat test.
- ASSUMPTION PAHT_FAT: PAHT CF fatigue_1e5_ratio 0.30 (record ASSUMPTION, no published data). Settled by: CA-1 coupon test (TEST_PLAN): the flip points in section 5 say what each part needs.
- ASSUMPTION SIM_BODY: Bag cases on the body: the sim's 1 g frame-side forces scaled by g x load / 68 kg; side-hill keeps the beam's lateral and downhill part, the upright part is replaced by the sim x cos 25 deg. The set is closed on the payload weight at the sim's CG: the force left over (under-straps, W straps, doublers and fabric the extraction does not map: 67-96 N, 10-14 % of the weight) goes to the four under-strap pins, and the sagittal moment left over to a fore-aft couple between the IM3 hook and hinge nodes (-38 to +201 N). Settled by: T9 loaded CG + frame strain gauges at the stays z 450-550 in the T1 carry test.
- ASSUMPTION CINCH_STATIC: The meat straps' cinch (rev C 150 N per strap, the sim's meat-face push) is a static preload set at the kill site: it is in every strength and creep set but does not cycle with the walking g, so it is left out of the static-carry set that gives fatigue. The bound with the cinch cycling is reported beside it. Settled by: MT-1 strap tension readout over a 10 min loaded walk in the T2 meat test (amplitude of the strap tension against its mean).
- ASSUMPTION SETDOWN_BEAM: The single-stay set-down keeps the beam's lumped bag masses (inertia relief through one foot); it does not enter fatigue. Settled by: T5 drop / set-down test.
- ASSUMPTION STRAP_REACT: Meat case in the sim: the meat-face push is reacted at the six IM anchors: the overturning share per level and 30 % of the meat weight as in rev C, and the cinch loop (the spreader push it causes) by strap pulls at the three levels that balance that push in force AND moment (the meat bundle is in equilibrium), as near an equal split as that allows. The sim's push sits low (z 220-230), so the cinch goes almost all to IM1 (z 190). Settled by: MT-1 strap tension readout in the T2 meat test.
