## 8. Assumptions

- ASSUMPTION PAWL-ZONE: The pawl's swing zone is 9.3 mm deep fore of the rack's tooth tips, the room it had in the printed channel (18.3 = 3 plate + 6 rack + 9.3); the pawl pivots on a 4 mm pin through the side walls. Settled by: Pawl dry fit on the first print: pawl fully lifted (2 mm tooth depth + 0.5) clears the fore wall.
- ASSUMPTION RUN-CLEAR: 1.0 mm running clearance on the tube (radial) and each side of the rack: FDM hole tolerance 0.3 + tube OD tolerance 0.1 + 0.6 for grit and ice. Settled by: Slide the bonded spine + rack through the printed housing by hand over the full travel, dry and with grit, before bonding the clamp.
- ASSUMPTION RACK-SADDLE: The flat rack sits on the tube's fore face on a filled-adhesive saddle (MMA with glass beads), so the moving envelope is the tube plus a 20 x 16 mm block from the tube axis to the tooth tips. Settled by: Bond trial on a spine offcut; the rack's base must seat flat.
- ASSUMPTION YOKE-PATH: The housing's fore face stays at today's y -38.8 (at the pawl line): the yoke on the spine passes it at S 16.8-18 in and M 17.5-18.3 in (geometry.yaml yoke anchors y -38.5..-42.3). Settled by: Crown dry fit at kit S 18 in: the yoke slides past the housing.
- ASSUMPTION GUIDE-B: Moving pulley guide B hangs from the tube's lower end, centred on the tube axis, its 39 mm across x; at each kit's top setting it enters the housing from below (tube end 15.8 mm below the pawl line). Settled by: Crown dry fit at each kit's top setting.
- ASSUMPTION TOP-BAR-AFT: The crown top bar runs 25 mm aft of the legs' plane at x 0 (geometry.yaml top_bar_aft_offset_mm, corner dog-legs from x 103 to the leg); the beam model's top bar lies in the legs' plane. real_crown moves the top bar and models the dog-legs as straight members from the leg top to x 95 (the existing split node). Settled by: T6 coupon of the clamp fitting on the real offset; crown dry fit.
- ASSUMPTION CLAMP-OFFSET: The clamp's T-fitting spans the 25 mm from the top-bar axis to the spine axis as a solid machined block (saddle, socket and two webs); modelled as a 25 mm 7075 beam with the webs' section. Settled by: T6 coupon of the fitting.
