## 0. Summary

- Booked today (belt M): **4000.4 g (8.82 lb)**. Target **2994 g (6.6 lb)**, gap **1006 g**. The DECISIONS row of 2026-11-05 published 2976 g; `corrie.TARGET_G` (2994 g) is used here.
- Booking gaps found: **+43 g** of real mass no table books (body binding, CORRIE-sized fly). Real estimate **4043 g**.
- Package **A** (no-regret): **-10 g** real (-10 g booked), -3.21 $/pack, +0.0 beginner h -> **4033 g (8.89 lb)**, +1039 g vs target.
- Package **B** (owner OK): **-153 g** real (-116 g booked), -2.87 $/pack, -6.0 beginner h -> **3880 g (8.55 lb)**, +886 g vs target.
- Package **C** (needs FEA / tests): **-101 g** real (-101 g booked), +23.52 $/pack, +0.0 beginner h -> **3779 g (8.33 lb)**, +785 g vs target.
- **The 6.6 lb target is not reachable with CORRIE's features on the printed frame.** After A+B+C the pack is about **3779 g (8.33 lb)**. Even with every 'what it would take' item (lighter frame, pouch as accessory, no scope sleeve, 2 meat straps) it is about **3100 g**. To hit 2994 g with A+B+C and all features, frame + spreader would have to weigh **440 g** (today 1225 g).
- Recommendation: adopt A now, take B item by item, send C to the structural-fea-engineer, and **re-baseline the published weight for CORRIE on the printed frame to about 3800 g (8.4 lb)**. Keep 6.6 lb (and 5.0 lb) as targets for a lighter-frame variant (section 6).
