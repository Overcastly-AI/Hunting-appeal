The B-24 links (54.4 g) stay as built in the meat-trip kit, where the owner decision already put them; that move is not counted as a saving here.

## 2. Research summary (detail and URLs in `docs/research/strap_systems.md`)

- **Stone Glacier:** meat rides on an integrated load shelf in the frame, between frame and bag; the frame is connected to the bag at 12 points by 1 in (25 mm) webbing with Duraflex hardware. Pack-out: separate the bag, load, tighten the shelf, re-attach the bag bottom-up.
- **Exo:** 4 pairs of straps on the frame: 2 pairs secure the load, 2 pairs connect the bag; with the bag off all 4 pairs hold the load. One strap set, two jobs: the closest model to REC's frame-anchored W1-W3.
- **Seek Outside:** 8 removable, movable compression straps + 1 over-the-top, joinable for long loads; the same straps do compression and odd-load lashing.
- **Mystery Ranch:** Overload sling between frame and bag (a fabric shelf; we may not use one, patent guardrail), bag folds over the meat and its own compression straps cinch the quarters.
- **Kifaru:** ARK frame with Loop Lock attachment points for strapping quarters; no strap specs published (secondary).
- **Rated light hardware:** AustriAlpin 25 mm Al G-hook 8 g / 1.2 kN break / 0.9 kN no-set; 38 mm 16 g / 1.5 kN; Austere 25 mm Al cam 12.2 g (no rating); a 25 mm alloy cam at 450 kg break (secondary); ITW ladder locks 687-981 N tensile, no maker publishes slip. No leader publishes a strap or hook rating.

Takeaway: the leaders use **one 25 mm strap set** for compression and meat, mostly frame-anchored (SG, Exo). Nobody runs 38 mm webbing for meat straps, and nobody carries side wings and wrap straps together.

### (a) lighten in place

IM1-IM3: 38 -> 25 mm webbing (same cuts), 25 mm Al cam (ASSUMPTION 13 g, slip 1100 N), left end a sewn loop girth-hitched on the left fairlead eye, so 3 rated 25 mm G-hooks (8 g, 1200 N) instead of 6 sliding-gate hooks. US1/US2: 25 mm webbing, the same 25 mm cam. B-24: 25 mm webbing and the 25 mm rated G-hook. Saves 137.7 g; short of 200 g on its own. The B-24 hook swap puts the 941 N misuse load above the hook's 900 N no-set load, so REC does not take it (the B-24 hook stays the 2200 N bag-hook part, which is also the repair-kit spare).

### (b1) three straps at the IM heights, wings and W1-W3 deleted (as the ticket worded it)

S1-S3 at z 190/330/520, cut 1351, 1410, 1516 mm (haul path interpolated + 150 mm tail). It saves 103.2 g, but **it cannot do F3**. To close bag and meat to the frame, each strap needs a frame anchor at both ends. At the IM heights the only frame points are the IM eyes. In float the IM3 eye already carries the IM3 meat-strap hook and the B-24 link hook side by side in its 18 mm throat (`float_fea.md` assumptions), so a third hook does not fit. Bag-anchored straps, on the other hand, pull the bag's own seams, not the stack. The W anchors (z 80/250/420 lash points and the crown bar) are the only free frame anchors, and adding new ones means a new printed feature, which is out of bounds. Also, with no strap above z 520 the float bag's top tips aft: its CG (about z 550, 129 mm aft of its face) sits above and behind the link. W3 at the crown holds it today. **Rejected.**

### (b2) W1-W3 as the one external set, wings deleted

W1-W3 are already frame-anchored and sized for the float path. They take over carry compression and side carry from the wings (team B's FELL did this). Deleted: M-01 x2, SH-06 x2, 4 wing straps, 101.6 g. The heights are W's (mean z 165 / 335 / 703), not the IM heights: W2 is at IM2, W1 is 25 mm below IM1, and W3 stays at the crown for the float-bag top, the C6 tall-load lash (>= 25 mm on the crown bar) and the F2 bag stash. Cost: W1/W2 cross the door in carry, so a door opening adds 2 hook-offs and 2 hook-ons (16 s); the roll-top route adds nothing. IMP-1 (scope sleeve, inside the left wing today) re-anchors on the left gusset B-03L, as team C did; IMP-8 (bow cam cup) is already sewn in the corner seam below the wing and stays.

### (c) meat-trip kit

The only parts used in F3 and nowhere else are the B-24 links (54.4 g). The owner decision of 2026-11-10 already books them in the meat-trip configuration, so (c) is today's booking and saves nothing further. Extension (c+), the cradle US1/US2 straps to the kit as well, is rejected: the cradle is not F2/F3-only (in F1 it cinches the floor and takes 55 % of the floor vertical, `meat_haul.CRADLE_SHARE`), and F2 is the day-of-kill mode, so a cradle left in camp fails the required mode when it matters.

## 5. Procedures and seconds (gloves)

Unit times from `attach_system.PROCEDURES` (the method behind `meat_haul.DETACH_S`): one hook = 4 s (bag-off step 3, 4 hooks in 16 s); one two-hook IM strap with its cam = 8 s (float step 3), so a one-hook IM strap = 4 s. ASSUMPTION: a strap end through a port 3 s, kit out 5 s.

| procedure | today, as documented | today, IM straps counted | REC |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| F1 meat into the bag (strap steps) | - | 24 s | 12 s (door route 28.0 s) |
| bag off (limit 120 s) | 52 s | 76 s | 85 s |
| bag on | 59 s | 83 s | 92 s |
| F2 bag-off haul | 108 s | 132 s | 129 s |
| F3 float | 128 s | 152 s | 154 s |

Today's documented bag-off (52 s) never unhooks the IM straps, yet they hook to the fairlead eyes through the B-12 ports; the middle column counts them (6 hooks). REC is about as fast as today in F2/F3 and slower on bag-on because W1-W3 now go on with the bag; it is faster in F1 (3 hooks instead of 6).

**F1 meat in the bag, REC:**

1. Open the roll-top. Using the door instead: unhook the W1 and W2 G-hooks first (2 x 4 s). (0 s)
2. Set the meat against B-01 over the spreader. The IM straps are already on the left eyes. (0 s)
3. Hook each IM free end on its right eye (1 hook each) and cam. (12 s)
4. Close. Door used: re-hook W1, W2 and snug (2 x 4 s). (0 s)

**F2 bag-off haul, REC:**

1. Unhook W1-W3 at their G-hooks (3 x 4 s). (12 s)
2. Bag off (attach_system bag_off). (52 s)
3. Unhook the 3 IM free ends and pull them out through the B-12 ports; they stay on the left eyes. (21 s)
4. Set the quarter in the cradle, cam to a 70 mm belly. (12 s)
5. Hook each IM free end on its right eye over the meat and cam. (12 s)
6. Fold the empty bag flat and strap it over the crown with W3. (20 s)

**F3 float, REC:**

1. Unhook W1-W3 (3 x 4 s). (12 s)
2. Bag off. (52 s)
3. Unhook the 3 IM free ends, pull them out through the ports. (21 s)
4. Cradle. (12 s)
5. IM free ends on the right eyes over the meat, cam. (12 s)
6. Take the B-24 pair out of the kill kit. (5 s)
7. Hook a B-24 link on each IM3 eye. (8 s)
8. Hang the bag on the loop that touches. (14 s)
9. Close W1-W3 round bag and meat. (18 s)

**F4 carry compression, REC:** pack; hook W1-W3 at their left G-hooks; pull each tail snug at the right ladder lock; tuck the tail in its keeper loop. Small load: pull further; a W3 tail can go once more round.

**F5 side carry, REC:** tripod feet in the IMP-8 cup, legs under W1 and W2 on the right gore, cinch; poles or a pad on the left the same way; spotter in IMP-1 on the left gusset.

**F6 door, REC:** unhook W1/W2, unzip, load, zip, re-hook W1/W2 and cinch before the next heavy carry. The zip is closed after the straps are snug (never used as the clamp).

**F7, REC:** unchanged roll-top and pouch straps.
