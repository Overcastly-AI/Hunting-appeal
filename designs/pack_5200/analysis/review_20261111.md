## 0. Verdict in short

- **4 blockers, 11 should-fix, 8 notes.** None of the blockers says a part is known to break. Each one says a pass
  is claimed (or a release rule is written) that the evidence does not support.
- **Blockers:** (B1) the CA-1 release rules still use the 0.234 flip point. That would release an H1 whose IM3 eye
  fails fatigue at any coupon ratio below 0.290. (B2) the 27 mm IM3 cheek's fatigue 1.04 is smaller than the
  scatter in its own inputs, and nothing has checked the H1 body against the cloth-sim IM3 force, which is
  about 3x the beam model's. (B3) R-28 at 23 in passes only on the coarse mesh. The fine-mesh face shift is
  larger than the 3 mm allowance, and with it the result is 30 deg, a fail. (B4) deleting the hood and the IM2
  bag hooks reverses an owner decision (2026-11-06) and was adopted as "delegated".
- **Weight roll-up:** the totals reproduce exactly. No saving is double-counted between BL-003, BL-004, BL-010,
  BL-011 and BL-013 (the step sums close to 0.1 g). However, "proven now" holds about 220 g of savings that, by
  the roll-up's own rule, belong in "after tests". The cheek is booked at 25 mm instead of the adopted 27 mm (+1.5 g).

## 2. Blockers

### B1. The CA-1 release rule would release an H1 whose IM3 eye fails fatigue
- **Where:** `designs/pack_5200/tests_physical/CA1_fatigue_rig.md:16-17` ("H1 still passes if ... >= 0.234"),
  `:126` ("released as designed (body fatigue margin 1.28)"), `:129-130`; `tests_physical/TEST_PLAN.md:223`;
  `analysis/frame_h1.md:30`, `:286`.
- **Failure:** the flip point is 0.30 / margin. On the adopted bases it is body 0.30 / 1.150 = **0.261**
  (1.144 with BL-004 loads: 0.262), and for the **27 mm IM3 cheek 0.30 / 1.036 = 0.290** (R3). The rig doc tells
  the owner to release H1 at any ratio >= 0.234. A coupon result anywhere in 0.234-0.29 (for example the
  "test 3 bars at 0.25" branch, `:129`) releases a frame whose IM3 eye is below 1.0 in fatigue. The A_c17 OD 17
  legs (old basis 1.209) and the BL-007 variants (estimated 0.97 / 0.93) have no per-torso flip point either.
- **Fix:** restate the CA-1 table with flip = 0.30 / min(body, IM3 eye, joint) on the per-torso basis = **0.290**
  today. Gate R1 on that number. Also put the IM3 eye into CA1_fatigue_rig.md section 6.

### B2. The IM3 eye margin (1.04) is smaller than the scatter of its inputs, and the H1 body has not been checked against the forces that broke it
- **Where:** `analysis/loadpath_verification.md:11`, `:246-258`, `:337`; DECISIONS row "IM3 fairlead cheek set to
  27 mm"; `huntkit/gendesign/loadpath_check.py:208-228` (sizing), `:320-321` (frozen forces);
  `data/materials/polymers/pa12_cf_fdm_paht.yaml:39,52` (fatigue_1e5_ratio 0.3 is an ASSUMPTION, "no published data").
- **Failure:** the 4 % margin sits inside each of these, one at a time:
  1. FEA grid: fi99 moves by -1 % / +8 % between h 1.0, 0.8 and 0.7 mm (R4). The sizing picked 27 mm partly because
     26.5 mm "voxelises to 26" (`loadpath_verification.md:337`). The metric is grid-dependent.
  2. Cloth-sim force: the 552.4 N comes from the coarse h 40 mm mesh. No fine run of the meat case exists (R10).
     R and L differ by 4 % on the same run, and S15 vs CORRIE F moved the force by 9 % (507 -> 552 N).
  3. Material: the 0.30 ratio has no data behind it (CA-1 has not been run).
  4. Direction: ASSUMPTION FAIRLEAD_DIR (`loadpath_verification.md:379`) applies the sim magnitude along the
     beam model's rev C vector, not the sim's hook vector. Its "test" is a static pull, which does not settle a
     fatigue direction.
  5. Combination: in F1 the same eye carries the bag hook and the IM3 meat strap free end (two carabiners in one
     throat, `:256`). The sim does not model the meat straps (`attachment_diet.md:309`), and nobody has checked the
     combined eye load.
  6. Physical gate: "a 1e5-cycle coupon at 884 N" is the demand x 1.00 on one coupon, with no load-enhancement or
     life factor. It cannot show a margin above 1.0.
- **Also:** H1 body fatigue 1.150 / 1.144 governs at "C_im3@H23 static_carry" (`loadpath_verification.md:299-300`)
  on beam loads. The beam's worst IM3 bag force is 143-190 N at 1 g, while the cloth sim reads 460 N at 23 in
  and 552 N at 15 in (R8). BL-010 found this gap at the eye ("the beam model understates the IM3 share of the bag
  couple"), but did not carry it to the stay or body near z 520.
- **Fix:** (a) size the cheek to fatigue >= about 1.25 at h 0.8 (needs new FEA: none of the frozen variants
  reaches 1.1 at 15 in; 24 x 10 mm gives 1.09). The cost is about 1-3 g a pair. (b) Run `F_PR15` meat on the fine
  mesh and take the hook force and vector from the sim. (c) Re-run the H1 body check with the sim's IM3 and hinge
  forces at 15 / 19 / 23 in. (d) The eye coupon should run at least 1.15 x 884 N, or 3 coupons. Until (c) is done,
  the R1 print release should not rely on 1.150.

### B3. R-28 at 23 in "passes" only on the coarse mesh
- **Where:** `analysis/corrie_f_body.md:20-22`, `:180-186` (5.2); `huntkit/gendesign/attach_system.py:119`
  (`R28_ALLOW = 3.0`, "ASSUMPTION: fabric thickness + coarse-mesh scatter"); DECISIONS BL-011 row ("R-28 pass at
  15 / 19 / 23 in (34 / 34 / 32 deg)").
- **Failure:** I reproduced 32 deg with a 4.0 mm gap (R5). The only coarse/fine pair of CORRIE F sims moves the
  frame-side face **5.3-6.4 mm toward the head at z 735-835** (R7), which is twice the 3 mm allowance, and that
  allowance is also meant to cover fabric thickness. With 6 mm more wall, R-28 at 23 in is **30 deg, a fail** (R6).
  BL-011 says itself that "the margin is 2-3 deg" (`corrie_f_body.md:186`).
- **Fix:** run `corrie_f_sim.run('F', 23, cases='meat', fine=True)` (one run) and re-read R-28. If it fails, set the
  top setback to about 40 mm (costs about 0.7 L) or accept it as an owner decision. Until then, record R-28 at 23 in
  as "pending fine mesh".

### B4. Hood and IM2 bag-hook deletion reverses an owner decision
- **Where:** DECISIONS row 69 (2026-11-06, **Owner:** "attachment system accepted as designed (crown hood plus fixed
  guys, 2200 N rated bag hooks at all four points)"); row 108 (BL-004 "adopted (delegated)");
  `analysis/attachment_diet.md:313-321` (BL-004 itself lists both as "Owner decisions needed"); RULES.md rule 8.
- **Failure:** the change replaces the owner's "one lift releases the top" (2026-11-06) with two toggles.
  It also makes "guys on" a carry rule that protects a rev C CG exceedance (guys off, S15 15 in meat CG
  199.5 mm > 190, `attachment_diet.md:320`). The project's own research named "threaded / clipped at the top with
  the bag on" as the field failure point (`attach_system.py:1443-1445`). The wing deletion, a comparable reversal of
  an owner choice (2026-11-07), went to the owner. This one did not. The 2026-11-10 delegation (row 95) covers
  release order, not owner design choices.
- **Fix:** put the hood deletion and the IM2 hook deletion to the owner with the -103 g, the toggle handling and the
  carry rule spelled out. Until the owner answers, record both as "proposed". The roll-up (`P_F`) books them as proven.
