# BL-002 Close out the bag competition

**Wave:** 1 · **Agent:** design-reviewer · **Size:** M · **Depends on:** BL-001

## Why
The five bag teams were stopped by the usage limit before reporting. Their files, sims and renders are saved, but nobody verified them or judged them.

## Goal
A verified, normalised verdict the owner can pick from.

## Read first
`docs/backlog/RULES.md`; `designs/pack_5200/bag_comp/RULES.md` (gate and scoring weights); each team's folder `bag_comp/{A,lean,C,D,form}/`, its `result.json`, its write-up `designs/pack_5200/analysis/bag_comp_{A,lean,C,D,form}.md`, its tests `tests/test_bag_comp_{A,lean,C,D,form}.py`; the renders `docs/screens/20261110_bag{A,B,C,D,E}_*.png` (team B = folder `lean`, team E = folder `form`); `designs/pack_5200/analysis/mass_ledger.md` from BL-001.

## Tasks
1. For each team: run its tests; check that `result.json` can be regenerated by its own script and matches; list missing required items (team C has 4 screenshots, not 5; some write-ups stopped before their final section).
2. Check each entry against the fixed list in `bag_comp/RULES.md` (frame interface, 85 L ±3, width, CG limits with the +4 mm rounding bias added, access).
3. Look at every render (Read each PNG). Score render quality against the bar and name each visible defect.
4. Put all five on the BL-001 bag-system basis. Score with the RULES weights. Show sensitivity: mass weight 30 %, build weight 30 %.
5. Write the verdict: winner, runner-up, and the best idea to keep from each entry. Do not re-design anything.
6. Make one contact sheet (the five hero renders side by side with name, bag-system mass, hours, 15 in meat CG, access misses).

## Deliverables
- `designs/pack_5200/analysis/bag_comp_verdict.md`, `designs/pack_5200/bag_comp/verdict.json`
- `outputs/screens/20261111_BL002_1.png` (contact sheet), `_2.png` (score bars)

## Acceptance
- Every claim in the verdict cites a file or a test you ran.
- Entries that fail the gate are named, with the failing number.

## Out of scope
Editing team files. Re-running fine cloth sims.
