# How leading hunting and expedition packs build the main bag (Nov 2026)

Owner: pack-designer. Date 2026-11-07. Purpose: research step before drawing alternative bag bodies for Pack 5200
(`designs/pack_5200/analysis/bag_alternatives.md`). Benchmarks for **performance and construction method only**: no
shape, name, layout or trade dress is copied. Every claim carries the URL it came from. Where a claim comes from a
forum, a retailer listing or a search-engine summary of a page rather than the maker's own spec, it is marked
**(forum)**, **(retailer)** or **(secondary)**. Forum posts are anecdotes, not failure rates.

Earlier research this builds on: `docs/research/pack_market_2026.md` (benchmarks, pain points P1..P10, IP notes).

## 1. Summary for the designer

1. **Roll-top plus one front zip is the dominant hunting architecture.** Exo's whole K4 line is roll-top with a front
   zip: a straight full-length zip on the 3600 / 7200 and a horseshoe panel on the 5000
   ([Exo, K4 main bag access](https://exomtngear.com/blogs/article/k4-main-bag-access)). Stone Glacier's Sky 5900 bag
   has a large front U-shaped / panel zip (retailer:
   [goHUNT Sky 5900](https://shop.gohunt.com/products/stone-glacier-sky-5900-backpack)). KUIU Pro bags use a "full
   horseshoe zipper" main opening (retailer:
   [ActiveJunky, PRO LT 4000](https://www.activejunky.com/product/kuiu-pro-lt-4000-pack),
   [KUIU PRO Divide 4500](https://www.kuiu.com/products/pro-divide-4500-pack-ash)).
2. **The roll-top is the default top closure for the "top-loader" step** (break camp, load the bag); the zip is for
   daytime access. Exo states this split directly and says front access matters both "when the bag is completely
   full, as well as when it is nearly empty and compressed"
   ([Exo](https://exomtngear.com/blogs/article/k4-main-bag-access)).
3. **A straight zip is chosen for simplicity and weight; a horseshoe for visibility.** Exo: "The straight zipper
   offers great access, a simple design, and helps keep weight to a minimum"; the horseshoe "can be laid back" for wide
   visibility ([Exo](https://exomtngear.com/blogs/article/k4-main-bag-access)).
4. **Side zips and 3-zip panels exist, mostly at Mystery Ranch.** The Metcalf uses a 3-ZIP design
   ([Mystery Ranch Fall 2023 hunt workbook](https://issuu.com/mysteryranch/docs/f23_hunt_mr_2022_final_digital));
   the Marshall has full-length dual side zips plus a top-loading shroud (retailer:
   [Bass Pro, Marshall](https://www.basspro.com/shop/en/mystery-ranch-marshall-backpack)); the Overload pack is
   "Classic 3-ZIP design for easy, rapid access" ([Mystery Ranch, Overload](https://www.mysteryranch.com/overload-pack)).
5. **Rear-entry (back-panel) access is a whitetail / tree-stand and camera-bag idea, not a framed meat-hauler idea.**
   We found it only on day packs, where the pack is swung to the front to reach the back-panel zip
   ([Bowhunter, 2010](https://www.bowhunter.com/editorial/gear_fieldwear_bw_packhounds_unite022210/310675),
   [Field & Stream, best hunting backpacks](https://www.fieldandstream.com/outdoor-gear/best-hunting-backpacks/)).
   On a framed pack the back panel is the frame; on ours it is B-01 with the meat straps, fairleads and hood.
   **Rear entry is not a candidate for Pack 5200.**
6. **Zips are the field failure everyone fears.** Hunters report main zips blowing out on a full pack and finishing a
   hunt "with just the two slider bodies holding the lid closed" (forum:
   [ROKSLIDE, wary about zippers](https://rokslide.com/forums/threads/is-anybody-else-wary-about-zippers-on-hard-use-packs.138401/),
   [ROKSLIDE, ROKMAN review](https://rokslide.com/forums/threads/rokman-extreme-combo-pack-and-gear-on-going-review.102533/post-1027627),
   [Eastmans forum, Blacks Creek](https://forum.eastmans.com/threads/blacks-creek.796),
   [HuntTalk, why my pack sucks](https://www.hunttalk.com/threads/why-my-pack-sucks-kifaru-stone-glacier-exo-seek-mystery-ranch-kuiu-etc.290968/post-2852334)).
   Most posters blame over-stuffing. The design answer: keep the zip short, out of the hoop-load path, and keep the
   roll-top able to carry the load if the zip fails.
7. **Cottage makers keep the body simple.** Seek Outside builds the Unaweep as a roll-top bag and offers a side zip
   only as a custom option; a forum post quoting the company says they dropped vertical side zips to keep bags fully
   waterproof (secondary/forum:
   [SectionHiker, Unaweep 4800](https://sectionhiker.com/paradox-packs-unaweep-4800-backpack-for-winter-use/),
   [Backpacking Light forum](https://backpackinglight.com/forums/topic/92072/page/2/)). Beginner MYOG roll-top
   patterns are built almost entirely from rectangles
   ([trueelena roll-top backpack pattern](https://sewing-patterns.trueelena.org/accessories/bags/roll_top_backpack/index.html),
   [Vicky Myers roll-top tutorial](https://www.vickymyerscreations.co.uk/?p=14820),
   [MYOG 40 L frameless roll-top guide, "beginner friendly"](https://myogweb.pages.dev/40-litre-frameless-ultralight-backpack)).

## 2. Architectures

### 2.1 Top-loader roll-top
- **Who:** Exo K4 (all), Seek Outside Unaweep, most UL cottage packs
  ([Exo](https://exomtngear.com/blogs/article/k4-main-bag-access),
  [SectionHiker, Unaweep](https://sectionhiker.com/paradox-packs-unaweep-4800-backpack-for-winter-use/)).
- **Why:** a roll-top "creates a true sealed system" and is more secure and weather-resistant than a drawstring
  ([Exo](https://exomtngear.com/blogs/article/k4-main-bag-access)). It is also the volume adjuster (rolled down vs
  full).
- **Pain point:** reaching low items. A Unaweep owner selling a custom bag wanted a zip so he did not "go armpit deep
  into the bag" (forum: [BPL gear swap](https://backpackinglight.com/gear-swaps/seek-outside-custom-unaweep-5400)).
- **Build:** a straight tube collar with a stiffened hem; the simplest closure there is (the stuff sack is the classic
  first MYOG project: [bikepacking.com, make your own stuff sack](https://bikepacking.com/?p=74525)).

### 2.2 Panel loader / U-zip / horseshoe
- **Who:** Exo K4 5000 (horseshoe panel under a roll-top), KUIU Pro LT / Pro Divide (full horseshoe), Stone Glacier
  Sky 5900 (front U-zip panel), Mystery Ranch 3-ZIP
  ([Exo](https://exomtngear.com/blogs/article/k4-main-bag-access),
  [ActiveJunky, KUIU PRO LT 4000](https://www.activejunky.com/product/kuiu-pro-lt-4000-pack),
  [goHUNT Sky 5900](https://shop.gohunt.com/products/stone-glacier-sky-5900-backpack),
  [Mystery Ranch, Overload](https://www.mysteryranch.com/overload-pack)).
- **Why:** best visibility and reach, "including items on the bottom, without unloading the entire pack"
  (retailer, Sky 5900: [goHUNT](https://shop.gohunt.com/products/stone-glacier-sky-5900-backpack)). Exo sews a stash
  pocket into the back of the horseshoe panel ([Exo](https://exomtngear.com/blogs/article/k4-main-bag-access)).
- **Pain points:** a long zip loaded in hoop tension when the bag is full; a "U-zip at the bottom" gives good access
  to the bottom "but not so great access to things in the middle" (forum:
  [BPL, side zip vs bottom zip](https://backpackinglight.com/forums/topic/thoughts-on-packs-with-a-single-side-zip-versus-bottom-zip/)).
  Lashing gear over a horseshoe blocks it (retailer review summary, KUIU Ultra 3000:
  [byland.co](https://byland.co/blog/kuiu-ultra-3000-review)).
- **Build:** the zip must turn two corners (radius) and is sewn into a window or a curved seam; this is the hardest zip
  install of the four architectures (our assessment; see §4).

### 2.3 Clamshell / full-length side zip
- **Who:** Exo K4 3600 / 7200 (one straight full-length zip), Mystery Ranch Marshall (dual full-length side zips),
  Metcalf (side zip into the main bag)
  ([Exo](https://exomtngear.com/blogs/article/k4-main-bag-access),
  [Bass Pro, Marshall](https://www.basspro.com/shop/en/mystery-ranch-marshall-backpack),
  [Mystery Ranch hunt workbook](https://issuu.com/mysteryranch/docs/f23_hunt_mr_2022_final_digital)).
  Seek Outside offers a side zip as a custom option (secondary:
  [SectionHiker](https://sectionhiker.com/paradox-packs-unaweep-4800-backpack-for-winter-use/)).
- **Why:** a straight zip is light and simple ([Exo](https://exomtngear.com/blogs/article/k4-main-bag-access)).
- **Pain points:** on a full bag a single side zip "might become just a narrow slit"; "a busted zipper is also very
  difficult to repair" (forum: [BPL](https://backpackinglight.com/forums/topic/thoughts-on-packs-with-a-single-side-zip-versus-bottom-zip/)).
  A Metcalf reviewer found the side zip annoying because it sits under the side buckles (retailer review, per search
  summary of [Sportsman's Warehouse, Metcalf](https://SportsmansWarehouse.com/hunting-gear-supplies/hunting-packs/hunting-expedition-packs/mystery-ranch-metcalf-hunting-backpack-coyote/p/p237115)).
  An Exo K4 5000 reviewer wanted a side zip into the main bag in a future version
  ([Outside Bozeman, K4 5000 review](https://outsidebozeman.com/gear/product-reviews/misc/review-exo-k4-5000-hunting-pack)).
- **Build:** the easiest zip to fit: a straight zip set into a straight seam you are sewing anyway.

### 2.4 Rear-entry / back-panel access
- Found on day packs and camera bags, where the pack is rotated to the front
  ([Bowhunter](https://www.bowhunter.com/editorial/gear_fieldwear_bw_packhounds_unite022210/310675),
  [backpackies, rear access packs](https://backpackies.com/blog/backpacks-with-zipper-on-back)). Reviews also flag
  "interior zipper design isn't super durable" on one such pack
  ([Field & Stream](https://www.fieldandstream.com/outdoor-gear/best-hunting-backpacks/)).
- We found **no** framed meat-hauler with rear entry in this search. On Pack 5200 the frame-side panel B-01 carries
  the fairleads, the meat-strap ports, the hood ladder and the hinge eyes: a zip there would cross every load path.

### 2.5 Simple box versus tapered body
- The leaders' copy stresses carrying the load close to the back
  ([Outside, Stone Glacier X-Curve](https://www.outsideonline.com/outdoor-gear/hiking-gear/stone-glacier-x-curve-backpack?scope=anon);
  Stone Glacier advises against heavy items at the very bottom or top:
  [Stone Glacier pack guide](https://www.stoneglacier.com/blogs/the-vestibule/the-complete-guide-to-stone-glacier-hunting-packs)).
  We did not find a maker's own statement that justifies a tapered silhouette (arm swing, rifle butt); our taper is a
  design assumption already used in v3.1 (narrow at the belt), not a cited fact.
- Beginner patterns are rectangles ([trueelena](https://sewing-patterns.trueelena.org/accessories/bags/roll_top_backpack/index.html),
  [Vicky Myers](https://www.vickymyerscreations.co.uk/?p=14820)); Vicky Myers rates even a rectangle roll-top as
  intermediate, not complete-beginner, work. A **tapered box** (trapezoid panels) keeps every seam straight: taper costs
  nothing in sewing difficulty, curves do.
- Our own data (the v3.1 BUILD_PLAN "What's hard" table): the curved gore seams and the bottom oval are the two hard
  operations in SPINE (`designs/pack_5200/BUILD_PLAN.md` §2).

### 2.6 Panel count and seam types
- **Cottage UL packs:** few panels, heavier fabric on the bottom, seams taped on the vertical side seams (Hyperlite:
  body DCH 50 + bottom DCH 150; "taped all of the vertical side seams" per retailer/reviews:
  [CleverHiker, Unbound 40](https://www.cleverhiker.com/backpacking/hyperlite-mountain-gear-unbound-40-ultralight-backpack-review/),
  [REI, Southwest 55](https://www.rei.com/product/C02641/hyperlite-mountain-gear-southwest-55-pack)). Reviewers
  report the untaped bottom seam lets water in and bottom seams fraying
  ([REI, Southwest 55](https://www.rei.com/product/C02641/hyperlite-mountain-gear-southwest-55-pack),
  [Switchback Travel, Windrider](https://switchbacktravel.com/reviews/hyperlite-mountain-gear-3400-windrider)).
  Zpacks Arc Haul Ultra: "fully taped seams"
  ([SectionHiker, Arc Haul Ultra 60](https://sectionhiker.com/zpacks-arc-haul-ultra-60l-backpack-review/)).
- **Seam finish:** MYOG makers prefer **bound seams** (grosgrain or self-fabric binding) over French seams on packs:
  binding "reinforces with an extra row of stitches" and French seams are harder to make waterproof; binding also
  stiffens the seam; herringbone tape is preferred on curves (forum:
  [BPL, question on seams](https://backpackinglight.com/forums/topic/question-on-seams/),
  [BPL, VX21/VX42 back and bottom seams](https://backpackinglight.com/forums/topic/backpack-seams-myog-vx21-and-vx42-back-and-bottom/)).
  Pack 5200 already specifies 12 mm bound seams on the body (`panels.yaml` B-01..B-05).
- **Exo's minimalism:** external pockets and no internal organisation, by design
  ([Exo, minimal internal organization](https://exomtngear.com/blogs/article/why-do-k4-bags-have-minimal-internal-organization)).

## 4. What cottage makers use because it is simple to sew (synthesis)

1. **Straight seams on flat panels** (rectangles and trapezoids) and a straight tube roll-top collar
   ([trueelena](https://sewing-patterns.trueelena.org/accessories/bags/roll_top_backpack/index.html),
   [Vicky Myers](https://www.vickymyerscreations.co.uk/?p=14820)).
2. **One straight zip set into a straight seam**, or no zip at all
   ([Exo, straight zip rationale](https://exomtngear.com/blogs/article/k4-main-bag-access);
   Seek Outside custom-only side zip:
   [SectionHiker](https://sectionhiker.com/paradox-packs-unaweep-4800-backpack-for-winter-use/)).
3. **Heavier bottom panel, bound or taped seams** ([CleverHiker](https://www.cleverhiker.com/backpacking/hyperlite-mountain-gear-unbound-40-ultralight-backpack-review/),
   [BPL seams](https://backpackinglight.com/forums/topic/question-on-seams/)).
4. Curved two-way seams (gores, ovals) appear on premium bags but are exactly what a first-time builder fails on
   (our BUILD_PLAN §2). Horseshoe zips need curved corners and are the hardest zip.

## 5. Gaps (not found; do not quote as fact)
- No maker statement on why their bags taper toward the hipbelt.
- No failure-rate data for any zip architecture; all failure evidence above is anecdotal.
- No source for the panel count of any named premium hunting bag; we count our own.
