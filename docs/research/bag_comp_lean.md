# Bag competition, Team B LEAN: research (2026-11-10)

Purpose: find what the lightest credible hunting bag systems do, what users hate about light bags, and which of our
own records support a lighter bag. Every claim carries a URL. Reused sources are marked [BA] (bag_body_architectures.md)
or [HL] (hunting_loadouts.md); they keep their original URLs here. Market data is a performance benchmark only: no
shape, name, pattern or trade dress is copied.

## 1. Benchmarks: bag-only masses of light hunting bags

What that means for us: the bag-system number in the competition (bag, pockets, lid/pouch, compression, fly) is not
the same scope as these bag-only figures (ours excludes the frame-interface parts that sit in the frame budget and
includes the fly), so they are a sanity range, not a like-for-like target. CORRIE B.2's bag system books 716 g by our
own code (analysis/bag_comp_lean.md section 2).

## 2. What makes a bag light (and what it costs)

1. **Roll-top as the main closure** is the lightest, simplest closure and a volume adjuster; the cost is "armpit
   deep" access to low items ([Exo, main bag access](https://exomtngear.com/blogs/article/k4-main-bag-access) [BA];
   forum: [BPL gear swap, Seek Outside Unaweep](https://backpackinglight.com/gear-swaps/seek-outside-custom-unaweep-5400) [BA]).
   Kifaru's Gnargali is top-load only, no zip at all ([Kifaru Gnargali](https://kifaru.net/products/gnargali) [BA]).
2. **A straight zip is lighter and simpler than a horseshoe** ([Exo](https://exomtngear.com/blogs/article/k4-main-bag-access) [BA]).
   The cost: a single slit "becomes a slit when full" and is hard to repair (forum:
   [BPL, side zip vs bottom zip](https://backpackinglight.com/forums/topic/thoughts-on-packs-with-a-single-side-zip-versus-bottom-zip/) [BA]).
   Kifaru's Reckoning uses a full-length centre zip ([ROKSLIDE Reckoning](https://www.rokslide.com/kifaru-reckoning-review/) [BA]).
3. **Zips are the top field failure** on hard-use packs, so fewer and shorter zips is also a durability argument
   (forum: [ROKSLIDE, wary about zippers](https://rokslide.com/forums/threads/is-anybody-else-wary-about-zippers-on-hard-use-packs.138401/),
   [HuntTalk, why my pack sucks](https://www.hunttalk.com/threads/why-my-pack-sucks-kifaru-stone-glacier-exo-seek-mystery-ranch-kuiu-etc.290968/post-2852334) [BA]).
4. **Optional, separate lids and add-on pockets** are common and wanted: Granite Gear Crown3, Seek Outside, Kifaru
   Absaroka ([OutdoorGearLab Crown3](https://www.outdoorgearlab.com/reviews/camping-and-hiking/ultralight-backpack/granite-gear-crown3-60),
   [GearJunkie Absaroka](https://gearjunkie.com/outdoor/hunt-fish/kifaru-absaroka-review)); a KUIU owner disliked
   that the lid is not sold separately ([ROKSLIDE ICON 6](https://rokslide.com/forums/threads/new-kuiu-icon-6.455714/)) [BA].
   Kifaru sells add-on pouches; users complain about "price and weight once accessories are added"
   ([ROKSLIDE Ma Deuce](https://www.rokslide.com/forums/threads/kifaru-ma-deuce-review-updated-9-26-21.212029/page-3),
   [ROKSLIDE Ark vs Duplex](https://www.rokslide.com/kifaru-ark-frame-vs-kifaru-duplex-frame/)) [BA]. Lesson: modules
   only save weight if the owner actually leaves them at home; the mass case must be shown per loadout.
5. **Reachable pockets beat many pockets.** Exo added a forward-facing lower side compartment because users could not
   reach the top-entry side pockets ([Exo, stretch pockets](https://exomtngear.com/blogs/article/k4-stretch-pockets) [BA]).
   Pockets you cannot reach are the recurring hate ([CleverHiker Crown3](https://cleverhiker.com/backpacking/granite-gear-crown3-backpack-review) [BA]).
6. **Lashing over a zip blocks it** ([byland.co, KUIU Ultra 3000](https://byland.co/blog/kuiu-ultra-3000-review);
   retailer review: [Sportsman's Warehouse, Metcalf](https://SportsmansWarehouse.com/hunting-gear-supplies/hunting-packs/hunting-expedition-packs/mystery-ranch-metcalf-hunting-backpack-coyote/p/p237115)) [BA].
   Our design takes this cost knowingly: W1/W2 cross the centre zip (section 4 of the analysis).

## 3. Light fabrics: the evidence in our records

- **ULTRAGrid 210D** 132 gsm, Taber 1100 cycles (ASTM D3884), "does not tear" (UHMWPE grid)
  ([Challenge EcoPak guide 2023](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/62018e9b29f2cd0fc23c3a0c/t/63d8117224b90b2859f159a4/1675104626522/2023_EcoPak-Guide_v3.pdf),
  [Extremtextil ULTRAGrid](https://www.extremtextil.de/en/ultragrid-210den-recycled-nylon-with-black-uhmw-pe-ripstop-132g-sqm/72602));
  record `ultragrid_210d_uhmwpe_ripstop`; verdict O-4 "proceed after the owner sees a swatch" (fabric_swap_evidence.md).
  It matches 330D on abrasion, it is not better, and it is ~55-60 % of 500D on Challenge's own scale.
- **70D ripstop** 92 gsm, tear 51.6 / 37.4 N (D1424), grab 242 / 174 N
  ([RBTR 1.9 oz data sheet](https://ripstopbytheroll.com/cdn/shop/products/1.9ozPUCoatedRipstopNylon-FabricDataSheeta_5000x.png?v=1631631608));
  record `nylon_ripstop_70d`; verdict O-3 "proceed".
- **ULTRA 200X** 133 gsm, tear 459 / 593 N, Taber 4400, waterproof film
  ([Extremtextil ULTRA 200 X](https://www.extremtextil.de/en/ultra-200-x-backpack-laminate-with-uhmwpe-133g-sqm/72682));
  record `challenge_ultra_200`; verdict O-5 "proceed after PT-3 and a 50 mm strip test" (strip strength is not
  published anywhere). Ultra 100/200 are positioned for light pack bodies and pockets, less abrasion-resistant than
  Ultra 400 ([SectionHiker, Ultra fabrics](https://sectionhiker.com/ultra-fabrics-for-ultralight-backpacking-gear-tents-packs-and-stuff-sacks/),
  [Garage Grown Gear, Ultra fabric](https://www.garagegrowngear.com/en-au/blogs/trail-talk/ultra-fabric-ultralight-cottage-gear)).
- **Noise.** No hands-on report rates ULTRA laminates for hunting noise. Hunters do complain about crinkly pack
  fabric ([ROKSLIDE, Mystery Ranch noisy fabric?](https://rokslide.com/forums/threads/mystery-ranch-noisy-fabric.231040)),
  and quiet-marketed packs use brushed or fleece faces ([ALPS Impulse](https://www.scheels.com/p/4882-9620140/)).
  Our records rate ULTRA 200 at quietness 2 of 5 and ULTRAGrid / 70D / 330D at 3 (repo ratings, ASSUMPTION). So the
  laminate is limited to the frame face (already ULTRA on CORRIE) and the floor, which does not brush brush.

## 4. Patents / IP

No new mechanism: roll-top, a centre zip, webbing loops in seams and G-hook straps are generic. The frame interface
is unchanged, so guardrails H1-H4 in docs/DECISIONS.md are untouched. Name FELL is a common English noun for a bare
hillside; we found no pack product of that name in the sources above (not a trademark search).
