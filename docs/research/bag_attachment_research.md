# Bag-to-frame attachment on framed hunting and meat-hauling packs

Research for `designs/pack_5200/analysis/bag_attachment.md` (branch fairleads) and
`analysis/meat_haul_modes.md`. Date: 2026-11-05.

## How this was researched, and how far to trust it

- **Sources.** Brand pages and brand blogs come first. Retailer copy, reviews, forum threads and patents come after.
  Every claim below carries a URL.
- **Access problems.** Rokslide forum pages, the Rokslide review pages, the Exo product page on one retailer, the CIPO
  (Canada) patent page and the Justia assignee pages returned 403, 404, 429, 503 or 530 to the fetch tool.
  For those pages the content comes from a search-engine summary of the page, not from the page itself. Those
  claims are marked ***(reported)***. Treat them as one user's account. Where a forum post is quoted, it is one
  owner and not a brand statement.
- **Reddit.** Searches aimed at r/elkhunting, r/bowhunting and r/Ultralight returned no Reddit threads. The forum
  evidence here is Rokslide, HuntTalk, Backpacking Light, Carryology and Outside Bozeman. Reddit sentiment is
  **not covered**.
- **Unverified.** Anything not found is labelled "not found" or "unverified". Nothing is filled in from memory.
- **Patents.** Patent data comes from Google Patents pages. Legal status and expiry dates there are Google's own
  estimates, and Google says so on each page. This is not a freedom-to-operate opinion. A patent attorney has to
  read the claims before anything ships.

Units: mm, N, g internally, with imperial alongside.

## 1. Pack by pack

### 1.1 Stone Glacier: Krux / Xcurve frames (Load Shelf) and Terminus (Load Cell)

**Hardware and method**
- The load is "supported by 12 connection points to the frame via 1" webbing", using "Heavy-duty, 1" Duraflex
  military approved buckles and webbing"
  ([SG Krux frame](https://www.stoneglacier.com/products/krux-frame-foliage-tan),
  [Gearfool Krux](https://www.gearfool.com/collections/gear/products/stone-glacier-krux-frame-1)).
- One owner counts **four buckles** that have to be threaded to take the bag off and put it back
  ([HuntingBC archive](https://www.huntingbc.ca/forum/archive/index.php/t-93182.html) ***(reported)***).
- **Top attachment.** No brand diagram was found. Owners report that the top strap runs to "a sewn in tri slide on
  the frame". They also report that the top strap "wants to slip off the top if just going horizontal"
  ([Rokslide X Kurve thread](https://rokslide.com/forums/threads/stone-glacier-x-kurve.58677/page-2) ***(reported)***).
  On the Load Cell, an owner guessed that two grey buckles tie the frame's top straps to the bag. This is a guess
  ([Rokslide](https://rokslide.com/forums/threads/testing-the-2018-stone-glacier-evo-frame-40-56-bag.85646/page-4) ***(reported)***).
- **Swapping bags.** Swapping two buckles returns the frame to the standard Xcurve/Krux setup
  ([Black Ovis](https://blackovis.com/stone-glacier-minimalist-krux-evo-frame-2022) ***(reported)***). The frame-side male
  buckles can be replaced with female adjustable buckles to run the frame with no bag (Minimalist mode)
  ([EuroOptic buckle kit](https://eurooptic.com/Stone-Glacier-Minimalist-Buckle-Kit-Black-OSFM-40024-BK-OSFM.aspx) ***(reported)***).
- **Terminus.** The bag is **sewn to the frame**. Instead of a shelf it has a 2200 cu in (36 L) internal **Load Cell**
  with two drain holes
  ([SG Load Cell vs Load Shelf](https://www.stoneglacier.com/blogs/the-vestibule/load-cell-vs-load-shelf),
  [SG Terminus 8700](https://stoneglacier.com/products/terminus-8700)).

**Detachability and haul modes**
- "Start by separating the bag from the frame to unveil the Load Shelf." Then: "Reattach the bag starting with the
  bottom straps and work your way up to the top of the pack." Load lifters are tightened last
  ([SG complete guide](https://stoneglacier.com/blogs/the-vestibule/the-complete-guide-to-stone-glacier-hunting-packs)).
- The frame "can be used as a pack frame for load hauling without any bag attached"
  ([SG Krux](https://www.stoneglacier.com/products/krux-frame-foliage-tan)). Extending the Load Shelf straps adds
  "more than 2,500 cubic inches" (41 L)
  ([SG One Frame](https://www.stoneglacier.com/blogs/the-vestibule/one-frame-for-every-hunt)).
- **Time.** About 30 s to take the bag off and about 30 s to put it back, "if you can thread the buckles"
  ([HuntingBC archive](https://www.huntingbc.ca/forum/archive/index.php/t-93182.html) ***(reported)***).
- **Terminus.** The bag cannot come off. Owners found the cell hard to clean and lined it with game bags. SG staff
  recommend a separate load-cell bag
  ([Rokslide Terminus review p7](https://rokslide.com/forums/threads/stone-glacier-terminus-review.267237/page-7),
  [p4](https://rokslide.com/forums/threads/stone-glacier-terminus-review.267237/page-4) ***(reported)***).

**Load path.** The shelf "keeps the heaviest of loads more stable and closer to your back"
([SG Load Shelf](https://stoneglacier.com/pages/load-shelf)). The reattach order, bottom first, keeps the load high.
SG publishes no claim about controlling sway or barndooring.

**Weight.** Krux frame 2 lb 12 oz (≈1247 g) with a medium belt; Short frame 2 lb 10.5 oz (≈1205 g). Rated 150+ lb
(68 kg); tested to 340 lb (154 kg) with no damage
([Gearfool](https://www.gearfool.com/collections/gear/products/stone-glacier-krux-frame-1)). The mass of the attachment
hardware alone is **not published**.

**Complaints**
- Terminus: the load-shelf stays "popped out of the top of the frame" and the frame barrelled after the owner bounced
  it to settle meat. SG advised seating the stays with a rubber mallet
  ([Rokslide Terminus review p10](https://rokslide.com/forums/threads/stone-glacier-terminus-review.267237/page-10) ***(reported)***).
- The top strap slips off the top of the frame when it runs horizontally (see above, ***(reported)***).
- One Rokslide thread is titled "Stone Glacier Kiowa 3200 Failure on Hunt"
  ([Rokslide](https://rokslide.com/forums/threads/stone-glacier-kiowa-3200-failure-on-hunt.254197/page-2) ***(reported)***).
  The failure details were not retrieved.

### 1.2 Exo Mountain Gear: K3 and K4

**Hardware and method (K4)**
- The frame has "4 pairs of integrated compression straps". With a bag on, two pairs hold the load to the frame and
  two pairs connect frame to bag. Top to bottom the order is "bag connection, frame compression, bag connection, frame
  compression". Orange bartacks mark the frame-only straps, which "should not be used to make a frame-to-bag connection"
  ([Exo quick tip](https://exomtngear.com/blogs/article/k4-quick-tip-frame-compression-straps)).
- **Top attachment.** The reviewer says to "release the lid held by the top hooks" and then unbuckle the side straps
  ([Carryology K4](https://www.carryology.com/reviews-2/exo-mtn-gear-k4-review/)). Exo staff said that at the lid,
  "buckles have more play than ladder lock connections", so they used ladder locks to keep the lid snug to the frame
  top. Owners say the **lid is the bridge** at the top, and some never attach the very top of the bag at all
  ([Rokslide K4 vs K3 thread](https://rokslide.com/forums/threads/k4-improvement-vs-k3.313383/latest) ***(reported)***).
- Exo sells **Packout Straps** that run "from the top of the frame to the top of the bag"
  ([Exo Packout Straps](https://exomtngear.com/products/k4-packout-straps)).
- **K3.** A **hook-and-loop (Velcro) flap** holds the top of the bag to the frame. "The entire bag can be disconnected,
  all but the bottom straps, in about 15 seconds"
  ([Rokslide Exo on Kifaru how-to](https://www.rokslide.com/forums/threads/exo-k3-bags-on-kifaru-ark-frames-how-to.396995) ***(reported)***).

**Detachability and haul modes**
- K4 shelf access in Exo's own words: "Disconnect the side-release buckles", "Loosen or remove the lid", "Lift the bag
  over the frame", "Secure your load between the bag and frame"
  ([Exo shelf access](https://exomtngear.com/blogs/article/k4-load-shelf-access)).
- "The conversion takes only a few seconds." Three extra frame straps hold the meat
  ([Outside Bozeman K4 review](https://outsidebozeman.com/gear/product-reviews/misc/review-exo-k4-5000-hunting-pack)).
- A **Crib** load panel hauls meat with no bag on the frame
  ([Exo Crib](https://exomtngear.com/products/k4-crib-load-panel)).

**Load path.** "Angled compression straps" keep the bag from sagging
([Carryology K4](https://www.carryology.com/reviews-2/exo-mtn-gear-k4-review/)). An owner says the "top-side compression
straps and angle up to the frame" hold the top snug
([Rokslide](https://rokslide.com/forums/threads/k4-improvement-vs-k3.313383/latest) ***(reported)***).

**Weight.** K4 frame "2lb, 15oz" (≈1332 g), rated "150+ Pounds"
([Exo K4 frame](https://exomtngear.com/collections/k4-pack-systems/products/k4-frame-system)). The K4 3600 system
(frame, bag and lid) is 5 lb 8 oz (≈2.49 kg)
([Carryology](https://www.carryology.com/reviews-2/exo-mtn-gear-k4-review/)). The mass of the attachment hardware alone
is **not published**.

**Complaints**
- A dealer has seen about 18 K4 problems in three years, "almost always" from owners trying to re-attach the bag to the
  top of the frame with meat in the shelf. Exo staff say the top does not need re-attaching in that mode
  ([Rokslide K4 issue](https://rokslide.com/forums/threads/exo-k4-pack-issue.410398/page-2) ***(reported)***).
  **This is the most important single data point here: the top re-attach under meat is where the field breaks things.**
- With a full shelf, "it's always super tight getting the straps to reach to the bag over the meat"
  ([Rokslide K4 thread](https://rokslide.com/forums/threads/exo-mountain-k4.279017/page-137) ***(reported)***).
- K4 strap-keeper Velcro "no longer sticks to itself" on about half the keepers after six months. On a K3, the hip-belt
  pad Velcro would not stay locked under 80 lb (36 kg)
  ([Rokslide](https://rokslide.com/forums/threads/exo-k4-pack-issue.410398/latest),
  [Rokslide K3 belt](https://www.rokslide.com/forums/threads/k3-hip-belt-help.415360) ***(reported)***).
- Some owners never liked the K3's Velcro wings
  ([Rokslide](https://rokslide.com/forums/threads/k4-improvement-vs-k3.313383/latest) ***(reported)***).
- Squeaks: there is a thread titled "Squeaky Exo pack, any solutions"
  ([Rokslide](https://rokslide.com/forums/threads/squeaky-exo-pack-any-solutions.139880/post-1320302) ***(reported)***).

### 1.3 Kifaru: Duplex / ARK frames, Reckoning and other bags

**Hardware and method**
- The Guide Lid kit for Duplex frames has "Four 30″ compression straps with Polymer K-Clips", "4 male Autolock
  buckles" and "4 slotted female buckles". The ARK kit straps have "a loop on one end that girth-hitches through the holes
  on the side of the ARK frame" ([Kifaru kits](https://kifaru.net/products/guide-lid-and-compression-kits)).
- The MANIML bag's side straps use three-bar sliders for the ARK and come with a K-clip kit for Duplex frames
  ([search summary of Kifaru retail listings](https://shop.gohunt.com/products/kifaru-maniml-backpack-combo) ***(reported)***).
- **Top attachment.** On older frames a **Hypalon/mesh sleeve** covers the top of the frame, and the bag's lifter loop
  pokes through it. On newer bags the lifter loops are sewn directly on the bag. Without the sleeve, "there is
  nothing connecting the top of the pack. There is only side and bottom compression". With meat between bag and frame,
  "you just use the compression straps and the lid to hold it on"
  ([Rokslide Hypalon sleeve thread](https://www.rokslide.com/forums/threads/kifaru-duplex-lite-ultralite-with-hypalon-sleeve-bag.132714/),
  [Rokslide Reckoning/ARK question](https://rokslide.com/forums/threads/kifaru-reckoning-ark-frame-question.361728) ***(reported)***).
- A bag attaches to a Duplex "in two places, the hypalon sleeve and the bottom most tribar slider"
  ([Rokslide](https://rokslide.com/forums/threads/help-with-kifaru-setup.56865/post-588564) ***(reported)***).

**Detachability.** The same straps compress the bag and tie it to the frame, so getting into the bag with meat on is
awkward ([HuntTalk "why my pack sucks"](https://www.hunttalk.com/threads/why-my-pack-sucks-kifaru-stone-glacier-exo-seek-mystery-ranch-kuiu-etc.290968/post-2864510)).
**No published detach time was found.**

**Weight.** A Reckoning bag is 2 lb (907 g) "including all compression straps and buckles"
([Rokslide Reckoning review](https://www.rokslide.com/kifaru-reckoning-review/) ***(reported)***). The mass of the
attachment hardware alone is **not published**.

**Complaints.** "A ton of straps" and "love/hate the need to customize"
([HuntTalk](https://www.hunttalk.com/threads/why-my-pack-sucks-kifaru-stone-glacier-exo-seek-mystery-ranch-kuiu-etc.290968/post-2864510)).
A squeak was traced to "the small ribbon-like loops" at the frame, or to frame rails rubbing inside the bag
([Rokslide Kifaru noise](https://rokslide.com/forums/threads/kifaru-noise.178659/) ***(reported)***). The shelf
option "is on the outside of the pack, away from your body"
([Rokslide](https://rokslide.com/forums/threads/why-do-kifaru-owners-hate-load-shelves.148457/) ***(reported)***).

### 1.4 Mystery Ranch: Guide Light MT / Overload (Metcalf, Beartooth, Pop Up)

**Method.** OVERLOAD is the "separation of bag and frame, the bulk of the weight in between". Gleason's idea was
"Build a flexible shelf, elongate the straps"
([MR Lightening the Load](https://www.mysteryranch.com/post/lightening-the-load)). It is a load sling between pack and
frame ([MR workbook](https://issuu.com/mysteryranch/docs/s24_mr_outdoor_wb_issuu) ***(reported)***). The patents
describe it as follows. The sling is permanently fixed to the bag's front lower edge, and straps with adjustable
buckles clip to a frame cross member. A **collar with an inverted pocket** receives the top of the frame, so the bag
hangs from the frame top ([US 8,579,171](https://patents.google.com/patent/US8579171)).

**Hardware.** Listings describe "Quick attach frame cap hardware" for easier OVERLOAD conversion
([Lancaster Archery Metcalf](https://lancasterarchery.com/products/mystery-ranch-metcalf-pack-optifade-subalpine-camo) ***(reported)***).
Owners say you are "two buckles away" from separating bag and frame. One 2018 owner counted **four buckles to
unthread**, and said the bag cannot be used as a stand-alone meat hauler
([Rokslide Metcalf](https://rokslide.com/forums/threads/mystery-ranch-metcalf-just-arrived-first-impression.95353/post-928648) ***(reported)***).
On the original Guide Light, separating bag from frame meant undoing the load lifters. The MT frame removed that step
([Black Ovis Guide Light MT](https://new.blackovis.com/community/brand-highlights/new-mystery-ranch-guide-light-mt-frame/) ***(reported)***).

**Weight.** The mass of the attachment hardware is **not published**.

**Complaints.** "Mystery Ranch Metcalf loud squeaks"
([Rokslide](https://rokslide.com/forums/threads/mystery-ranch-metcalf-loud-squeaks.318235/)) and "Beartooth 80 noisy - QC
going down?" ([Rokslide](https://rokslide.com/forums/threads/mystery-ranch-beartooth-80-noisy-qc-going-down.287781/)) ***(reported)***.
"I tend to constantly lose the velcro strap retainers on my MR packs", and "So many straps and buckles while empty"
([HuntTalk](https://www.hunttalk.com/threads/why-my-pack-sucks-kifaru-stone-glacier-exo-seek-mystery-ranch-kuiu-etc.290968/post-2864510)).

### 1.5 Seek Outside: Revolution (breakaway) and Unaweep (integrated); Paradox Packs

**Hardware.** A load sling, an over-the-top strap and six compression straps are included
([SO Revolution](https://seekoutside.com/revolution-suspension-and-frame/)). The straps close with **Gatekeepers**, a
clip that hooks a webbing loop and locks with a gate. They take 3/4 in (19 mm) webbing and weigh **3.1 g (0.11 oz)
each**. "Always close the gate first", because overtightening an open gate is the most likely way to break one
([SO Gatekeeper](https://seekoutside.com/gatekeeper-set-10-pack/)). A load panel clips with Gatekeepers to any of
several loops on the frame panel, which sets the load height. The over-the-top strap is the heavier one, with three
connections
([BPL forum, SO employee](https://backpackinglight.com/forums/topic/see-outside-unaweep-or-revolution/page/3/) ***(reported)***).

**Detachability.** The Unaweep is integrated and has no breakaway shelf. One owner pulled the Unaweep bag and ran
the frame bare in **about 2 minutes**
([Rokslide integrated vs breakaway](https://rokslide.com/forums/threads/seek-outside-integrated-vs-breakaway-frame.253301) ***(reported)***).

**Load path.** The cross stays "prevent barreling at all frame heights and reduce back intrusion"
([SO Revolution](https://seekoutside.com/revolution-suspension-and-frame/)). Owner advice: keep the compression
"mid tight before loading" so the load goes up rather than being sucked down
([Rokslide](https://rokslide.com/forums/threads/seek-outside-pack.78239/page-2) ***(reported)***).

**Weight.** Revolution 24 in frame 2 lb 9 oz (≈1162 g); the two removable cross stays are 4 oz (113 g); rated 200+ lb
(91 kg) ([SO Revolution](https://seekoutside.com/revolution-suspension-and-frame/)).

**Paradox Packs.** The Evolution frame came before the Revolution and was later replaced by it
([Rokslide Paradox/Peregrine](https://rokslide.com/forums/threads/seek-outside-paradox-evolution-frame-and-peregrine-pack.56309/latest) ***(reported)***).
It had three compression straps per side and a load shelf. A user said that on the Evo, sag "could be more likely
depending on how you have it connected"
([Rokslide Paradox thread](https://rokslide.com/forums/threads/paradox-evolution-pack-frames.27798/page-3) ***(reported)***).
The full BPL review of the bag attachment is behind a paywall. A commenter there gives 37 oz (1049 g) for the frame
and 13.3 oz (377 g) for the 4800 VX-21 bag ([BPL](https://backpackinglight.com/paradox-evolution-review-chenault)).
The Paradox-specific attachment mechanics are **unverified**.

**Complaints**
- Webbing "kept working out" of a Gatekeeper near the load lifters. The fixes were a reversed Gatekeeper, a second
  pass of the webbing, or a backing tri-glide. One owner broke two or three Gatekeepers cranking a load strap: "You
  can't crank them as tight as a buckle"
  ([Rokslide SO question](https://rokslide.com/forums/threads/seek-outside-question.183258/post-1771700),
  [Rokslide Peregrine p8](https://rokslide.com/forums/threads/seek-outside-peregrine.90522/page-8) ***(reported)***).
- Dirt can "cause the frame to squeak when walking"
  ([HuntTalk](https://www.hunttalk.com/threads/why-my-pack-sucks-kifaru-stone-glacier-exo-seek-mystery-ranch-kuiu-etc.290968/post-2864510)).

### 1.6 KUIU: PRO (carbon frame) and ICON 6

**PRO.** Load-sling mode: "disengage the load lifter straps that connect the top of the bag to the shoulder straps",
then "slightly loosen the lower bag attachment buckles that pull the bag tight to the frame", load, then "reconnect
the side straps, and pull tight". It gives +2500 cu in (41 L). Tighten the lower straps evenly, or the hip belt
rotates (KUIU help, [search summary](https://kuiu.gorgias.help/en-US/how-do-i-put-my-pro-pro-lt-or-pro-divide-backpack-in-load-sling-mode-1352882) ***(reported)***;
the page returned 404 to the fetch tool).
- KUIU has said the load sling "is designed so the bag pulling on the top frame stay from the back is countered by the
  load lifter pulling from the front". It also said that a failure came from misrigging, and that bulky bone-in
  quarters are too thick for the sling and go in the bag
  ([Rokslide KUIU load slings, 2012 rep note](https://rokslide.com/forums/threads/kuiu-load-slings.61726/post-635022) ***(reported)***).
  **This is the clearest statement found of the top-of-bag peel problem and how a leader resolves it.**
- Owners say it takes less than a minute to attach or detach the bag. KUIU uses proprietary 3/4 in buckles
  ([Rokslide KUIU on Kifaru](https://www.rokslide.com/forums/threads/kuiu-bag-on-kifaru-frame.113674/) ***(reported)***).

**ICON 6 (newer, not compatible with PRO).** "Undo eight buckles and two straps. Swap. Reconnect." The **upper frame
stays slide into Hypalon frame-stay pockets on the bag**, and the attachment straps are pulled "moderately tight to
seat the upper frame stays". Two bag attachment straps run to the waist belt, and in sling mode they go to
hook-and-loop patches on the bag. In sling mode: "do not reroute the top compression straps through the upper on-bag
Hypalon frame stay pockets." The frame is 13.1 oz (371 g) regular, 13.7 oz (388 g) tall, and the ICON 6 3500 bag is
1 lb 15 oz (879 g)
([KUIU ICON 6 article](https://kuiu.com/blogs/innovation/the-latest-advancements-in-hunting-pack-design),
[KUIU load sling mode](https://www.kuiu.com/blogs/innovation/kuiu-pro-pack-system-load-sling-mode)).

**Complaints.** "I kept breaking the quick release buckles on the Kuiu when cinching heavy loads" (Ultra 3000). "The
lid is huge and flops around." "Bag attaches to frame weird"
([HuntTalk](https://www.hunttalk.com/threads/why-my-pack-sucks-kifaru-stone-glacier-exo-seek-mystery-ranch-kuiu-etc.290968/post-2864510)).
Quarters are hard to get to ride above the hip belt, and the bag "seems to sag a little with weight in it". Owners
added their own frame straps to lash meat to the frame
([Rokslide quarters on ICON Pro](https://rokslide.com/forums/threads/hauling-meat-quarters-with-kuiu-icon-pro.229231/) ***(reported)***).

### 1.7 Initial Ascent

- Older models buckled the bag to the top of the frame. "The new ones feature slots that the bag slips over, creating a
  much tighter fit with less movement." The lid has holes that slide over the frame
  ([Born Hunting](https://bornhunting.com/tested-initial-ascent-backpacks/)).
- Meat haul: take the bag off, load the optional **Pannier Load Carrier**, lash it, then strap the gear bag over the top
  (same source). The pannier is sold separately for about $64
  ([goHUNT](https://shop.gohunt.com/products/initial-ascent-pannier-load-carrier-accessory) ***(reported)***).
- Complaints: a frame attachment strap was lost, and the lid-to-bag strap kept coming off
  ([Rokslide IA review](https://www.rokslide.com/initial-ascent-backpack-review/) ***(reported)***). IA sells spare
  buckle sets ("This is rare")
  ([IA buckle set](https://initialascent.com/products/extra-buckle-set) ***(reported)***).
- The mass of the attachment hardware and the detach time are **not published**.

### 1.8 Eberlestock

- On the Team Elk, a sewn-in load panel lets the main bag pull away from a tubular aluminium frame. On the Brute series,
  an "Open-Away" meat shelf lets you unclip the bag and slide it off the frame. The Mainframe II takes zip-on EMOD bags
  ([Atomic Defense Brute](https://www.atomicdefense.com/collections/eberlestock-brute-hunting-packs),
  [Buy American Mainframe II](https://buyamerican.com/products/eberlestock-mainframe-ii-the-most-comfortable-frame-for-heavy-loads-no-shoulder-pain) ***(reported, retailer copy)***).
- Eberlestock holds a **2025 US patent on a load sling** (US 12,495,885, §2).
- Complaints: "the strapping system isn't very good for multiple antlers"
  ([HuntTalk](https://www.hunttalk.com/threads/why-my-pack-sucks-kifaru-stone-glacier-exo-seek-mystery-ranch-kuiu-etc.290968/post-2864510)).
  A broken buckle was replaced under warranty, but too late for the hunt
  ([Rokslide](https://rokslide.com/forums/threads/stone-glacier-kiowa-3200-failure-on-hunt.254197/page-2) ***(reported)***).
- Bag-attach mechanics, weight and time are **unverified**.

### 1.10 Hill People Gear (relevant only as a contrast)

The Decker Frame "shares the same frame and suspension elements as the Ute" and holds any bag "which will fit within its
compression straps" ([Soldier Systems](https://soldiersystems.net/2021/02/26/hill-people-gear-decker-pack-system-expanded/)).

### 1.11 Historical prior art: external-frame clevis pins

Kelty external frames attached the bag (and the shoulder straps) to the frame with **clevis pins and split rings**
([Rust Mag](https://www.rustmag.com/gear/2018/4/26/keltybackpacks)). Kelty's 1952 frames list "clevis-pins"
([Popup Backpacker](https://popupbackpacker.com/update-your-old-kelty-external-frame-backpack/)). Dick Kelty did not
patent the original design ([Carryology](https://www.carryology.com/?p=21051) ***(reported)***). Before Kelty,
canvas bags fastened to frames with steel pins ([Wild Bounds](https://wildbounds.com/blogs/brands/kelty-the-story) ***(reported)***).
**A pin through a frame lug holding a bag eye is about 70 years old and public domain.**

### 3.2 Design patterns that work

1. **Positive top capture.** The best top anchoring puts the **frame top inside a pocket or sleeve on the bag**, so
   the crown, not a strap, resists peel. Examples: KUIU ICON 6 Hypalon stay pockets, Initial Ascent's slots and lid
   holes, the Mystery Ranch collar or frame cap, and the old Kifaru Hypalon sleeve. Initial Ascent's reviewer says it
   gives "a much tighter fit with less movement".
2. **Lifters that pull forward against the bag's aft pull.** KUIU says outright that the top stay is balanced by the
   load lifter. The lifter is a structural member of the top attachment, not just a fit tweak.
3. **Close bottom first.** SG says to reattach "starting with the bottom straps" and work up. Owners say a shelf gets
   meat "higher and tighter to the frame"
   ([Rokslide meat shelves](https://www.rokslide.com/forums/threads/meat-shelves-like-or-dislike.153705/page-3) ***(reported)***).
4. **Separate frame-load straps from bag straps.** Exo's colour-coded alternating pairs let meat be cinched to the
   frame on its own. KUIU owners added frame straps for exactly this. Riders want the meat anchored to the frame, not
   only through the bag.
5. **Ladder locks over side-release buckles where play matters.** Exo found that buckles "have more play" at the lid.
6. **Simple, few, gloved-hand closures.** Times of 15 to 30 s come from systems with 4 to 8 closures.

### 3.3 Failure modes to avoid

- **Re-attaching the top under meat.** The K4 dealer's ~18 cases came from this. Any top anchor that has to be
  stretched over a full wedge will be forced, and something will break.
- **Side-release buckles cranked under load.** KUIU, Eberlestock, Kifaru and Seek Outside owners report breaks. A
  hunter tied off a broken buckle with paracord mid-packout
  ([HuntTalk](https://www.hunttalk.com/threads/props.302850/) ***(reported)***).
- **Webbing walking out of low-grip hardware.** Gatekeepers, and SG's horizontal top strap. Back it up with a
  tri-glide or a second pass.
- **Velcro.** It wears, loses grip under 80 lb, gets lost, and is loud when opened.
- **Sag and low ride.** The KUIU sling with quarters, and the Paradox Evo depending on rigging.
- **Stays dislodging when the pack is bounced to settle meat** (SG Terminus).
- **Bag cannot come off**, or is hard to clean (SG Terminus, the older Metcalf).

### 3.4 What "quiet" requires

The noise sources owners name are: dry webbing and buckles; webbing loops at the frame; frame rails rubbing inside
the bag; dirt in frame joints; and cold fabric
([Rokslide pack squeak cure](https://rokslide.com/forums/threads/pack-squeak-cure.179512/page-3),
[Rokslide Kifaru noise](https://rokslide.com/forums/threads/kifaru-noise.178659/),
[HuntTalk](https://www.hunttalk.com/threads/why-my-pack-sucks-kifaru-stone-glacier-exo-seek-mystery-ranch-kuiu-etc.290968/post-2864510) ***(reported)***).
Owner fixes are wax, hockey tape over buckles and cams, taping tubes, and tighter lifters.

What follows is engineering inference from that list. It is not a sourced finding.
- **No hard part can tap a hard part when the pack is empty.** Every hook or buckle needs a keeper or a sleeve.
- **No sliding cloth-on-tube contact at the stays.** Rubbing, not impact, causes squeaks.
- **No relative motion at the pins.** A pin that rattles in an oval hole will click every step.
- **No Velcro in the field-opening path.**
- Joints should be sealed, or open enough to drain grit.

Our concept already has TPU hook sleeves, elastic keepers for slack hooks, UHMWPE sleeves on the pins and a 2 mm air
swell over each cleat. Two items are **open**: oval wear in the UHMWPE sleeve (it will rattle once oval), and the W3
wrap rubbing on the crown.

### 3.5 What best-in-class does that others don't

The leaders **make the frame top part of the bag's structure**. In KUIU ICON 6 the upper stays sit inside bag
pockets. In KUIU PRO the lifter counters the top stay. Initial Ascent's bag slips over the frame. In each case the
crown, not friction or a single wrap, resists the bag peeling aft. The others (SG, Kifaru without a sleeve, Exo K4
with the lid as the only bridge) rely on straps and lids at the top. Owners report that slip or overload at that
point, and the K4 dealer's failure count is concentrated there.

### 3.6 Open gaps an original design could fill

1. **A top anchor that does not need stretching over a full wedge.** One example: a crown pocket that stays engaged
   while the bag hinges at the bottom, plus a length-adjustable link. The K4 failure mode says this is unsolved.
2. **No-thread closures with no side-release buckle in the load path.** Hooks on eyes already do this.
3. **Meat anchored to the frame independently of the bag**, through the same hard points. Ours already does this
   (IM straps eye-to-eye).
4. **Published attachment mass and timed detach.** Nobody publishes either. A timed, gloved-hand trial would be a
   differentiator, and it is an honest benchmark.
5. **Field-replaceable wear parts** (pins, sleeves, hooks) instead of sewn or Velcro parts that wear out.

## 4. Our concept against the field (honest read)

**Where we are ahead or level**
- **No side-release buckle in the structural path.** The field's most-reported hardware break is avoided. The hook
  rating is still an assumption (1500 N; proof test five hooks to 830 N).
- **Pins on cleats are field-serviceable.** Pin, sleeve and hook are spares, and a cracked cleat has a webbing
  girth-hitch bypass. No competitor publishes anything like this.
- **Meat anchors on the frame's own hard points**, separate from the bag. This matches Exo's best practice and fixes
  the KUIU owners' complaint.
- **The hinged bag-bottom wedge** gives SG/MR-style "bag off the frame, load in between" without threading anything.
  The MR sling patents that described this are listed expired.
- **Detach time of 34 s** is in the field's band (15 s to 2 min). It is an **ASSUMPTION** until timed.

**Where we are behind: top-of-bag anchoring**
- The fairlead redesign **retired the bag-top LINK toggles** (`panels.yaml` webbing note, `geometry.yaml` crown note).
  The highest bag-to-frame hard point is now the IM3 hook at z 520. The crown top bar is at z ≈ 601 to 804
  (`geometry.yaml` crown_settings, ≈ z 703 at the 19 in torso), and the bag top is near z 1100. Above IM3, the only
  thing tying the bag to the frame is the **W3 crown wrap**, an exterior strap, plus the lifters. The cloth sim shows
  what this does. The bag peels aft above z 520, its aft-most point moves from 339 mm to **438 mm (meat)**, and W3
  "barely holds it", with tension falling to about 20 N as the top folds and shortens the strap path
  (`cloth_sim.md` §3.2). The contents CG lands at 206 mm aft against HA-LS-1's 150 mm.
- In field terms, we are at the **SG and Kifaru-without-sleeve level** (strap at the top), below the KUIU ICON 6 and
  Initial Ascent level (frame top captured in the bag). The peel is the predicted outcome of that choice, not a sim
  artifact.

**Options this research supports (for the pack-designer; not designed here)**
1. **A crown pocket or sleeve on B-01** that the crown top bar (or the crown legs above z 530) slides into. This is
   the KUIU ICON 6, Initial Ascent and MR collar pattern. The MR patents with the collar are listed expired, but
   confirm that. It must survive bay mode: with the bag hinged open 29°, the pocket either pulls off cleanly, or has
   to be a pocket open at the bottom that the bar drops into as the bag closes. **It must not need forcing over
   meat** (the K4 lesson).
2. **A fifth and sixth fairlead or hook at the crown legs**, around z 600 to 700. The same pin-and-eye hardware moves
   the top hard point up about 100 to 200 mm. Check the crown-leg telescoping: anything on a leg must move with the
   torso setting.
3. **Treat the lifters as a top anchor, not only a fit strap** (the KUIU principle). Our lifters go to the crown
   corner wraps, which is fine for US 10,952,523 if they never switch. Their bag end, though, is the harness/bag top
   path. Check in the cloth sim whether lifter tension at the factored 71 N per side closes the peel.
4. **Make W3 a ladder-lock with a backing keeper**, or route it through the crown so its path does not shorten as
   the top folds. This is Exo's ladder-lock lesson and SO's backing tri-glide lesson.

Any of these needs a re-run of the cloth sim (aft-most point and CG against HA-LS-1 150 mm) and a mass delta. The
5.0 lb target is already 711 g over.
