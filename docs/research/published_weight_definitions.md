# What the leaders count in their published pack weights (BL-008)

Owner: pack-designer. Date: 2026-11-10. Ticket: `docs/backlog/BL-008-weight-publishing-research.md`.
Companion proposal: `designs/pack_5200/analysis/weight_configs_proposal.md`.

**Method.** Manufacturer pages first, then retailers, then reviews. Every cell below has a URL or says
"not stated". Quotes are verbatim from the fetched page. Where a page would not render for the fetch tool
(KUIU PRO kit page, the Mystery Ranch Issuu workbook) or blocked it (Rokslide articles, HTTP 403), the figure
comes from the search engine's summary of that page and is marked **secondary**. Conversions:
1 lb = 453.592 g, 1 oz = 28.3495 g. "Inference" marks a conclusion I drew from the brand's own numbers (usually
arithmetic), not from a sentence on the page.

## 1. Answer in five lines

1. **Only Exo and Initial Ascent state what their system weight includes**, and both include the lid. Stone Glacier,
   Kifaru and KUIU publish separate frame, bag and lid numbers that show by arithmetic what the system figure holds.
   Mystery Ranch publishes one number and doesn't say what it covers.
2. **No leader includes a rain cover, a bottle holder (except Kifaru's sewn-in Nalgene pockets) or a hydration sleeve
   (SG sells it separately) in the published weight. Hipbelt pockets are sold separately by SG, Exo, Seek and Kifaru.**
   KUIU is the exception: its kits ship with hip-belt pockets.
3. **The meat-carry parts sit on the frame, not in the bag.** SG's load shelf is "integrated" in every frame, and its
   frame weight is quoted "w/ medium belt". Exo's "compression straps built into the frame" carry the meat. The
   review's ASSUMPTION (`bag_weight_review_20261110.md` §2) that the leaders' *bag* weights include their meat-carry
   straps is therefore **not supported** for SG and Exo. Their meat straps are counted in the *frame* weight.
4. Bag compression straps: Seek states they're included (8 + 1 over-the-top). KUIU's 8 are removed when the bag is
   swapped, so they belong to the bag. SG and Exo don't say.
5. **Independent weighings are rare.** Outdoor Life weighed SG, MR, Kifaru, Sitka, Eberlestock and KUIU packs. Most
   came within ±0.1 lb (45 g) of the claim. Sitka was the outlier at +0.85 lb (+15 %). I found no independent
   weighing for Exo, Seek Outside or Initial Ascent.

**Reading.** The claims of the four brands that matter most to us hold to about ±50 g when an outlet weighs the same
configuration. The spread *between* brands comes from configuration (lid in or out, belt size, pockets), not from
inaccurate scales. Sitka shows that a measured figure can sit 15 % above the claim.

## 4. What this means for us (detail in the proposal)

- Compare **system to system** only. Bag-only and frame-only figures aren't comparable across brands, because SG and
  Exo book the meat straps or shelf in the *frame*, KUIU books the compression straps on the *bag*, and nobody says
  where the attachment webbing goes.
- The market's "headline" is bag + frame + belt (M) + harness + built-in meat carry + built-in compression. The lid is
  in it for Exo, Seek and IA, and out of it for SG and Kifaru. Hipbelt pockets, bottle holders, rain covers and
  hydration sleeves are out of it, except KUIU's hip pockets and Kifaru's and Seek's sewn bottle pockets.
- Our current booking (CORRIE B.2 on H1, belt M, 3926.9 g) carries a top pouch, a removable bottle holster, a rain fly
  and float links that no leader counts.
